## What the documents require

### 31 23 19 — Dewatering
- Dewatering plan before construction (methods, equipment, wells and sumps, discharge points, settlement contingency), kept current like the TESC plan. (31 23 19 ¶1.2; main spec p.389 — Verified)
- Backup pumps and a standby generator on site; groundwater held at least 2 ft below any structure base; wells to WAC 173-160. (31 23 19 Part 2, ¶3.1–3.2; main spec pp.390–391 — Verified)
- No discharge to the sanitary sewer, outfall or WWTP; discharge checked with a Rossum sand tester (10 ppm silica maximum) on 24-hour notice; settlement points near critical structures. (31 23 19 ¶3.2–3.3; main spec pp.391–392 — Verified)

### 31 20 00 — Earthwork
- Dewatering is incidental to the earthwork (pay basis conflict: Issues CS-25). (31 20 00 ¶1.04 B; main spec p.384 — Verified)

### Appendix B — applies by its scope; not on the Ledger row (Inferred)
- Shallowest logged groundwater was 2.50 ft below the measuring point on Feb 19, 2021; 13.75 ft on Aug 24, 2020. (Appendix B main spec p.535 — Verified)

### Appendix C — applies by its scope; not on the Ledger row (Inferred)
- Groundwater in boring B-3 was 13.75 ft below grade, about El. 9.45 ft NAVD88, on Aug 24, 2020; the drawings use NGVD 1929 (Issues CS-33). (Appendix C §2.4.2; main spec p.543 — Verified)
