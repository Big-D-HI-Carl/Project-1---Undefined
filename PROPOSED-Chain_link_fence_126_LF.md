## What the documents require

### 02 83 00 — Chain Link Fences and Gates
- All chain-link fence is 6-ft galvanized fabric with 3-strand barbed wire on top (7 ft total), with bottom rails. (02 83 00 ¶1.01 A; main spec p.163 — Verified)
- Submittals: three samples (fabric, post section, accessories) and shop drawings with heights, post and gate locations, footings and hardware. (02 83 00 ¶1.04; main spec p.163 — Verified)
- Fabric ASTM A392 No. 9 gage, 2-in mesh, Class II galvanized; Sch. 40 posts (2.375-in line; 2.875-in end, corner and pull); 1.66-in rails and braces. (02 83 00 ¶2.01–2.09; main spec pp.164–166 — Verified)
- Line posts at 10 ft maximum set in concrete; fabric hung no sooner than 5 days after posts are set; fence under or within 50 ft of a primary power line is grounded. (02 83 00 ¶3.04, ¶3.06, ¶3.08; main spec pp.167–168 — Verified)
