### 33 32 00 — Water Distribution Piping
- Submittals: field-fabrication data, material certifications, all pipe and appurtenances, and a water main shut-down / temporary bypass plan with customer notices (one week for construction, 48 hours for shutdowns). (33 32 00 ¶1.03; main spec pp.436–437 — Verified)
- Buried potable pipe AWWA C900 Class 235 DR 18 PVC or Class 50 cement-lined DI; potable service pipe PE4710 HDPE from continuous coil with fusion joints; 3 ft minimum cover. (33 32 00 ¶2.02–2.03, ¶3.04 C.1; main spec pp.438, 441 — Verified)
- Connections to the existing system at a restrained joint or fitting; only Association staff operate existing isolation valves; 48-hour door-hanger notice for shut-offs. (33 32 00 ¶1.05 A.1, ¶3.06; main spec pp.437, 443 — Verified)

### 33 30 00 — Piping Systems
- Submittals: field-fabrication data, shop laying drawings, pipe support drawings, material certifications and field welding procedures. (33 30 00 ¶1.02; main spec pp.420–421 — Verified)
- Tracer wire on all new piping: #12 AWG steel-core, tied at 10-ft intervals, color-coded (green sewer, purple non-potable, blue potable); box locations submitted before construction (conflicts with C7.1/C7.4: Issues CS-28). (33 30 00 ¶3.03; main spec pp.426–427 — Verified)
- Tests witnessed by the Owner on 48-hour notice; test sections up to 1,500 ft; Contractor supplies test water; lines flushed before connecting to facilities. (33 30 00 ¶3.04.1–5; main spec p.427 — Verified)
- Water lines hydrostatic-tested per WSDOT 7-09.3(23) and disinfected, with DOH-lab chlorine reports (50 mg/L initial, 25 mg/L at 24 hours) and a bacteriological report. (33 30 00 ¶3.04.9; main spec p.428 — Verified; WSDOT test method Unresolved: external reference, not staged)
