## Summary

The new third treatment train, configured to match the dimensions of the two existing bullseye treatment cells: construction starts Aug 2023, it is complete July 8, 2024, and it is seeded with 10,000 gallons of activated sludge from Train #1 or #2, July 8–12, 2024 (00 31 13 ¶A.10 p.21, ¶A.22–23 p.22; QA plan §2.0 p.6; Verified). It includes the aeration basins, anoxic basins and clarifier, which get the 2-hour clean water leakage test (01 50 00 ¶1.04.C p.143; Verified). It is part of Substantial Completion (00 73 00 SC 19 p.92; Verified).
