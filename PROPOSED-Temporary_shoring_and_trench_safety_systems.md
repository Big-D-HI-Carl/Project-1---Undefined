## What the documents require

### 31 40 00 — Temporary Shoring and Bracing
- Washington PE-designed shoring where a trench box isn't used or is inadequate, trench depth exceeds 4 ft and back-sloping isn't used; embedded elements drilled, not driven. (31 40 00 ¶1.03 B, ¶2.01; main spec pp.401–402 — Verified)
- Submittals: shoring layout, design drawings and calculations; settlement monitoring and contingency plan for excavations over 10 ft. (31 40 00 ¶1.04; main spec p.401 — Verified)
- Temporary benchmarks on adjacent structures, resurveyed weekly during excavation; stop work and run the contingency plan if settlement shows; remove shoring in stages. (31 40 00 ¶1.05 A, ¶3.03–3.04; main spec pp.402–403 — Verified)

### 31 23 33 — Trenching and Backfill
- Trench safety is solely the Contractor's responsibility (29 CFR 1926). (31 23 33 ¶1.04, ¶3.02; main spec pp.393–395 — Verified)
