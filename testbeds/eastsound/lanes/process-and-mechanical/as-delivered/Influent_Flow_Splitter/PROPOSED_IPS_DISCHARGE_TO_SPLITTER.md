**Installation and construction**
- Fittings: 12-inch DI 90-degree bends and flange spools, 12x6-inch DI eccentric reducer, 6-inch DI restrained bend and pipe; 12-inch supports per 5/C7.2.

**Description and design data**
- Status Inferred (joins the proposed pump station to the proposed splitter, G0.6).
- In-station: small pumps 4x3 reducer, 4-inch check and gate valves, 4-inch DI force main restrained; large pumps 6x4 reducer, 6-inch check and gate valves, 6-inch DI restrained; 6x4 DI tee restrained; 4-inch and 6-inch buried gate valves; flanged coupling adaptors; pressure gage, isolator and shutoff valve (C4.1).
