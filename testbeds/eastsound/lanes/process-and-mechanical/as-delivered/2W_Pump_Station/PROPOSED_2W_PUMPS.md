## Requirements and notes (sentences from the Ledger Notes, grouped by keyword)

**Testing, start-up and training**
- 43 22 10: ITT Goulds 55GS50 (4-inch well) with Centripro M50434 5 HP 460 V 3-phase or equal; constant pressure control, set point about 50-75 psi; alternate pumps each cycle; Orenco HV discharge assemblies; verify rotation (¶3.01 D); supplier training.

**Controls and electrical interface**
- Add. 4 replaces the base text quoted in Add. 4 ("Pumps are to include variable Frequency Drives (VFDs)").
- Bid Item 1 pumps and 6 VFDs in the MCC per 04 Add. 4 table.
- Base ¶2.01 A now cited; Add. 4 governs the VFD text.

**Procurement and basis of design**
- Each pump: 2-inch high-pressure discharge assembly (ball valve, true union ball check valve, flex hose, quick disconnect; Orenco or equal) within 12 in. of the lid; pressure relief valve rated 110 psi or more within 24 in. of the wet well top.
