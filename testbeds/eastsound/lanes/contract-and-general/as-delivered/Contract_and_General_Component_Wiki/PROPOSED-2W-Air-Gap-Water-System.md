## Summary

A new 2W air gap water system: construction starts May 2023 and it is complete Oct 18, 2023 (00 31 13 ¶A.7 p.21, ¶A.15 p.22; Verified). Start-up takes at least 1 supplier day and must show 60–90 psi constant pressure from 0 to 50 gpm, a working automatic valve at the air gap, and working floats (01 91 00 ¶1.10 p.159; Verified). The pump station wet well is a separate component.
