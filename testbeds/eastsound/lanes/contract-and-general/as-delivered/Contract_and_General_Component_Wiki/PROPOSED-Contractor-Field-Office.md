## Summary

A field office is encouraged but not required (01 50 00 ¶1.01.B p.142; Verified), yet the schedule must be kept there and restrooms may go there (01 31 00 ¶1.05.D p.128; 01 50 00 ¶1.04.D.1 p.143; Verified).
