## Summary

Bypass pumping and other temporary process equipment are watched by on-site staff or an alarm system with automatic cellular notification (01 50 00 ¶1.04.H p.144; Verified). In every case, backup alarms and Normal/Running status are wired to the District's existing WWTP control system, and the PLC/SCADA programming is incidental (Verified). Whether that programming draws on Item 17 is not stated.
