## Summary

Removing temporary pump and process equipment is part of Final Completion (00 73 00 SC 19 p.92; Verified), and while in use it needs the same continuous oversight as bypass pumping (01 50 00 ¶1.04.H p.144; Verified). Which equipment this is, and whether it is the bypass pumping system or the temporary bypass pipe set-up, is not stated. 01 70 00 wants temporary facilities out before the Substantial Completion request; SC 19 governs for this equipment (Issues 1.19, Inferred).
