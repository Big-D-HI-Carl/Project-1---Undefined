## What the documents require

### 32 32 23 — Gravity Block Retaining Wall
- Foundation soils inspected and tested before the leveling pad; no more than two courses ahead of fill; footing drain and backfill placed; caps glued. (32 32 23 ¶3.01, ¶3.03–3.04; main spec pp.411–412 — Verified)

### 33 31 00 — Wastewater Piping
- Footing drain: 4-in perforated corrugated polyethylene tubing (WSDOT 9-05.2(7)); conflicts with Appendix C §3.7 (Issues CS-31). (33 31 00 ¶2.01 A; main spec p.430 — Verified; WSDOT 9-05.2(7) Unresolved: external reference, not staged)

### 31 23 33 — Trenching and Backfill
- Drain rock and drainage geotextile for retaining wall drains (WSDOT 9-03.12(4)). (31 23 33 ¶2.04–2.06; main spec p.395 — Unresolved: external reference, not staged)

### 33 41 00 — Storm Utility Drainage Piping
- Footing drain: perforated corrugated PE with PVC tight line to a drainage structure; bedding, backfill and construction per Sections 3.5 and 3.7 of the geotechnical report (cited as Sept 7, 2021). (33 41 00 ¶2.02 A.5, E, ¶3.02 F; main spec pp.448–449 — Verified)

### Appendix C — Geotechnical Report
- Geotechnical §3.7 recommends rigid perforated pipe, 4 in minimum, in 6 in of 1-in washed gravel with non-woven geotextile (Mirafi 140N or similar), tight-lined to storm, with cleanouts (conflicts with 33 41 00: Issues CS-31). (Appendix C §3.7; main spec p.550 — Verified (reference document))
