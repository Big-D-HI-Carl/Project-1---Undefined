## What the documents require

### 32 12 16 — Hot Mix Asphalt Paving
- HMA Class 1/2-in PG 58H-22 per WSDOT 5-04.2, 91% minimum density, commercial classification; mix design on WSDOT form 350-042. (32 12 16 ¶2.01; main spec p.404 — Unresolved: external reference, not staged)
- Lab certificates (sieve analysis, mix design) at least 5 days before placement. (32 12 16 ¶1.02; main spec p.404 — Verified)
- 2-in lifts matching existing thickness or the plan repair section, whichever is thicker; no material transfer devices; joints sealed with hot-poured sealant. (32 12 16 ¶3.02, ¶3.04; main spec p.405 — Verified)
- Contractor QC testing: asphalt content, gradation, voids, fracture, equivalence and in-place density. (32 12 16 ¶3.05; main spec p.405 — Verified)
- Sawcuts continuous and full depth at Owner-approved locations; slurry kept out of storm and sanitary systems; edges recut before paving. (32 12 16 ¶3.06; main spec pp.405–406 — Verified)
