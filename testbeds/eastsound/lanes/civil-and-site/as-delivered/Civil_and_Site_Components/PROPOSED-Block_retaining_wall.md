## What the documents require

### 32 32 23 — Gravity Block Retaining Wall
- Contractor-designed wall: construction drawings and calculations stamped by a Washington PE, plus the manufacturer's certification, before work starts. (32 32 23 ¶1.01, ¶1.04; main spec pp.408–409 — Verified)
- Keystone Compaq II or approved equal (ASTM C1372), gray straight split face, running bond, 3,000 psi minimum, 8% maximum absorption, 8 x 18 x 12-in minimum units, two fiberglass pins per unit. (32 32 23 ¶2.02–2.03; main spec pp.410–411 — Verified)
- Leveling pad 6 in minimum of crushed surfacing top course (WSDOT 9-03.9(3)), compacted to 95% standard or 92% modified Proctor. (32 32 23 ¶2.04, ¶3.02; main spec p.411 — Unresolved: external reference, not staged)
- Foundation soils inspected and tested before the leveling pad; no more than two courses ahead of fill; footing drain and backfill placed; caps glued. (32 32 23 ¶3.01, ¶3.03–3.04; main spec pp.411–412 — Verified)
- Tolerances: ±1.5 in per 10 ft vertical and horizontal, batter within 2°, corners ±1 ft, gaps 1/2 in maximum. (32 32 23 ¶3.05; main spec p.412 — Verified)

### Appendix C — applies by its scope; not on the Ledger row (Inferred)
- Design basis for walls: active 35 pcf and at-rest 55 pcf (drained), passive 300 pcf, traffic surcharge about 250 psf or 70 psf uniform, seismic 8H psf; backfill compacted to 90–92% with light equipment; 18-in free-draining gravel zone. (Appendix C §3.5; main spec p.548 — Verified (reference document))
