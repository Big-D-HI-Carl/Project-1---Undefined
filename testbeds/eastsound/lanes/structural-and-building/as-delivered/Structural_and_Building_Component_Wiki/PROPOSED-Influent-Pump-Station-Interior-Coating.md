# Influent Pump Station Interior Epoxy Lining

- **Component tag:** PROPOSED-Influent-Pump-Station-Interior-Coating (proposed — no tag shown in the documents)
- **Type:** Coating — interior epoxy lining
- **Discipline:** Architectural
- **Revision/Date:** Base documents govern; no Add. 4 item
- **Summary:** 09 97 23 lines all interior concrete surfaces of the Influent Pump Station with a spray-applied, 100%-solids epoxy (Raven 405 or Tnemec G436 PermaShield), locations per the drawings [main spec pp.236–237 — Verified]. It goes on concrete cured at least 28 days and blasted to CSP 3–5, by a manufacturer-certified applicator, at 80 mils min. and 100 mils average wet film [main spec pp.237–239 — Verified]. Testing covers wet film, holiday testing at 100 V/mil and pull-off adhesion (2 of 3 pulls over 200 psi), and submittals cover product data and applicator qualifications [main spec pp.236, 239–241 — Verified]. The station drawing, C4.1, sits in the Process & Mechanical lane [01 — Verified].
