- **Summary:** Base A1.1 shows the Blower Building motor control center as a panel bank labeled "Electrical Panels" with a 3.5-ft clearance note [plans_7 p.5 — Verified-Visual]. It is Electrical & Controls scope (E9.1), carried as Bid Item 6 per 04's inferred mapping, with requirements outside this stack [03 Add. 4 map; 04 — Verified].
