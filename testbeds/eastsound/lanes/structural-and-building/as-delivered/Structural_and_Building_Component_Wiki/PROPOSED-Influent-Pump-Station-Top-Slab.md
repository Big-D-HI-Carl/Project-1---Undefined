# Influent Pump Station Top Slab

- **Component tag:** PROPOSED-Influent-Pump-Station-Top-Slab (proposed — no tag shown in the documents)

- **Discipline:** Structural
- **Revision/Date:** Add. 4 (issued Feb 9, 2023) governs — Add. 4 p.1, Clarification 2 (exterior of all precast joints sealed with CANUSA-CPS WrapidSeal or approved equal)
- **Summary:** The Influent Pump Station top slab is built per the Contract Plan details and fitted with a new aluminum access hatch with safety grates [main spec p.183, ¶2.02 D.1 — Verified]. ¶2.02 D.1 calls that hatch 3'-0"×5'-0" light-duty (Flygt FLED-13 or equal), while the ¶2.02 E.2 table gives the station a 48"×30" H-20 hatch (LW Products HD-3C) — an open conflict [main spec p.183 — Verified; Unresolved]. The "Contract Plan details" are not identified in this stack, and no document says whether S2.4's drain pump station lid is that detail [plans_8 p.4 — Verified-Visual; Unresolved]. Precast requirements and the Add. 4 exterior joint seal apply as for the wet wells [main spec pp.182–184; Add. 4 p.1 — Verified].
