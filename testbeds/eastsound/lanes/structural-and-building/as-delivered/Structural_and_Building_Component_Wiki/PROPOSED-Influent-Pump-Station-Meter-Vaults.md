# Influent Pump Station Meter Vaults

- **Component tag:** PROPOSED-Influent-Pump-Station-Meter-Vaults (proposed — no tag shown in the documents)

- **Discipline:** Structural
- **Revision/Date:** Add. 4 (issued Feb 9, 2023) governs — Add. 4 p.1, Clarification 2 (exterior of all precast joints sealed with CANUSA-CPS WrapidSeal or approved equal)
- **Summary:** The Influent Pump Station Meter Vaults are 4'×4' Oldcastle 4686-LA precast vaults with 36"×36" H-20 single-leaf hatches (Halliday H1R3636), per 03 40 00 and drawing C4.1; the quantity is not stated [main spec p.183 — Verified]. Precast wet wells and vaults are 5,000 psi with A615 Gr 60 rebar to ACI 318-14 and ASTM C857, with WA-engineer-sealed shop drawings and calculations and no testing stated [main spec pp.182–183 — Verified]. Add. 4 seals the exterior of all precast wet well and vault joints with CANUSA-CPS WrapidSeal or approved equal [Add. 4 p.1, Clarification 2 — Verified; Add. 4 governs]. C4.1 sits in the Process & Mechanical lane, which locates and counts the vaults [01 — Verified].
