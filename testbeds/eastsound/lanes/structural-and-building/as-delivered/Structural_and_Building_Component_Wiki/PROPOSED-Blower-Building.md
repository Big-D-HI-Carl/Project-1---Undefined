# Blower Building

- **Component tag:** PROPOSED-Blower-Building (proposed — no tag shown in the documents)
- **Type:** Building — pre-engineered metal building
- **Discipline:** Architectural
- **Revision/Date:** Add. 4 (issued Feb 9, 2023) governs — Add. 4 p.1, Clarifications 3 and 4; p.2, 09 90 00 ¶2.01 I (Service Condition J); p.3, Drawings A1.1/A1.2 and S2.3; p.4 (A1.1); p.5 (A1.2); p.9 (S2.3)
- **Summary:** The Blower Building is a single-room pre-engineered metal building (Room 101, Blower Room, FF 23.30) with a 1:12 single-slope roof and X-braced walls, 44.00' × 9.25' with a 10.25' minimum haunch clearance under Add. 4; the base drawings show 42.00' × 9.50' and 9.00', and 13 12 20 ¶2.05 A.1 still reads 42' × 9.75' with 9'-0" [plans_7 pp.5–6 — Verified-Visual; Add. 4 pp.1, 4, 5 — Verified; main spec p.249 — Verified; Add. 4 governs]. It sits about 9" from the existing Train 2 building with the 10-ft setback kept from the south and east property lines, uses a 5" semi-flush girt at the north (high) sidewall with bypass Zee girts elsewhere, and resists lateral load with ordinary concentric braced frames and ordinary moment frames on 6 manufacturer-designed columns [Add. 4 p.3 — Verified; Add. 4 p.9, plans_8 p.3 — Verified-Visual]. 13 12 20 specifies Garco or equal with 24-ga Galvalume standing-seam roof panels and 26-ga R- or A-type wall panels, factory-finished inside and out (confirmed by Add. 4 Clarification 3), and requires WA SE-sealed design calculations, an anchor bolt embedment design and erection drawings — matching the S1.1 deferred submittal — with no testing article [main spec pp.246–251 — Verified; Add. 4 p.1 — Verified; plans_7 p.8 — Verified-Visual]. Inside, A1.1 calls for R-25 walls and R-30 roof, GWB walls painted per Add. 4 Service Condition J, and a sealed concrete floor [plans_7 p.5 — Verified-Visual; Add. 4 p.2 — Verified]; the insulation assembly conflicts with the 07 20 00 liner system, and the paint system for other surfaces points to a section that does not exist [Unresolved].
