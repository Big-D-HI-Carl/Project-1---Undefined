# Drain Pump Station Lid

- **Component tag:** PROPOSED-Drain-Pump-Station-Lid (proposed — no tag shown in the documents)
- **Type:** Concrete — pump station lid
- **Discipline:** Structural
- **Revision/Date:** Base documents govern; no Add. 4 item
- **Summary:** The drain pump station lid is a 16"-thick cast-in-place concrete lid over an 8' min., 96" I.D. precast wet well, designed for H-20 truck loading, with two hatch openings and a center beam of (2) #8 top and bottom with #4 closed ties @ 3" max. [plans_8 p.4, Details 1–3, Notes 1–6 — Verified-Visual]. Bars go in a set order with 1-1/2" clear, the lid keys into the precast wall, and its diameter matches the wet well O.D. [plans_8 p.4 — Verified-Visual]. S1.1 gives it its own mix (4,500 psi, 0.45 w/cm, 3/4" aggregate, 5% air, 20% fly ash or slag), and 03 30 00 governs submittals, Owner testing and special inspection [plans_7 p.8 — Verified-Visual; main spec pp.173–180 — Verified]. Which pump station this is remains open: no sheet in 01 is titled "drain pump station," and 03 40 00 schedules 96" wet wells only at the 2W and Influent pump stations [01; main spec p.183 — Verified; Unresolved].
