## Summary

Site piping plan with a pipe identification legend giving size, material (DI, Sch. 80 PVC, SS, HDPE) and minimum slope for 21 base pipe IDs: influent, Train 1–3 influent and effluent, UV disinfection effluent and drains, digested sludge, WAS, 2W and potable water, building septic, vault drain, air vents and process air (base plans_3Part-1 p.1 and Add. 4 p.7, C1.3 — Verified). Add. 4 changes pipe 10 from 2-in Sch. 80 PVC to a 3-in HDPE 2W water line and adds IDs 22 (1-in yard hydrants, 3 locations), 23 (1-in 2W water line, HDPE), 24 (8-in Train 1 effluent, DI), 25 (4-in pressate drain, DI) and 26 (4-in building floor drain, DI) — Add. 4 governs (Add. 4 p.7, C1.3 — Verified-Visual; Add. 4 p.3 — Verified). Add. 4 red markups extend the Blower Building 2 ft east to 44 ft (not 42 ft) while keeping the 10-ft east and south setbacks, move the bladder tanks from the Blower Building to the existing building, and move Hot Boxes #1 and #2 with conduit, wiring and plumbing adjusted; Add. 4 p.3 adds that the 2W (air gap) water hot box orientation changed to allow vehicle traffic (Add. 4 p.7, C1.3 — Verified; Add. 4 p.3 — Verified). Keyed notes: cut the existing 8-in pipe and connect the new 8-in influent to Train 2; extend new 8-in pipe to Train 1 and abandon and plug the existing Train 1 influent (Add. 4 p.7, C1.3 — Verified-Visual). Sheet notes point to C0.1 for existing conditions and C2.1–C2.4 for storm grading, drainage and piping (Add. 4 p.7, C1.3 — Verified-Visual).
