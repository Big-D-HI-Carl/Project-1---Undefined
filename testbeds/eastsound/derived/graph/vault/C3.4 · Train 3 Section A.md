## Summary

Section through Train 3: top of tank 26.80, freeboard ±1.50, static WL 25.30, ADF WL 25.34, PHF WL 25.38, finished grade 23.30 and floor elev 9.80, with a 17.00' wall and 15.50' static depth; diameters 18.00' (clarifier), 32.00' and 34.00'. The clarifier shows the scraper arm drive, feed well, level adjustment rod, sludge scraper arms, a 1'-8" O.D. sludge well, grout fill on the floor, scum baffle, skimming arm, V-notch weir and FRP launder cover, with the 10" clarifier inlet at IE 14.31. The annulus shows the air header, a submersible mixer, a plenum diffuser, a 6" nitrate recycle underflow (IE 10.57) and the 2" WAS/clarifier drain (pipe to WAS pump manifold, IE 10.3). Davit crane assemblies (typical, both locations) and a prefab aluminum ladder with a 4' × 4' × 6" thick concrete pad at FG 23.30 are shown. The 12" base under the slab is "gravel backfill for foundations, Class 'A' per WSDOT Standard Specification 9-03.12(1)A" — Unresolved: external reference, not staged.
