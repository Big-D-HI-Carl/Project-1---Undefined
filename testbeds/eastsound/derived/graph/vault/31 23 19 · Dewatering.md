## Summary

Covers dewatering of all excavations until backfill reaches finished grade (¶1.1; main spec p.389 — Verified). Submittal: a dewatering plan before construction, 'in accordance with Section 01 30 00', showing methods, equipment, well and sump details, stormwater diversions, discharge points and contingency plans for settlement; it is kept up to date like the TESC plan (¶1.2; p.389 — Verified; section number not in the manual, see Issues). Groundwater was monitored for ten months (Appendix B) and much higher levels are expected in winter; the section also cites the 'September 7, 2021' geotechnical report (¶1.3–1.4; p.390 — Verified; date already flagged, 02 Merge flag 2). The Contractor keeps backup pumps and a standby generator on site, dewaters at least 2 ft below any structure base, builds wells to WAC 173-160, and may not discharge to the sanitary sewer, outfall or WWTP (Part 2, ¶3.1–3.2; pp.390–391 — Verified). Testing: discharge is checked with a Rossum sand tester (10 ppm silica maximum) with 24-hour notice, and settlement reference points are observed near critical structures (¶3.2–3.3; pp.391–392 — Verified).
