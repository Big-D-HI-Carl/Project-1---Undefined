## Summary

Furnish and install two mixer assemblies, two masts and two davit cranes, with one mixer in each anoxic zone of new Train #3 (¶1.01 A p.486). Mixers are at most 5 HP nameplate, 460 V 3-phase, with 316 Ti stainless propeller (EverClean blades), shaft, housing and frame, a duplex stainless casing cover, silicon carbide double mechanical seals in an oil chamber, a plug-and-receptacle power cable with resin-sealed entry, three PTC thermal switches and a moisture detector, bearings over 100,000 hours B10, maintenance intervals of at least 16,000 hours or 2 years, and 10 starts per hour (¶2.01 A–B, F pp.486–489). Each mixer rides a permanent 4"-square stainless guide mast with sockets at 10 ft max, a 14-position rotary top plate and an adjustable stop bracket (or a floor mounting pedestal), and two portable stainless lifting davits with manual brake winches mount on the top mast bracket, with removable handrail or chain sections so mixers need not be lifted over the handrail (¶2.01 C–E p.488); the mixer supplier furnishes motor-sensor monitoring relays that others install in the motor control panel (¶2.01 B.4 p.487). Controls follow 26 80 00 ¶3.09 B (¶2.02 p.489); submittals per 01 33 00 are catalog data with wiring diagrams and O&M information (¶1.02 A p.486); the contractor gives a 1-year parts-and-labor extended warranty (¶1.03 A); three sets of wear parts are furnished (¶2.03); and testing covers factory checks (balance, leakage, electrical, insulation, no-load rotation), field testing after installation, 2 days of start-up and 1 day of training (¶2.04 p.489; ¶3.01 B–3.02 p.490). The same two mixers are also specified in 46 53 00, whose single-source and precedence clauses overlap this section (Issue 30).
