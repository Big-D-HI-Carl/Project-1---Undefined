## Summary

Before requesting Substantial Completion the Contractor delivers tools, spare parts and extra stock, changes over locks and hands over keys, completes start-up testing and operator instruction, removes temporary facilities, turns over record information, finishes cleanup, and provides O&M manuals for all equipment, controls, telemetry and SCADA plus 2015 WSEC compliance documentation per C103.6.3 (¶1.03.A, pp.152–153; Verified; temporary facility removal conflicts with 00 73 00 SC 19 — Issues 1.19; energy code edition — Issues 1.20). The request carries a completion notice, incomplete-items list, pay request, change accounting, warranties and bonds, releases and occupancy/operating certificates, and record documents; "substantially complete" is defined by the WSDOT General Specifications and "Sections 00 72 00 and 00 73 00a" (¶1.03.B, p.153; WSDOT part Unresolved: external reference, not staged; section numbers — Issues 2.5), and warranties start on the Date of Substantial Completion (¶1.03.D.1, p.153; Verified; bears on Issues 1.11). Retainage is released only against an affidavit of paid payrolls and bills, lien releases, the DOR tax form, the L&I affidavit of wages paid and the ESD release (¶1.05.B, p.154; Verified). All mechanical and electrical equipment is tested before any facility goes into operation: procedures due 14 days before start-up covering every control device, equipment item and control circuit, joint sign-off forms, testing witnessed by the Engineer, and manufacturers' representatives who train Owner staff and certify each installation by letter (¶1.06, p.155; Verified). A daily record print set and a marked-up project manual are kept on site, then final cleanup (¶1.07–1.10, pp.155–156; Verified).
