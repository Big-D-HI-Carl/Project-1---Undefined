## Summary

PWM VFDs for 480 V 3-phase, provided by the system integrator as a complete system integral to the MCC or other control panels; 1-year parts-and-labor guarantee from acceptance (¶1.01, ¶1.02 C, p.316) [Verified]. Submittals beyond 26 05 00: outline dimensions and elevations, wiring diagrams with all interface points and terminals, component and option data, and the operations and programming manual (¶1.02 A–B, p.316) [Verified]. Basic drive: pulse width modulated with a dual full-wave diode bridge converter (¶2.03, p.317) [Verified; section pp.317–318 scanned by key terms]. Quality assurance and start-up: each VFD functionally tested under motor load with simulated overload and 100% parameter verification; certified factory start-up for every drive with a start-up form, one week notice to the Engineer, and testing on both utility and standby power where a generator serves the load (¶3.02–3.03, p.319) [Verified]. Consistent with E9.2 (PowerFlex 753) and 26 24 19 ¶2.01 (Allen-Bradley VFDs, no exceptions); Add. 4 p.2 moves the sludge and 2W pump VFDs into the MCC, as the E sheets already show (pp.316, 306; plans_12 p.4; Add. 4 p.2) [Verified].
