## Summary

The Contractor supplies and erects a temporary construction sign to Department of Ecology and CDBG Department of Commerce requirements (Part 1 ¶A; main spec p.252 — Verified). The sign is 4 ft x 8 ft x 3/4-in APA A-B exterior plywood, black lettering on white, with Ecology, CDBG Commerce, Engineer and Contractor logos (¶1.02 A.1–4; p.252 — Verified). Text includes the project name with loan number WQC-2021-EaSoWd-00208, the Owner, San Juan County, Wilson Engineering and the Contractor (¶1.02 A.5; p.252 — Verified). Submittal: proof of graphics before printing (¶1.01; p.252 — Verified).
