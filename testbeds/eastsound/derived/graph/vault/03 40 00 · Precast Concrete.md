## Summary

Covers design, supply and installation of all precast concrete structures shown — vaults, top slabs and "the effluent wet well" — with WA-engineer-sealed shop drawings and calculations, mix designs 15 days ahead, material certificates, 5 years of NPCA certification, and Owner/Engineer plant inspection rights [¶1.01 A, p.181; ¶1.06 A–F, ¶1.07, p.182 — Verified; WSDOT M41-10 references ¶1.04 C and ¶1.05 B — Unresolved: external reference, not staged]. Wet wells and vaults are 5,000 psi with A615 Gr 60 rebar to ACI 318-14 and ASTM C857: 96" Oldcastle wet wells with 48"×30" H-20 aluminum hatches (LW Products HD-3C) at the 2W Plant Water Pump Station (C4.2) and Influent Pump Station (C4.1), and 4'×4' Oldcastle 4686-LA vaults with 36"×36" H-20 single-leaf hatches (Halliday H1R3636) for the Influent and Effluent Flow Meter Valve Vaults and Plants 2 & 3 Flow Meter Vault (C7.4) and the Influent Pump Station Meter Vaults (C4.1) [¶2.02 E, p.183 — Verified]. The top slab schedule separately calls for the Influent Pump Station top slab per the Contract Plan details with a new 3'-0"×5'-0" light-duty aluminum hatch with safety grates (Flygt FLED-13 or equal), which conflicts with the 48"×30" H-20 hatch in the vault table [¶2.02 D.1 and E.2, p.183 — Verified; Unresolved — Issues, Conflicts]. Accessories are non-shrink grout (6,000 psi at 7 days) for anchor bolt and handrail pockets and non-mechanical base plates, 1:2 Portland cement grout (4,000 psi) for wall-slab slopes, ASTM C881 Type IV epoxy grout for pump bases, RAM-NEK or Quikseal sealant gaskets, and Canusa WrapidSeal heat-shrink external joint sleeves [¶2.02 F–I, p.184 — Verified], which Add. 4 applies to the exterior of all precast wet well and vault joints [Add. 4 p.1, Clarification 2 — Verified; Add. 4 governs]. Installation requires watertight gasketed joints, primed heat-shrink sleeves with 3 days' notice by a manufacturer-trained installer, backfill compacted to 95% within 4 ft of vault walls, frames set within 1/4" vertical, and hatches machined for a solid fit [¶3.02 B–G, p.185 — Verified].
