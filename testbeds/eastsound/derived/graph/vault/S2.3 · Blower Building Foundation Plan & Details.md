## Summary

Add. 4 revises the foundation plan from 42'-0" × 9'-6" (both "verify w/ civil") to 44'-0" with a 9'-3" building on a 10'-0" slab, adds "and to verify dimensions" to plan note 1 (see civil), and changes the blower equipment pad from 4" min. to 3-1/2" (both "per civil") [plans_8 p.3 — Verified-Visual; Add. 4 p.9 — Verified-Visual; Add. 4 governs]. Both issues show a 6" slab-on-grade with #4 @ 18" o.c. each way at mid-depth, a 20"W × 18"D perimeter grade beam with (3) bars top and bottom (bar size not legible at stored resolution), 6 steel columns by the metal building manufacturer, and a note that the foundation design may change once the metal building is designed [plans_8 p.3; Add. 4 p.9 — Verified-Visual; bar size — Unresolved: need native sheet]. Add. 4 adds the north-side condition next to the existing building — grade beam widened 9" (verify with civil) to abut the existing foundation, with #4×30" dowels @ 36" o.c. drilled and epoxied 4" into the existing concrete (special inspection not required) — and a 5" semi-flush girt at the north wall with bypass girt framing elsewhere [Add. 4 p.9, Details 1, 3, 4 — Verified-Visual; Add. 4 p.3 — Verified; Add. 4 governs]. Detail 2 shows the metal building lateral system used as the foundation design basis — steel ordinary concentric braced frames and ordinary moment frames with pinned bases, all by the metal building manufacturer — and Detail 4 anchors columns with threaded-rod anchor bolts sized by the manufacturer [plans_8 p.3; Add. 4 p.9 — Verified-Visual]. Details 5–7 cover slab control and construction joints (no more than 12'-0" o.c. each way), sleeved grade beam penetrations, and perimeter foundation drains of 4" rigid ABS perforated pipe or drain tile in drain rock that discharge by gravity or mechanical means, with a foundation drainage plan submitted to civil for review [plans_8 p.3; Add. 4 p.9 — Verified-Visual].
