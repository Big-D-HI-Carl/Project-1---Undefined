## Summary

GeoEngineers' revised report for 143 Cessna Road describes the site as fill (2–4.5 ft) over medium stiff to very stiff undifferentiated glacial drift (clay) to 19–24.5 ft, over glacial till, from borings B-1 to B-3 drilled Aug 10, 2020 (§§1.0, 2.3–2.4.1; main spec pp.542–543 — Verified). Groundwater in the B-3 monitoring well was 13.75 ft below grade (about El. 9.45 ft NAVD88) on Aug 24, 2020, with perched water likely in wet seasons; the report uses NAVD88 while the drawings use NGVD 1929 (§2.4.2; p.543 — Verified; see Issues). Design basis: Site Class D, not liquefiable, 2,500 psf allowable bearing on native drift or structural fill, settlement under 3/4 in, 6-in capillary break under slabs, and moisture-sensitive soils best worked in dry months (§3.0 summary; p.544 — Verified). Walls use 35 pcf active, 55 pcf at-rest and 300 pcf passive (drained), with an 18-in free-draining gravel zone and backfill compacted to 90–92%; structures get a perimeter drain of rigid perforated pipe (4 in minimum) in 6 in of 1-in washed gravel wrapped in non-woven geotextile (Mirafi 140N or similar), tight-lined to storm, and trench backfill compaction is 85% in landscaping, 90% below 2 ft and 95% in the top 2 ft under pavement (§§3.5, 3.6.2, 3.7; pp.548, 550 — Verified; conflicts, see Issues). The Dec 16, 2022 addendum suggests parking-area pavement of 2.5–3 in HMA over 3–4 in CSBC over 8–12 in gravel base, but its continuation page (p.578) and pages 539 and 576 have no text or image (p.577 — Verified; pages already deferred, 02 decision G).
