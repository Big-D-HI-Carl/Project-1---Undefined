## Summary

Covers temporary shoring and bracing for excavations, trench excavation safety systems under RCW 49.17 and WAC 296-155-655, and their maintenance (¶1.01–1.02; main spec p.401 — Verified). Where a trench box is not used or is inadequate, trench depth exceeds 4 ft and back-sloping is not used, the Contractor provides shoring designed, stamped and signed by a Washington PE; embedded elements must be drilled, not driven (¶1.03 B, ¶2.01; pp.401–402 — Verified). Submittals: shoring layout and design drawings, calculations and backup data, plus a settlement monitoring and contingency plan for excavations deeper than 10 ft (¶1.04; p.401 — Verified). The Contractor sets temporary benchmarks on adjacent structures, resurveys them weekly during excavation and stops work to implement the contingency plan if settlement is detected (¶1.05 A, ¶3.03; pp.402–403 — Verified). Shoring comes out in stages, damage from installation or removal is repaired by the Contractor, and the Owner takes no responsibility for trench safety (¶3.04–3.05; p.403 — Verified).
