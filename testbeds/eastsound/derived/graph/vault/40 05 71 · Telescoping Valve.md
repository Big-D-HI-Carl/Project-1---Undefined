## Summary

One rising-stem 4" telescoping valve with at least 48" of travel on the aerated digester outlet, as indicated on the plans (¶1.01 A–B p.451). Construction: seamless stainless tube at least 1/8" thick on a stainless lifting bail; stainless companion flange with a neoprene slip-seal gasket at least 1/4" thick (ID 1/8" under the tube OD), 125# drilling to match the riser; handwheel lift at least 12" in diameter with UHMW thrust bearings, a self-locking 304 SS acme stem, a galvanized square-tube torque nut (or vee-keyed shaft) against rotation, and a clear butyrate stem cover with a position indicator in 1/4" increments; 316 SS pedestal on the concrete side wall with six 5/8"×5" stainless wedge anchors (¶2.01 pp.451–452). Basis of design is Waterman TS-2 or equal (¶2.02 p.452); submittals per 01 33 00 are catalog data, fabrication and installation drawings, and O&M information (¶1.02 A p.451); installation follows the manufacturer's instructions and no testing is specified (¶3.01 p.452). The 48" travel matches the 21.3–25.3 T-valve operating range on C5.3 (Inferred check).
