## Summary

Gives general submittal procedures and a non-exhaustive checklist; item-specific requirements sit in the individual sections (¶1.01, ¶1.03, p.129; Verified). The checklist's bid-phase entries cite sections and attachments that do not exist in the project manual — 00 43 36, 00 22 13, 00 45 14, and DBE forms as "Attachments 6 and 7" of 00 73 00 — and move Contractor Qualifications to 24 hours after opening (¶1.03.A–C, p.129; Issues 1.14, 2.2, 2.5). Before the Agreement: bonds, insurance certificates, prevailing wage filings; 10 days after execution: construction schedule, substitution requests, Schedule of Values; monthly: pay application and updated schedule; certified payroll; 14 days before any WWTP shutdown: a bypass pumping and emergency response plan; closeout: affidavits, record drawings (¶1.03.D–J, pp.129–130; Verified). Submittals are PDF with a transmittal, stamped by the Contractor, with an approved-submittal file kept on site; samples in pairs, product certificates in triplicate, test reports 3 copies (¶1.04–1.05, pp.130–131; Verified). O&M manuals (4 hard, 2 PDF sets) go in draft with shop drawings, final draft before the 75% pay application, and complete before payment above 90% (¶1.05.G, pp.131–132; Verified).
