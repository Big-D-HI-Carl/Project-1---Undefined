## Summary

Plan and profiles of the west and east trench drains, with rim labels for catch basins SDCB #2 (22.65), #3 (22.70), #4 (23.15), #6 (23.00), #7 (23.00), #8 (22.78), #9 (22.75) and #10 (23.10) (plans_3Part-7 p.1, C2.4 — Verified-Visual). The east trench drains connect on the north and south sides of SDCB #10 (Type 1, STA 0+77.60, 6-in west invert 20.88), with trench drain rims at 23.10 (plans_3Part-7 p.1, C2.4 — Verified-Visual). The west trench drain runs about 51 ft in plan and connects on the north side of SDCB #7 (Type 1, rim 23.00, 12-in west and 6-in east inverts 20.77) (plans_3Part-7 p.1, C2.4 — Verified-Visual). Add. 4 adds 33 41 00 ¶2.02 F for the trench drains: Zurn Z886-HD or approved equal, HDPE channels with 0.75% built-in slope and 4-in inside width, heavy-duty steel frames, slotted ductile iron grates bolted after final inspection, Load Class C — Add. 4 governs (Add. 4 p.2 — Verified). 01 lists C2.4 as Text, but its only real text is the view titles; the rest is UV equipment block labels (plans_3Part-7 p.1 — Verified; see Issues).
