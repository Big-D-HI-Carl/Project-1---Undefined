## Summary

The four elevations show a single-slope (1:12) metal building with X-braced walls on a concrete foundation; Add. 4 changes the length from 42.00' to 44.00' and the width from 9.50' to 9.25' [plans_7 p.6 — Verified-Visual; Add. 4 p.5 — Verified; Add. 4 governs]. Add. 4 raises the building so both through-wall exhaust fans clear, taking the east-elevation minimum haunch height clearance from 9.00' to 10.25' and the floor-to-vent dimension from 6.75' to 8.00' [plans_7 p.6 — Verified-Visual; Add. 4 p.5 — Verified; Add. 4 governs]. The conceptual framing view (not to scale) has the Contractor provide a pre-engineered metal building with a similar column and bracing layout; Add. 4 sets a semi-flush girt at the high sidewall (5" from outside of column flange to outside of Zee girt flange), bypass Zee girts on all other sides, and Contractor means and methods for a weather-tight north wall with exterior metal panels that may be built on the ground and lifted into place [plans_7 p.6 — Verified-Visual; Add. 4 p.3 — Verified; Add. 4 governs]. The south elevation shows door 101C at 5.00' × 8'-0", two door pairs of 5.00' leaves, and door 101B at 3.00' × 6'-8"; the west elevation shows the 7.00'-tall motorized louver and a 2.00' × 2.00' louver [plans_7 p.6 — Verified-Visual]. A footing-perimeter note calls for R-10 extruded polystyrene from top of concrete to bottom of footing, top chamfered 45°, exposed faces covered with grey Dryvit EIFS or equal, and metal-building-color flashing lapped at least 3" over the board [plans_7 p.6 — Verified-Visual].
