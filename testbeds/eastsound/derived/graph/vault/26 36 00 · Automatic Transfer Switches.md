## Summary

Basis of design Cummins OTECSE with NEMA 12 enclosure and PowerCommand 40-01 controller; other makes acceptable only if approved at least two weeks before bid with a line-by-line compliance statement (¶2.01, p.339) [Verified]. Switches designated 3-pole get a neutral bus rated 100% of the switch rating; enclosures third-party certified to NEMA ICS 6 (¶2.02, p.341) [Verified]. Controller functions include a programmed transition of 0–60 s (default 3 s), test and exercise modes, and two normally open auxiliary contacts per position rated 10 A at 240 VAC; service entrance transfer switch requirements follow in ¶2.04 (pp.342–344) [Verified; pages scanned by key terms]. Submittals: action, informational and closeout (¶1.03–1.05, p.338); testing: factory dielectric test per NEMA ICS 1 and field tests and inspections by the manufacturer representative with reports (¶2.05, ¶3.03–3.04, pp.345–347) [Verified]. Consistent with E6.1 (400 A service entrance ATS, 4-wire 3-pole, NEMA 12, delayed open transition) and Add. 4 Clarification 5 (permanent ATS, temporary generator connects to it) (plans_10 p.2; Add. 4 p.1) [Verified].
