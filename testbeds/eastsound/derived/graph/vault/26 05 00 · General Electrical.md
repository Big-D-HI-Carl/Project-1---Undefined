## Summary

Drawings and specifications are complementary; motor horsepower on the drawings is an estimate for equipment furnished under other sections, with overloads sized to actual nameplate current (¶1.011 B–C, p.274) [Verified]. Submittals (¶1.012, p.274): electronic PDF with an index, identified by section and paragraph or equipment, each sheet naming the equipment and part number with manufacturer, model, nameplate data, dimensions and local representative; largely incomplete packages are returned unreviewed; variances requested in writing [Verified]. Construction power and downtime (¶1.015–1.016, p.276): the contractor provides and pays for a dedicated construction power service and its energy; power to the operating plant is kept at all times, with one week notice to the District before any utility disruption, and temporary equipment (generator, pumps, piping) plus an Owner/Engineer-approved written plan for any disruption over 20 minutes [Verified]. Utility interface (¶3.03, pp.279–280): OPALCO serves the site and the Owner pays utility charges; the contractor provides the new 480Y/277V underground secondary from the existing transformer pad to the CT enclosure, service-rated ATS and 400 A panel, the CT enclosure and meter bases, demolition of the existing 208V service (raceway, conductors, CT enclosure, meter base, generator, ATS) and temporary power to hold downtime to 20 minutes; the Utility removes the existing 208V transformer, sets the new 480V transformer on the existing pad and provides the CTs and meter [Verified]. Pads, tests and training (¶2.04, ¶3.04, ¶3.05, pp.278–280): a concrete housekeeping pad under each floor-mounted MCC and electrical equipment, 3 in wider each end and 3 in minimum height; feeder, motor and equipment testing and a functional checkout of every control circuit, with procedures submitted first; training of 8 h for electrical and 24 h for control systems [Verified].
