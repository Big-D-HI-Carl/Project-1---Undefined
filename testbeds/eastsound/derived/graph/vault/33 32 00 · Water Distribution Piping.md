## Summary

Covers HDPE, PVC and DI water main and appurtenances, chlorination, pressure testing and start-up, and as-built records per 01 70 00 (¶1.01 A; main spec p.436 — Verified). Submittals: field-fabrication data, material certifications, submittals for all pipe and appurtenances (fittings, valves, valve boxes, services), and a water main shut-down / temporary bypass plan with customer notices (one week for construction, 48 hours for shutdowns) (¶1.03; pp.436–437 — Verified). Buried potable pipe is AWWA C900 Class 235 DR 18 PVC or Class 50 cement-lined DI, both poly-encased fittings; potable service pipe is PE4710 HDPE (AWWA C901, ASTM F714) from continuous coil with fusion joints; cover is 3 ft minimum (¶2.02–2.03, ¶3.04 C; pp.438, 441 — Verified). Fire hydrants are Iowa or M.H. 929T with a 5-in Storz port, shutoff valve 5–20 ft away, crash posts where exposed, a 200 psi hydrostatic test and a flow test, and installation per 'ESWD Standard Detail W-1', which is not in the library (¶2.011; pp.439–440 — Verified). Testing and disinfection follow 33 30 00, and only Association staff may operate isolation valves on the existing system (¶3.05–3.06; p.443 — Verified).
