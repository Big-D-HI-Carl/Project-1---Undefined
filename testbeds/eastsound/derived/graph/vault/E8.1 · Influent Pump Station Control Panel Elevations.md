## Summary

Exterior: NEMA 4X Type 316 stainless steel enclosure with drip shield, sized to hold all equipment, on a concrete equipment pad "per civil specifications"; key note 1 calls for local control of the influent pumps, seal-fail and overtemperature wiring and instrumentation, with controls on an internal swing-out door behind a lockable outer door (plans_11 p.5) [Verified; enclosure label Verified-Visual]. Swing-out door: influent wet well level display; for each of pumps 1–4 a hand-off-auto switch and run, fail, seal-fail and overtemperature lights with reset; four float-activated lights with float test buttons (plans_11 p.5) [Verified-Visual; labels also in text]. Backpanel: main 480 V disconnect; panel LP2 with its transformer and overcurrent protection; area light hand-off-auto and GFCI receptacle; panel heater and thermostat; power distribution block and phase monitor; pump circuit breakers and full-voltage NEMA starters for influent pumps 1–4; field terminals for motor connections; network switch and PLC rack; DC power supply and UPS; interposing relays; seal-fail relays; intrinsically safe area with barriers for the floats and level transducer (plans_11 p.5) [Verified]. Key notes 2–4: flow meter transmitters for influent, Cell 1 (future space), Cell 2, Cell 3 and plant drain, with coil and electrode cables from the meter vaults; seal-fail and overtemperature relays (Mini-CAS or equal) furnished by the pump manufacturer and coordinated by the system integrator; field conduits not shown — route all raceway shown on the plans to the influent control panel (plans_11 p.5) [Verified]. The backpanel takes "power from MCC" and holds the pump starters, agreeing with E6.1 rather than E6.3 on the influent pump feeders; the WAS/dewatering meter is not in the transmitter list, consistent with its local display (E4.1 key note 2) — see Issues (plans_11 p.5; plans_10 pp.2, 4; plans_9 p.6) [Verified-Visual; WAS reading Inferred].
