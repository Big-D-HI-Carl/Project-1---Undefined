## Summary

Covers excavation, hauling, disposal, backfill, compaction and final grading; cites the 'September 7, 2021' GeoEngineers report as Appendix C (¶1.01; main spec p.383 — Verified; date conflict already flagged, 02 Merge flag 2). Submittals: gradation and moisture-density curves for imported materials, separation geotextile data, and proposed compaction equipment; field density testing is by the Engineer's independent lab (one test per 100 LF of roadway, 1,000 SF of subgrade, 500 CY of structural backfill), with Contractor-paid retests (¶1.02–1.03; pp.383–384 — Verified). Crushed surfacing base course goes under all slabs and foundations: 12 in minimum for mat foundations and vaults, 18 in for shallow foundations, 24 in for pile structures (¶3.01 D; p.386 — Verified). Structural fill is placed in 10-in loose lifts and compacted to 95% MDD (ASTM D1557); unsuitable subgrade is over-excavated 18 in and replaced with CSBC on written authorization, with haul receipts to the Owner (¶3.03, ¶3.05–3.06; pp.387–388 — Verified). All fill materials are specified by WSDOT section (9-03.9(3), 9-03.10, 9-03.12, 9-03.14, 9-03.19 and others), and dewatering and boulder excavation are incidental (¶2.01, ¶1.04 B; pp.384–386 — Verified; material gradations Unresolved: external reference, not staged).
