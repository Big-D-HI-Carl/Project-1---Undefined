## Summary

Five Allen-Bradley 1769-IQ16 16-point 24 VDC input cards (80 channels) with 74 field points drawn (plans_10 p.8) [Verified-Visual; counts taken from the image]. Card 1: DC UPS power normal and replace battery, main panel network switch status, ATS utility power normal and emergency position, generator running and alarm, UV1 and UV2 controller status, and digester blower hand, auto, VFD running and VFD fault; cards 2–3: aeration blowers 1–4 hand, auto, VFD running and VFD fault, high motor temperature (key note 1, blower supplier PTC relay) and high enclosure temperature for the digester blower and aeration blower 1–4 enclosures, and the Blower Building intrusion door switch and smoke detector (plans_10 p.8) [Verified-Visual; labels also in text]. Card 4: Treatment Cell 1 and Cell 2 control panels (greyed, "FUTURE") and the Treatment Cell 3 control panel, each running, alarm and shutdown; 2W pump station floats F1–F4; WAS pump 1. Card 5: WAS pump 2, sludge pump, and 2W pumps 1 and 2, each hand, auto, VFD running and VFD fault (plans_10 p.8) [Verified-Visual]. Numbering errors: card 4 carries 17 points on 16 channels (DI14 used twice), card 5 numbers both 2W pumps DI13, DI14, DI14, DI15, and fuse label FU-DI3 repeats on cards 3 and 5; the 74 points fit the 80 channels if renumbered — see Issues (plans_10 p.8) [Verified-Visual; fit Inferred by count].
