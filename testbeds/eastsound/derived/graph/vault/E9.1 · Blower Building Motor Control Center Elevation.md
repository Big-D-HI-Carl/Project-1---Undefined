## Summary

MCC elevation: seven 20-in sections, 140 in (11.7 ft) long, 90 in high, on a 4-in pad; general note 1 makes the contractor verify and provide the final layout and names the MCC as Allen-Bradley Centerline 2100 IntelliCENTER (plans_12 p.3) [Verified-Visual; general note not in the text layer]. Add. 4 p.1 Clarification 4 answers a question on this sheet: the MCC grows to eight 20-in sections, 160 in total, with the Blower Building footprint adjusted to 9.25 ft x 44.00 ft — Add. 4 governs; what goes in the added section is not stated (Add. 4 p.1; plans_12 p.3) [Verified]. Nameplate schedule, section 1–2: Ethernet switch NP-1A, surge protector NP-1D, power monitor NP-1E, 400 A main breaker NP-1H; feeders for Train 1 and Train 2 panels (future) NP-2A/NP-2B, Train 3 panel NP-2C, dewatering skid NP-2D and Influent Pump Station NP-2E; spaces NP-2G, NP-2K (plans_12 p.3) [Verified]. Sections 3–6 hold ten VFD units: 2W pumps no.1 and no.2 (5 HP) NP-3A/NP-3D, sludge pump (3 HP) NP-4A, WAS pumps no.1 and no.2 (3 HP) NP-4D/NP-4G, digester blower (5 HP) NP-4K, aeration blowers no.1–4 (20 HP) NP-5A, NP-5G, NP-6A, NP-6G; spaces NP-3G, NP-3K (plans_12 p.3) [Verified]. Section 7: lighting panel 120/240 V 1-ph NP-7A, transformer disconnect NP-7G and 480–120/240 V 20 kVA transformer NP-7J — LP1 and T2 (plans_12 p.3) [Verified; LP1/T2 match Inferred from E6.1].
