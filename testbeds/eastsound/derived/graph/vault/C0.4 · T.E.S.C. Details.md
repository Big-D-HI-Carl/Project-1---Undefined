## Summary

Five TESC details, all not to scale: 1 Plastic Covering for Slopes & Stockpiles, 2 Catch Basin Insert, 3 Filter Fabric Fence, 4 Stabilized Construction Entrance, 5 Geotextile Encased Check Dam (BMP C208) (plans_2Part-3 p.1, C0.4 — Verified; detail numbers Verified-Visual). Plastic covering is 6-mil minimum with 12-in seam overlap and toes buried in 4x6-in trenches; catch basin inserts go in before clearing and grading and are emptied when half full (Details 1–2 notes; plans_2Part-3 p.1, C0.4 — Verified-Visual). The construction entrance is 4–8-in quarry spalls on geotextile, 12 in minimum thick and 100 ft minimum long, based on Figure II-3.1 of the 2019 Stormwater Management Manual for Western Washington (Detail 4; plans_2Part-3 p.1, C0.4 — Verified-Visual). The filter fabric fence defers to WSDOT Standard Plans I-30.10-02 or I-30.15-02 (high-visibility fence I-30.16-01 or I-30.17-01), and the check dam notes defer to 'Standard Specifications' 8-01.3(6)A, 9-14.5(4) and 8-01.3(15) (Details 3, 5; plans_2Part-3 p.1, C0.4 — Verified-Visual; Unresolved: external reference, not staged). The Contractor is responsible for updating coverage under Ecology's Construction Stormwater General Permit (Detail 5 note; plans_2Part-3 p.1, C0.4 — Verified-Visual).
