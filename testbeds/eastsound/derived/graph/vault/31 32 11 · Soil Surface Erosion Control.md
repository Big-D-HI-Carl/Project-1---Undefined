## Summary

Covers temporary erosion and sediment control under an Ecology Construction Stormwater General Permit (NPDES) whose SWPPP sets the requirements; the permit transfers to the Contractor at contract execution and the Contractor pays the yearly fees (¶1.01 A; main spec p.398 — Verified). Submittals: geotextile for silt fence, compost for compost berm, inlet protection products, barrier fence, track clean plates and any other erosion control product; the installer must be a specialist (¶1.04–1.05; p.398 — Verified). Products: silt fence to WSDOT 9-33 Table 6, straw mulch 9-14.4(1), compost 9-14.4(8), orange barrier fence 9-14.5(8), inlet protection per plans, specs and San Juan County catch basin insert requirements, and Track Clean construction entrance plates or equal per BMP C105 (¶2.01; p.399 — Verified; WSDOT materials Unresolved: external reference, not staged). All BMPs go in before land disturbance, stockpiles are covered with secured plastic, and areas stripped with no work for 14 days are stabilized immediately (¶3.01–3.02; pp.399–400 — Verified). The Contractor and Owner inspect BMPs periodically, the Contractor corrects impairments immediately and sweeps streets if soil migrates (¶3.03; p.400 — Verified).
