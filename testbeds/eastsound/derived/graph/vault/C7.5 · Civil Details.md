## Summary

Four details, not to scale: 1 Typical Plant Water (2W) Yard Hydrant Stations, 2 Post Hydrant Detail, 3 Traffic Manhole Ring Details (double manhole condition), 4 Influent Sample Sump (plans_6 p.7, C7.5 — Verified; numbering Verified-Visual). 2W yard hydrant stations use a 1-in frost-proof hydrant on a 2-in HDPE supply with a 2-in gate valve and valve box, a free-standing or wall-mounted aluminum hose rack, and a red 'CAUTION: 2W PLANT WATER NON POTABLE' sign; pipe color follows 'Orange Book' standards per 'Section 15051', which is not in the project manual (Detail 1; plans_6 p.7, C7.5 — Verified-Visual; see Issues). The post hydrant ('see plan for size') is fed by a 2-in HDPE line with a buried 2-in gate valve, thrust block, drain rock and concrete collar (Detail 2; plans_6 p.7, C7.5 — Verified-Visual). The double manhole condition uses lightweight composite manholes (Morrison Bros. 418LC-3800 AM or equal, 35-5/16-in minimum ID, locking, gasketed, 55 lb cover) in a 3,000 psi cast-in-place ring on Class A foundation gravel per WSDOT 9-03.12(1)A (Detail 3; plans_6 p.7, C7.5 — Verified-Visual; WSDOT part Unresolved: external reference, not staged). The influent sample sump is a 24-in by 6-ft precast well curb (H2 Precast WC-24 or equal) on a 12-in precast base, top flush with pavement at about 23.50, with 8-in DI gravity in at IE 19.65 (field-confirm), 8-in influent at IE 19.55, a 4-in drain at IE 18.20, a 2-in sampling conduit, and the traffic-rated lid of 3/C7.1 (Detail 4; plans_6 p.7, C7.5 — Verified-Visual).
