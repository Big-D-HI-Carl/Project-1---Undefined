## Summary

Three 3 HP packaged sludge pumps "per 22 13 36 packaged sludge pump equipment" (keyed note 8) sit side by side against an existing building exterior wall: a WAS pump, a swing pump (standby for either the WAS or the digested sludge pump) and a digested sludge pump. WAS #1, #2 and #3 each enter through an ASCO Redhat series 8221 2" normally closed solenoid valve (catalog 8221G013, 120 V coil, 150 psi max differential, brass body, stainless internals, NEMA 4X; keyed note 1) between 2" PVC manual ball valves (keyed note 7); WAS goes "to digester" in a 2" line carrying a 2" flow meter "per electrical design requirements" between 2" unions (keyed notes 2, 3). Digested sludge comes "from digester" in 4" SCH 80 PVC (keyed note 9) with a 4"×2" reducer, and the digested sludge pump discharges through a 2"×3" expansion "to rotary fan press"; manifold piping is 2" SCH 80 PVC (keyed note 4) on supports per 5/C7.2 (keyed note 5), with the wall annulus filled watertight with Dow Corning 732 sealant (keyed note 6). General note: coordinate piping alignment with the Owner and field configure for best access.
