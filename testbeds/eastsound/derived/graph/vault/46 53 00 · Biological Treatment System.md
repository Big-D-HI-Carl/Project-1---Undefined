## Summary

Specifies Train 3 ("3rd Train") as a single bullseye multistage anoxic/aerobic activated sludge system, basis of design Smith & Loveless Model R Oxigest, with the in-basin aeration system, mixers, clarifier equipment, instrumentation and controls supplied and guaranteed by one manufacturer (¶1.01 A p.492; ¶1.03 A p.493); this section's valves, equipment, materials and controls supersede other specifications (¶1.03 F p.493), and alternate systems are bid as an Equipment Alternate with a submittal package (¶1.03 E, ¶1.04 p.493). Design influent is 63,333 gpd average to 178 gpm peak hour, with effluent BOD5 and TSS 30 mg/L and TIN 8 mg/L (¶1.02 p.492); the supplier gives a 2-year process performance guarantee with the equipment submittal and a 12-month mechanical guarantee from Substantial Completion (¶1.05 A, C p.494). The tank has a reinforced concrete outer wall and structural steel plate inner walls of at least 1/4" (¶2.01 B.1 p.494), 32 ft ID, 15.5 ft static depth and 16.9 ft overall wall height, 43,000 gal of annular aeration and 26,508 gal of anoxic volume in two compartments, each with a 5 HP 460 V submersible mixer, and 240 scfm through S&L medium bubble diffusers plus 75 scfm of airlift air, 315 scfm total (¶2.01 C.1 p.495; header and drop pipes ¶2.01 C.2 p.496). The 18 ft clarifier has 1.4 ft freeboard, a 1:12 concrete floor placed by the purchaser, an 800 ft-lb drive with a 1/2 HP 460 V motor, a scum airlift discharging to the aerobic digester and drive-failure alarm contacts; the system also includes a 3'-0" walkway, a 2'-0" service walkway, aluminum grating, handrail and ladder, a RAS airlift (51 gpm at 15 cfm, 3" riser), MLR airlifts (two at 101 gpm and 30 cfm, 6" risers) and carbon feed by two Stenner S3V02AA302N peristaltic pumps, 1 duty and 1 standby (¶2.01 B.2–B.3 p.495; ¶2.01 C.3–C.8 pp.496–498). Blowers are in 43 11 00 and air piping in 33 31 00 ¶2.01 D, the air header gets a 15 psi, 60-minute low-pressure air test (¶2.01 D–E p.498), and the manufacturer furnishes a NEMA 4X 316 SS control panel and spares and performs programming, start-up and a witnessed Functional Acceptance Test (¶2.01 F–G p.499; ¶3.03 pp.500–501).
