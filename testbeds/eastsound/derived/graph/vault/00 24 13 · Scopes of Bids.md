## Summary

Describes where costs belong for each bid item in 00 41 00; each item is furnished, installed and tested, complete and in full operation before Owner acceptance (p.16; Verified). Item 1 WWTP Upgrade (LS) is paid per the approved Schedule of Values and covers all work in the Contract Documents; Items 2–5 cover trench safety (per WSDOT and OSHA — Unresolved: external reference, not staged), 12 bollards at Owner-chosen locations, SWPPP preparation with transfer of the NPDES construction stormwater permit, and SWPPP maintenance (¶1–5, pp.16–17; Verified except as tagged). Item 6 Electrical covers all E sheets and Division 26 (breakdowns in 26 05 00 and 26 80 00), and Item 7 Minor Changes covers changes of $100,000 or less that do not change Contract Time (¶6–7, p.17; Verified). Items 8–16 are unit-price contingencies for HMA, structural concrete, over-excavation and aggregates, and Item 17 is force-account SCADA/PLC programming; aggregate specs and force account defer to WSDOT 9-03 and 1-09.6 (¶8–17, pp.17–20; Unresolved: external reference, not staged). Bid Alternates 1 and 2 (Items 18, 19) add Type 316 stainless steel for Train 3 in-basin steel components above water (to 1 ft below) or throughout, excluding FRP weirs and covers, per 46 53 00 (¶18–19, p.20; Verified); the Item 18 conflict with Add. 4 is flagged in 02/03/04 (cited, not re-raised).
