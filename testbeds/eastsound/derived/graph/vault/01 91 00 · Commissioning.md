## Summary

Sets minimum supplier start-up supervision and operator training as a non-exhaustive checklist; every entry covers installation inspection, motor amperage checks, access, controls operation, an O&M manual review and controls training (¶1.01.B, pp.157–160; Verified). Minimums: influent pump station submersible pumps 5 days; Train 3 biological treatment equipment 2 weeks (clarifier drive, mixers, valves, DO and pH sensors, air lift pumps; weirs and baffles set level); blowers 2 days; UV system 2 days (intensity monitoring); peristaltic WAS and digested sludge pumps 2 days (¶1.02–1.06, pp.157–158; Verified). Dewatering equipment suppliers each give "a minimum of one (2) days" (¶1.07, p.159; Unresolved — Issues 1.21); the 2W water system supplier 1 day, verifying constant 60–90 psi from 0 to 50 gpm, the automatic valve at the air gap and the floats; the magnetic flow meter supplier 2 days, checking that submerged units are sealed and rated (¶1.10–1.11, pp.159–160; Verified). Paragraph numbers jump from 1.07 to 1.10 and ¶1.11 starts at "B" (p.159; Issues 2.8). No paragraph covers the generators, transfer switch, MCC or PLC panel — the list is stated as not exhaustive (¶1.01.B.3; Verified).
