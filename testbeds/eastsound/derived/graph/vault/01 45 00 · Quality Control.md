## Summary

Splits inspection and testing between the parties: the Contractor keeps records of all inspections and tests, pays for repeat tests after any failure (including Owner-responsibility tests), gives 48 hours' notice before work needing inspection, and uses only Owner-approved laboratories (¶1.05.A–E, ¶1.06.C, pp.137–139; Verified). Contractor-paid: pipe pressure testing and potable water pipe disinfection per the technical sections, equipment testing per the equipment sections (Division 46, p.141), clean water leakage testing, and clean water mechanical testing of pumps, diffusers, mixers and air lift assemblies (¶1.05.B.1, p.137; Verified). Owner-paid, Contractor-coordinated: building special inspections for concrete and structural steel (the p.141 summary adds air barrier testing per WA Energy Code Section 402), earthwork, concrete, asphalt (Rice density, compaction, extraction/gradation), coatings and field welding (¶1.05.B.2, pp.137–138, p.141; Verified); the WSDOT/AASHTO compaction and asphalt testing standards (¶1.03.B, p.136) are Unresolved: external reference, not staged. Before startup, eight new structures get a fill-to-rim clean water leakage test held 2 hours with no visible leakage — influent pump station, flow splitter, aeration basins, anoxic basins, clarifier, UV disinfection chamber, digester, 2W water system pump station (¶1.09, pp.139–140; Verified; repeated in 01 50 00 — Issues 1.22). The Owner's on-site representative cannot change the contract or accept work, and missed defects can still be rejected later (¶1.07–1.08, p.139; Verified).
