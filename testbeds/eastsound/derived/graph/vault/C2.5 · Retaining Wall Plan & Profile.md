## Summary

Plan and profile of a block retaining wall from STA 0+05.00 (begin) to STA 1+31.00 (end), with corner coordinates in state plane values (begin N 629481.3930, E 1139430.8639) taken at the center of block at finished asphalt grade (plans_3Part-8 p.1, C2.5 — Verified-Visual). Keyed notes: 1 block retaining wall per Detail 1/C7.8; 2 waterproof expansive foam to fill the gap between proposed and existing walls; 3 relocated power and communications as needed per the demolition plan; 4 126 LF of chain link fence connected to the existing fence per specifications (plans_3Part-8 p.1, C2.5 — Verified-Visual). The fence height mark in Keyed Note 4 reads 6" at stored resolution (plans_3Part-8 p.1, C2.5 — Verified-Visual; see Issues). The profile shows wall breaks at STA 0+22.56, 0+33.81, 0+71.31, 1+08.06, 1+20.81 and 1+22.31 (plans_3Part-8 p.1, C2.5 — Verified-Visual). Wall and coordinate details are on 1/C7.8 and 5/C7.7, both unavailable (plans_3Part-8 p.1 — Verified; 01 item 9).
