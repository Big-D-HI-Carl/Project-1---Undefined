## Summary

General notes: conduit routing is schematic and the electrical contractor chooses routes and circuit combinations; equipment locations are schematic and the contractor coordinates connection requirements with each manufacturer (plans_12 p.7) [Verified]. Key note 1: flow meters sized per civil drawings — Endress+Hauser Promag W400, Class I Div 2, carbon steel flange, 120–240 VAC or 24 VAC/DC, EtherNet/IP, polyurethane liner, 316L electrodes, remote housing, IP68 Type 6 potted submersible sensor, two 316L grounding rings per tube; consistent with Add. 4 p.2 (26 80 00 ¶2.04 D) (plans_12 p.7; Add. 4 p.2) [Verified]. Flow meter summary: influent 6 in, Train #2 and Train #3 8 in and plant drain 4 in, all in vaults with submersible kits and transmitters in the influent control panel; Train #1 8 in is a "FUTURE PROJECT - NOT IN THIS PROJECT" with transmitter space only; effluent 8 in in a vault with its transmitter in a panel near the UV chamber; WAS 2 in and 2W 1 in (Hot Box #1) with transmitters on the tube (plans_12 p.7) [Verified-Visual; the summary is image-only]. Vault flow meter standard detail: "CLASS I DIV 2 HAZARDOUS AREA INSIDE FLOW METER VAULT", 1/2 in liquid-tight flexible conduit for the factory electrode and coil cables, NEMA 4X junction box, seal-off fitting, grounding rings; remote display wiring detail with separate electrode and coil cables and Cat 6 Ethernet to the switch and PLC. The Class I Div 2 note conflicts with E0.4 for the effluent vault — see Issues (plans_12 p.7; plans_9 p.1) [Verified-Visual]. Chemical feed equipment detail, key notes 2–3: a dedicated GFCI receptacle from the lighting panel for each metering pump and a 4–20 mA PLC output scaled in proportion to the flow meter signal (plans_12 p.7) [Verified].
