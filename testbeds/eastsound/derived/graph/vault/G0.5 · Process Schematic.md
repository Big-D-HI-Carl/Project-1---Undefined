## Summary

8" STEP influent and an influent sample sump feed the Influent Pump Station, which pumps through a 6" influent flow meter to the flow splitter; the splitter feeds Treatment Cell #1 (Train #1) on INF1, Treatment Cell #2 (Train #2) through an 8" Train #2 flow meter on INF2, and New Treatment Cell #3 (Train #3) through an 8" Train #3 flow meter on INF3, with a fourth outlet marked FUTURE. Secondary effluent SE1–SE3 passes an 8" effluent flow meter into UV Channels #1 and #2, then 10" final effluent runs to SSMH *1288 and the 12" outfall; a 4" UV chamber drain is shown. WAS1–WAS3 go to a WAS pump and the aerobic digester above the UV chamber; a digested sludge pump with a flow meter feeds the rotary fan press and the existing screw press (drawn gray), and the 4" pressate drain returns through a 4" plant drain flow meter to the pump station. Train #3 shows Anoxic 1, Anoxic 2, Aeration 1, Aeration 2 and a clarifier, with ML recycle airlift pumps, a RAS airlift pump, and a carbon addition point into Anoxic 2. Legend: isolation valve, flow direction, flow meter (FM), airlift pump.
