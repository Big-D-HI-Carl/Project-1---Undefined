## Summary

Design basis is the 2018 IBC, Risk Category III, 100 psf floor live load, 25 psf ground and flat-roof snow (Ct 1.1, Ce 1.1, Is 1.1), 105 mph ultimate wind (Exposure C), and seismic Site Class D (default), SDC D, Ie 1.25, SDS 0.79g, SD1 0.51g [plans_7 p.8, S1.1 General Notes 1–6 — Verified-Visual]. Foundations use 2,500 psf allowable bearing (×1.33 for wind or seismic), a minimum 1'-6" frost cover, and the GeoEngineers report dated September 7, 2021 (clouded, revision 2) for subgrade, backfill and compaction [plans_7 p.8, Foundations & Subgrade 1, 2, 8 — Verified-Visual; report-date conflict already flagged in 02 Unresolved spec items #2 and the 03 Merge flag — cited, not re-raised]. The concrete material schedule sets 4,500 psi and 0.45 max w/cm for three groups: Blower Building slab-on-grade, grade beams and misc. equipment pads (3/4" aggregate, 6% air, 30% min fly ash/slag); hydraulic structures (1" aggregate, air per notes, 20%); and the "Drain Pump Station Lid" (3/4", 5%, 20%) [plans_7 p.8, Reinforced Concrete 2 — Verified-Visual]. Minimum structural shop drawings are concrete reinforcing (including wall elevations), mix designs, a slab-on-grade jointing plan and structural steel; deferred submittals, sealed by a Washington PE, are metal stairs & landings and the pre-engineered metal building for the Blower Building [plans_7 p.8, Submittals 4–5 — Verified-Visual]. Structural steel follows AISC 303-16 with A992 W/WT, A500 Gr B HSS, A53 Gr B pipe, A325 bolts, F1554 Gr 36 anchor rods, A36 misc. steel and E70XX welds, exterior steel hot-dip galvanized; a structural special inspection schedule covers steel, concrete, post-installed anchors, and soils and foundations [plans_7 p.8, Structural Steel 1–4 and Special Inspection schedule — Verified-Visual].
