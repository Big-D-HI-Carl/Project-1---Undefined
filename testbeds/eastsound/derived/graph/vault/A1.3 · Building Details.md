## Summary

Interior elevations of the proposed Blower Building show the four blowers, and the general note leaves process air and 2W piping off for clarity, pointing to civil sheets C1.1, C6.4 and C6.5 [plans_7 p.7 — Verified (labels); Verified-Visual (note, elevations)]. At the existing office building, the existing 4'-0" × 4'-0" louvered vent (south elevation) and 2' × 3' louvered vent (east elevation) are removed, the metal siding is replaced floor to eave with continuous siding to match (no horizontal seams), and the existing generator exhaust assembly is removed [plans_7 p.7 — Verified-Visual]. At the existing WWTP building, a new 3' × 7' louvered vent and a new 2' × 2' (24" × 24") exhaust fan are framed in, trimmed to match existing louvers and caulked, each marked "typical for all three locations," with final locations coordinated with the Owner [plans_7 p.7 — Verified-Visual]. Existing wall heights are shown as 9.50'± and the new exhaust fan is set 6.00' above the concrete slab floor [plans_7 p.7 — Verified-Visual].
