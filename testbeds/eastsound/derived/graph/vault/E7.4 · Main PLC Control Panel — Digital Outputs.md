## Summary

One Allen-Bradley 1769-OB16 16-point 24 VDC output card driving interposing relays CR0–CR15 (plans_11 p.1) [Verified-Visual; card and relay labels also in text]. Outputs 0–9 are "call to run" contacts into the MCC VFD control circuits: aeration blowers 1–4 (outputs 0–3), digester blower (4), WAS pumps 1 and 2 (5–6), sludge pump (7), and 2W pumps 1 and 2 (8–9) (plans_11 p.1) [Verified-Visual]. Output 10 switches 120 V to the 2W solenoid valve "call to open" at Hot Box #1; outputs 11–15 are spares, so 11 of 16 are used (plans_11 p.1) [Verified-Visual]. The text layer sets "CR10 … 2W PUMP #1 … OUT 10" and "CR12 2W SOLENOID VALVE … OUT 12" side by side; the image shows OUT 8 for 2W pump #1 and OUT 10 for the solenoid, and the assignments here follow the image (workflow log) (plans_11 p.1) [Verified-Visual].
