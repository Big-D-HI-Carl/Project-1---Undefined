## Summary

Ecology WPCRF clauses are incorporated and take precedence in any conflict within the contract (p.41; Verified) — not placed in the 00 73 00 SC 2 order (Issues 2.7). Required with the bid: Certification of Nonsegregated Facilities (Attachment 3), EPA DBE forms 6100-4 and 6100-3 (one per DBE sub; forms on the Ecology website), and the complete Bidders List (p.41; Verified); pp.45–46 then allow the DBE forms and the Bidders List "as part of the bid, or within one hour" after bid time (Verified) — timing conflicts inside this section and with 00 43 93 and 01 33 00 (Issues 1.6). Site and records clauses: safe access for Ecology and EPA, separate auditable project records kept 3 years after final audit, no discharge to state waters without Ecology approval, a funding-recognition sign visible from nearby roadways for the life of the project with Department logos, and the Inadvertent Discovery Plan kept on site with work stopped on any discovery (pp.41–42; Verified). American Iron and Steel applies to treatment works: iron and steel products (pipe and fittings, castings, hydrants, tanks, flanges, restraints, valves, structural steel, reinforced precast, construction materials) must be US-produced unless waived, with certification on request and damages for non-compliance (pp.42–43; Verified). Davis-Bacon, debarment, DBE six good faith efforts, 30-day sub payment, EEO (EO 11246) and segregated-facilities clauses follow, with Attachment 1 (DB terms, pp.53–61), Attachment 2 (DB wage determination — an unfilled placeholder, p.62; Issues 2.4), Attachment 3 (p.64) and Attachment 4 notice to labor unions (p.65) (pp.43–65; Verified).
