## Summary

Grounding system detail (service equipment ground bus bonded to metallic cold-water piping and to foundation rebar at least once; contractor provides all grounding and bonding per NEC 250) and ground rod detail (precast grounding well, ground loop copper wire, rod) (plans_12 p.6) [Verified-Visual; detail text not in the text layer]. Electrical handhole detail: 3 ft-8 in x 2 ft-8 in precast vault, basis of design Oldcastle Precast #233-LA (vault #233-BL, cover #23-2436P) or equal, upsized where the NEC requires; galvanized diamond-plate traffic cover with locking latch, grounded per NEC; 6 in sump over compacted drainage rock (coordinate with civil); 18 x 18 in knockouts each end; 8 ft copper-clad ground rod; covers labeled "ELECTRIC" or "SIGNAL" (plans_12 p.6) [Verified]. Secondary and feeder raceway detail: burial 24 in for feeders and 36 in for secondary and primary, 18 in minimum trench width, "ELECTRICAL" warning tape 12 in below grade, backfill per civil; raceway notes require 12 in separation from water utilities, 2 in between conduits and to the trench wall, and sand bedding 3 in below and above the conduit (plans_12 p.6) [Verified-Visual]. Equipment rack detail (stainless Unistrut on posts set in concrete); intrinsically safe relay connection detail (floats and submersible transducer on factory cables to explosion-proof junction boxes with seal-off fittings in a handhole outside the wet well, then to the intrinsically safe area in the control panels); wet well instrumentation detail (floats and transducer on stainless hooks with Kellems grips, watertight core-drilled riser penetrations) (plans_12 p.6) [Verified]. The wet well detail labels a "LOW LEVEL FLOAT (TYP)", while E4.2 and E8.3 show four high-level floats and no low-level float — see Issues (plans_12 p.6; plans_9 p.7; plans_11 p.7) [Verified-Visual].
