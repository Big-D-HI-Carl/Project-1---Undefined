## Summary

Covers new storm sewer pipe, catch basins, roof drains and retaining wall drainage; submittals are catch basins with frames, grates and lids, and storm and roof drain piping and accessories (¶1.01, ¶1.03; main spec p.447 — Verified). Storm pipe is double-wall corrugated polyethylene (ADS N12, Hancor HiQ or equal), ductile iron where cover is under 18 in, and CDF-encased where cover is under 1 ft; roof drains are PVC and wall footing drains are perforated corrugated PE with PVC tight lines (¶2.02 A, ¶3.02 D.7; pp.447–449 — Verified; WSDOT 9-05.20, 9-05.13, 9-05.1(5), 9-05.2(7) Unresolved: external reference, not staged). Type 1 and 1L catch basins, frames, covers and herringbone grates follow WSDOT 9-05.50(3), 9-05.15 and Standard Plans B-5.20-03, B-5.40-02, B-30.10-03, B-30.20-04 and B-30.50-03, installed per WSDOT 7-05.3 with frames on precast grade rings in a full mortar bed (¶2.02 C, ¶3.02 E; pp.448–449 — Verified; Unresolved: external reference, not staged). Add. 4 adds ¶2.02 F: trench drains are Zurn Z886-HD or approved equal, HDPE channels with 0.75% built-in slope, 4-in inside width, 80-in minimum lengths with interlocking ends, black Dura-coated heavy-duty steel frames with embedment studs, and slotted ductile iron grates (5.375 in wide, about 18.5 sq in open area per LF) bolted after final inspection, Load Class C — Add. 4 governs (Add. 4 p.2 — Verified). Footing drain bedding, backfill and construction follow Sections 3.5 and 3.7 of the geotechnical report 'dated September 7, 2021', the Contractor provides construction layout staking, roof drains tight-line to catch basins with 12 in minimum cover, and removed storm sewer is fully removed or plugged (¶2.02 E, ¶3.02 B, F–H; pp.448–450 — Verified; date already flagged, 02 Merge flag 2; staking conflict, see Issues).
