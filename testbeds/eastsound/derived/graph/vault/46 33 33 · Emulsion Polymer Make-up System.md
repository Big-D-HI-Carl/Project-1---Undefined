## Summary

One fully operational emulsion (liquid) polymer make-up system that activates and blends neat polymer (40–55% active) into a homogeneous 0.1–0.5% solution (¶1.01 A p.481). Components: a water pressure reducing valve set to 40 psi (supply 45–100 psi) with gauge; a proportional dilution-water valve (ball valve and positioner needing at least 90 psi air; flow "0.1 to 20 GPM (0.4 to 75 LPH)" as printed — Issue 29) regulated on the polymer-to-sludge ratio from the Rotary Press PLC, using the flow meter on the flocculator supplied with the press; a peristaltic metering pump (0.1–500 mL/min, up to 7 bar) with an inlet strainer; and a static mixer, on a 304 SS frame with HDPE water piping (Part 2 items 2.1–2.5 pp.481–482). Electrical: a 24 VDC, 4–20 mA proportional valve, a 120 VAC or 24 VDC pump with 4–20 mA control, and a NEMA 4X junction box with a 120 VAC/24 VDC supply, powered from a 120 VAC outlet or the Fournier control panel and run from the Rotary Press PLC and HMI in hand or auto (items 3.0–4.0 p.482). The materials list names Fournier Industries (frame, brackets), Watson-Marlow (dosing pump), Koflo (static mixer), Watts (regulator), Festo (proportional valve), Hoffman (10×10×8 junction box) and McMaster (valves, strainer, gauges, hose), with one metering pump head kit as the spare (items 6.0–7.0 pp.482–483); p.484 carries no text. Submittals cite "Section 01340" with telescoping-valve wording (¶1.02 A p.481; Issue 29), installation follows the manufacturer's instructions with no testing specified (¶3.01 p.485), and the liquid system conflicts with C6.1's "dry polymer mix unit" label (Issue 16).
