## Summary

Ventilation control panel: NEMA 4X stainless steel enclosure; door carries H2S alarm and methane alarm lights, run lights and OFF/ON switches for ventilation fans 1–3, and an alarm horn (plans_12 p.5) [Verified-Visual]. Wiring: 120 V "FROM LP1"; each fan switch energizes a call-to-run relay (CR1–CR3) whose contact lights the run light and reports "called to run" to the Influent Pump Station PLC; PLC alarm outputs sound the horn and light the H2S and methane alarms (plans_12 p.5) [Verified; relay-to-PLC pairing Verified-Visual]. Fan 2 coil is printed CR3 while its contacts are CR2 (label error in the diagram) (plans_12 p.5) [Verified-Visual]. The panel is the Treatment Building unit (gas alarms, fans 1–3, IPS PLC points on E8.3/E8.4), but LP2 circuit 5 feeds the ventilation control panel on E6.2 and E4.1 — see Issues (plans_12 p.5; plans_10 p.3; plans_9 p.6) [Verified; unit identity Inferred].
