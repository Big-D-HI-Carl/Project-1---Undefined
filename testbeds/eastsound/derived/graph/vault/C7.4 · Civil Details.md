## Summary

Five details, not to scale: 1 D.O. Probe Elevation (typ.), 2 Typical Trenching & Backfill, 3 Typical Mechanical Equipment Pad, 4 Concrete Pavement Detail, 5 Trench Drain Detail (plans_6 p.6, C7.4 — Verified; numbering Verified-Visual). Trenches get 36 in minimum cover over water and sewer mains, pipe-zone gravel bedding and bank-run gravel or select native backfill at 95%, a 2-in metallic detector tape 8–12 in below grade, #10 AWG tracer wire on nonmetallic pipe, and restoration by hydroseed over 4-in topsoil, new crushed surfacing top course, or HMA Class 1/2 in two lifts matching the existing section (4 in minimum) (Detail 2 Keyed Notes A–I; plans_6 p.6, C7.4 — Verified-Visual). Material and paving callouts defer to WSDOT 9-03.9(3), 5-04.2, 9-03.19 and 9-03.12(3) (Detail 2; plans_6 p.6, C7.4 — Verified-Visual; Unresolved: external reference, not staged). The trench drain sits in a concrete surround 8 in each side with #3 at 24 in and four #3 continuous, trench drain 'per specifications' (Detail 5; plans_6 p.6, C7.4 — Verified-Visual). The D.O. probe mounts to handrail with U-bolts and Unistrut per manufacturer, with power, wiring and conduit by the electrical contractor; equipment pads are 8 in wider and longer than the bolt pattern or per architectural drawings (Details 1, 3; plans_6 p.6, C7.4 — Verified-Visual; cross-lane, see Issues).
