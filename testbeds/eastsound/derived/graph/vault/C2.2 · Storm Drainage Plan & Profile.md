## Summary

Plan and profile of storm drain alignment SD-1, which runs from existing SDCB 1076 (STA 0+04.63) east to new SDCB #9 (STA 1+09.93) and a 4-in roof drain connection at STA 1+14.26 (plans_3Part-5 p.1, C2.2 — Verified-Visual). New Type 1 catch basins on the alignment are SDCB #5 (STA 0+47.05, rim 22.94), SDCB #8 (STA 0+67.46, rim 22.78) and SDCB #9 (rim 22.75), joined by 12-in pipe in runs of 42, 19 and 42 LF at 0.5% (plans_3Part-5 p.1, C2.2 — Verified-Visual). The new 12-in pipe enters existing SDCB 1076 on its east side at IE 20.35, and the roof drain leg is 8 LF and 17 LF at 2.00% (plans_3Part-5 p.1, C2.2 — Verified-Visual). The profile shows two proposed water crossings 12 in clear below the storm drain and existing power and communication crossings of unknown size and depth (plans_3Part-5 p.1, C2.2 — Verified-Visual). Pipe material is not stated on the sheet (plans_3Part-5 p.1, C2.2 — Verified-Visual).
