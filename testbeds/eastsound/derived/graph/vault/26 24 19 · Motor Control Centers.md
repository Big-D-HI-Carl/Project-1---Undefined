## Summary

Free-standing factory-assembled 600 V MCC furnished by the contractor with all related equipment; MCC sizes on the drawings are based on Allen-Bradley and "Motor control centers and VFDs shall be Allen-Bradley, no exceptions"; the contractor verifies fit before ordering (¶1.01–1.02, ¶1.05, ¶2.01, pp.305–306) [Verified]. Submittals beyond 26 05 00: wiring diagrams for every unit, component data sheets with part numbers, scaled arrangement drawings, wire marker text, bill of materials, 11x17 maximum shop drawings (¶1.04, pp.305–306) [Verified]. Construction includes NEMA 1 gasketed enclosures (NEMA 12 in damp or dusty areas), draw-out plug-in starter and feeder units, at least 5 spare terminals per unit, tin-plated copper bus with 600 A minimum horizontal rating, and a power monitor (¶2.02, pp.307–310) [Verified; pages scanned by key terms]. Execution: MCC completed and wired in the contractor shop and tested there with the control system, including every I/O point, control device and motor controller at rated voltage (¶3.01 B, ¶3.02, p.311) [Verified]. Consistent with E9.1 (Allen-Bradley Centerline 2100 IntelliCENTER); Add. 4 Clarification 4 sets the MCC at eight sections, 160 in (pp.306, 311; plans_12 p.3; Add. 4 p.1) [Verified].
