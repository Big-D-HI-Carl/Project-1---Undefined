## Summary

A performance specification for the complete, tested Influent Pump Station — pumps, controls, discharge piping, vaults, flow meter and connection to existing piping — with the contractor responsible for field measurements and supplier coordination (¶1.01 A–E p.255). Pump schedule (¶2.02 B p.258): two small pumps, Flygt N3085 MT, 150 gpm @ 20.5 ft design (120 gpm @ 22.0 ft; 220 gpm @ 17.5 ft; shutoff 26 ft), 2.7 HP, 1800 rpm, 43% minimum efficiency, 135 mm Adaptive N-impeller, 3 1/8" discharge; two large pumps, Flygt N3102 MT, 385 gpm @ 20.5 ft (320 gpm @ 22.5 ft; 500 gpm @ 15.0 ft; shutoff 38 ft), 5.0 HP, 1800 rpm, 66%, 152 mm impeller, 3 15/16" discharge; all 460 V 3-phase, rated Class 1 Division 1 Groups C and D, one manufacturer for all (¶2.01 C; ¶2.02 C.1, C.9 pp.257–260). Each pump has a cast-iron discharge connection with a machined metal-to-metal seal, two 316 SS guide bars about 15 ft long (contractor to verify), 316 SS brackets and a 20 ft stainless lifting chain with spectra cord for a Grip-Eye device, three 316 SS cable holders, a Flygt MiniCAS-120 motor saver relay supplied to the Control Systems Integrator, and pressure gages (Marsh P0142, 0–25 psi) with Branom diaphragm isolators and Apollo shutoff valves (¶2.02 C.2, C.10, F; ¶2.03; ¶2.04 pp.259–261). Submittals include certified curves, shop drawings of the station and lifting system, control panel and interconnection wiring, warranty and factory test report (¶1.03 A p.256); testing includes certified non-witnessed factory performance tests to Hydraulic Institute standards, a factory operational test of the completed station submitted before installation, field tests with the manufacturer's representative, and at least one 8-hour start-up day with flow tests (¶1.03 C, ¶1.04 B–C p.256; ¶3.03 p.262). The warranty is 5 years or 10,000 hours (¶1.05 A p.256), and float switches hang at the plan levels with a submersible level transducer also used for pump control (¶3.02 A p.261).
