## Summary

Section A cuts the UV chamber (top of wall 23.80, FG 23.3 sloping 2% away, bottom elev 17.74, TOC 18.83 and 19.08) and the aerobic digester (top of wall 26.80, bottom EL 13.3, with a 1-ft thick lip projecting 1/2 ft all around for anti-buoyancy). On the UV side the 8" influent pipe (IE 19.61) enters past a valve hand wheel, the unit runs from HWL 21.49 to HWL 21.09 over a weir level control, with 1" drains, a 2"×1" reducer and 1" PVC ball valve, a floor drain (rim 18.74, IE 16.00), a wall control panel, and the 10" effluent pipe leaving at IE 18.75 (IE 19.44 also shown); guardrail tops are 42" above finish grade (field adjust) and a prefab aluminum ladder connects to the access platform. Between the chambers the labels TOC 17.25, bottom elev 16.25 and a 4" drain pipe at IE 17.25 fall in the effluent sample chamber (location Inferred from C5.1). On the digester side, 3" SS air pipes (centerlines 27.3 and 25.8) on 4 wall-mount pipe supports per 7/C7.2 feed 2" SS drop pipes to coarse bubble diffusers (detail 1 "coarse bubble mixer": 2" SS drop pipe and tee, 2"×1" reducer at the tee, 1"×3/4" SS reducer, diffuser centerline 2.0' above floor), with a normal operating range marked between EL 25.3 and EL 21.3. The 12" CSBC base is "gravel backfill for foundations, Class 'A' per WSDOT Standard Specifications 9-03.12(1)A" — Unresolved: external reference, not staged.
