## Summary

Covers furnishing and installing the valves in the plant piping systems, complete with operators, with submittals per 01 33 00 and NSF 61 materials for potable water (¶1.01–2.01 p.476). Operators open counterclockwise; buried valves take AWWA C504 2" square nuts 18–24" below grade in two-piece cast-iron screw-type valve boxes (Mueller H-10364 or Clow F-2452 or equal) unless in a vault; gear operators keep handwheel pull at 40 lb or less, and butterfly operators show disc position (¶2.02–2.03 pp.476–477). Products: Kennedy resilient-seated NRS gate valves 2"–12" (AWWA C509/C515, C550 epoxy) and all-stainless NPT gate valves under 4" exposed; swing check valves with external lever, spring and limit switch (MH 259-02ALS, AWWA C508) and Tideflex TF-2M duckbill checks for vertical service; Pratt Ballcentric plug valves; Watts S-FBV-1 stainless ball valves under 4" for general exposed non-potable use and Asahi Type 21 CPVC true-union ball valves 2" and under for interior potable water; M&H Style 47 flap gates, demonstrated in dry and wet tests; butterfly valves — Asahi Type 57 PVC for interior PVC systems, Bray Series 31 lug or Dezurik for exposed ductile iron or stainless air piping, AWWA C504 Class 150B for buried water mains 10" and larger; A.R.I. D-025 combination air release valves; 2"-and-under globe valves (Issue 28); and Kennedy gate valves for buried plant water hydrant isolation (¶2.04–2.014 pp.477–479). Every shut-off or control valve gets a 1-1/2" stainless tag with its ID "as listed on the valve schedule", a submitted valve directory, and a label marker in a concrete pad per the plans and valve schedule (¶2.015–2.016 p.479); no valve schedule is in the P&M stack (cross-lane item). Installation sets stem orientation by pipe centerline height (4'-6") and requires watertight joints, and valves are tested with the adjacent pipeline, with a manufacturer affidavit on request (¶3.01–3.02 pp.479–480).
