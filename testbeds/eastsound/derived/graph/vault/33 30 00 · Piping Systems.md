## Summary

General requirements for all piping systems shown on the plans, with trenching per 31 23 33 and submittals 'per Section 01 30 00' (not in the manual): field-fabrication data, shop laying drawings, pipe support drawings, material certifications and field welding procedures (¶1.01–1.02; main spec pp.420–421 — Verified; see Issues). Penetrations: poured-in-place DI wall pipe in slabs and walls, Link-Seal with 316 SS hardware (GPK sand collars for PVC) in new basins, core drilling in existing walls (grout at 6 in and under, Link-Seal at 8 in and over), and Link-Seal or Kor-N-Seal boots at precast manholes and vaults (¶2.01 C; pp.421–422 — Verified). All new piping gets #12 AWG steel-core tracer wire (Copperhead 1230-HS or equal) tied at 10-ft intervals, color-coded green for sewer, purple for non-potable and blue for potable, with Copperhead LD14/CD14/HS14 boxes whose locations are submitted before construction (¶3.03; pp.426–427 — Verified; conflicts with C7.1 and C7.4, see Issues). Testing is witnessed with 48-hour notice: force mains at 1.5 times operating pressure or 150 psi for 15 minutes (mixed liquor and West Plant Drain Pump Station force mains 150 psi, 2W and 3W piping 200 psi), HDPE per ASTM F2164, and valves with their pipelines (¶3.04.1–6, 8, 10; pp.427–429 — Verified). Gravity storm and sewer tests and water-line pressure tests and disinfection defer to WSDOT 4-04.3(1), 7-17.3(2) and 7-09.3(23), with DOH-lab chlorine (50 mg/L initial, 25 mg/L at 24 hours) and bacteriological reports (¶3.04.7, 9; p.428 — Verified; WSDOT test methods Unresolved: external reference, not staged).
