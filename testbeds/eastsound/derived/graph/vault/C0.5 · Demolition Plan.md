## Summary

Demolition plan with 25 keyed notes covering removal of existing pipe (40 LF 8-in and 60 LF 10-in effluent/outfall, 40 LF 4-in roof drain, 87 LF storm drain with a catch basin, 20 LF 6-in drain, 60 LF 1-1/2-in sump force main, 7 ft and 8 LF of storm drain, and the 8-in influent pipe), 149 LF and 119 LF of chain link fence, 61 LF of retaining wall, and the generator fuel tank with its concrete spill basin (Keyed Notes 1–2, 8–9, 11–12, 16–21, 24; plans_2Part-4 p.1, C0.5 — Verified-Visual). Removals follow their replacements: the effluent pipes come out after the interim 8-in HDPE pipe (C0.7) is installed and tested, the sump pump station and its piping after the new plant drain pump station, and the septic tank (SSMH 1285) after the new septic tank and influent pump station, with temporary pumps, piping and basins as needed (Keyed Notes 1, 3–5, 8, 11–12; plans_2Part-4 p.1, C0.5 — Verified-Visual). The Contractor keeps the 2-in water service and sump station power live until replacements are tested, protects the storm drain pump station manhole (DMH 1287), potholes storm, power and telecom before new work, relocates existing holding tanks on site per Owner, and sawcuts asphalt pavement (Keyed Notes 5–6, 10, 13–15, 25; plans_2Part-4 p.1, C0.5 — Verified-Visual). Construction phasing and the demolition schedule cover the existing basin: remove the 8-in slab over the original pre-aeration basin by saw cut near the building's east face, remove fill while supporting internal piping, bypass all flow during a time-critical step, complete a checklist (walls, coatings, penetrations, three weir gates) before accepting wastewater, and remove internal piping after the new flow splitter is operational (Construction Phasing 1–5, Demolition Schedule 1–3; plans_2Part-4 p.1, C0.5 — Verified-Visual). A demolition plan with schedule must be approved by the Engineer before any demolition; Add. 4 states saw-cut information is on C0.5 but new surfacing information is on C2.6, which is unavailable — Add. 4 governs (General Note, plans_2Part-4 p.1, C0.5 — Verified-Visual; Add. 4 p.1, Clarification 1 — Verified).
