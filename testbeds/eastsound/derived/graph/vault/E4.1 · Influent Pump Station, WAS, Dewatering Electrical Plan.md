## Summary

Area plan: influent pumps IP-1 to IP-4 (motors marked 3, 3, 5, 5) with floats and transducer and power/instrument handholes; WAS pumps WP-1, WP-2 and sludge pump SD-1 (marked 3) with local disconnects; dewatering skid control panel DW-1; three WAS solenoid valves; influent, plant 2, plant 3, plant drain and dewatering flow meters; methane and H2S sensors; exhaust fans EF-3 to EF-5; and one location holding the Influent Pump Station control panel, panel LP2 and the flow-meter transmitters (plan; plans_9 p.6) [Verified-Visual; IP-3/IP-4, EF-3 to EF-5, sensor and panel labels also in text]. Key notes 1–2: individual oversized conduits for every pump, float and transducer cable, duct-sealed at completion; flow meters are Endress+Hauser Promag W400 (Class I Div 2, EtherNet/IP, 316L electrodes, local display on the WAS meter, remote transmitters with submersible kits on vault meters) with two 316L grounding rings per tube — the drawing already shows the W400 that Add. 4 p.2 (26 80 00 ¶2.04 D) substitutes for the L400 (plans_9 p.6) [Verified]. Key notes 3–5: the building housing the dewatering press is unclassified only with Type C ventilation at 6 air changes per hour (NFPA 820-2020 Table 6.2.2 Row 12a, Chapter 9), and the contractor provides ventilation fans, gas sensors, a local alarm light and horn; the dewatering skid arrives pre-wired, factory tested and UL/ETL labeled per Washington L&I, with a 480 V 3-phase feeder and Cat 6 monitoring cable; WAS solenoid wiring routes to the Influent Pump Station panel (plans_9 p.6) [Verified]. Key notes 6–9: a dedicated 120 V GFCI receptacle and a 4–20 mA flow-pace signal from the PLC at four points (hypochlorite feed pump, polymer feed pump, influent sampler, effluent sampler; samplers Owner-furnished, contractor-installed); 120 VAC from LP2 and PLC control wiring to each of the three ventilation fans "in Treatment Building"; raceway to handholes with at least two 1-in spares from each power and control handhole stubbed and capped; influent pump motor disconnects on the building wall within sight of the wet well (plans_9 p.6) [Verified; numbering Verified-Visual]. General note 4: outdoor control panel and disconnect enclosures are lockable Type 316 stainless with drip shields; key note 3 names the building "Dewatering Building" while key note 7 and the LP2 schedule say "Treatment Building" for the same outline (see Issues); P-TPS re-feeds the existing 208Y/120V power panel (plans_9 p.6) [Verified; same building Inferred from the plan].
