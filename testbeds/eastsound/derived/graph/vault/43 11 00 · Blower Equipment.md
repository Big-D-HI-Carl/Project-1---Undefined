## Summary

Four identical Kaeser Com-paK DB 166 C positive-displacement blower packages (20 HP, 460 V 3-phase, VFD variable speed) serve the Biological Treatment System from the Blower Building, and one Kaeser Com-paK BB 52 C (5 HP, VFD) serves the aerobic digester from the existing metal building just north of the proposed blower building, "just west of" and "adjacent to" Train #2; Kaeser, Aerzen or approved equal (¶1.01 A–B p.462; ¶2.01 A pp.462–463; ¶O p.469). Design points: 315 scfm at 8.9 psig differential per biological blower (325 icfm, 10.1 psig max, relief at 12.0 psig, 17.4 bhp, 3,999 rpm, 207°F discharge) and 120 scfm at 4.0 psig for the digester blower (124 icfm, 6.6 psig max, relief at 8.0 psig, 3.8 bhp), each in a 72 dB(A) sound enclosure (¶2.01 A; ¶P p.470). Packages are oil-free tri-lobe and belt-driven with automatic belt tensioning, integral inlet filter-silencer and discharge silencer, spring relief valve, flexible connector, pressure, temperature and filter differential gauges, TEFC IP-55 motors with PTC thermistors at 18–60 Hz, 100,000-hour L10 bearings and vibration to VDI 3836 (¶2.01 B–K pp.463–467). Each blower is slip tested and run 30 minutes at maximum speed and differential, each package runs 15 minutes before shipment with a conformance certificate, the blower warranty is 60 months from shipment (other components 1 year from substantial completion), and submittals per 01 33 00 are catalog data, fabrication and installation drawings, and O&M information (¶2.01 L–N pp.467–468; ¶1.02 A p.462). Controls sit in a NEMA 12 cabinet with the VFDs in the MCC, and the contractor wires the over-temperature, over-pressure and motor temperature sensors to the PLC control panel and supplies tees, supports and fittings (¶2.01 Q, ¶3.01 A p.471).
