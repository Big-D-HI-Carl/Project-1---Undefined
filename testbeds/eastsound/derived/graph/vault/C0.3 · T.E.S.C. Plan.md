## Summary

A stabilized construction entrance (per 4/C0.4) runs along the north property line inside the 20-ft easement and is marked as the sole ingress and egress location, and two emergency access areas on the west side allow no parking, blocking, stockpiling or staging but may be used for vehicle turnaround (legend; keyed notes 1–2) (Missing_Pages_Page_001.png, C0.3 — Verified-Visual). Inlet protection (per 2/C0.4) is shown at SDCB #1 through #10 and at existing SDCB 1076 (Missing_Pages_Page_001.png, C0.3 — Verified-Visual). The legend also lists silt fence (3/C0.4) and check dams (5/C0.4), but no silt fence or check dam run could be identified on the plan at stored resolution, and no compost berm is shown (Unresolved — confirm on a native copy). Storm and sewer structure tables repeat the existing structures (DCB 1072, DCB 1076, DMH 1287; SSMH 1285, 1286, 1288, 2258) with rims and inverts, and the sheet refers to C0.1 for existing conditions (Missing_Pages_Page_001.png, C0.3 — Verified-Visual).
