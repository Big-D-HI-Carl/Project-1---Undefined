## Summary

The drain pump station lid is a 16"-thick cast-in-place concrete lid over an 8' min., 96" I.D. precast wet well (lid diameter to match the wet well O.D.), designed for H-20 truck loading, with two hatches per civil at 2'-6" × 4'-0" rough openings and a center beam between them [plans_8 p.4, S2.4 Detail 1 and Notes 1–6 — Verified-Visual]. Lid bars are placed in a set order (E/W bottom, N/S bottom, N/S top, E/W top) with 1-1/2" clear, the center beam has (2) #8 top and bottom continuous with #4 closed ties @ 3" o.c. max., and the lid keys into the precast wall with joints to be "seal AA at joints per civil" [plans_8 p.4, Details 1–3 — Verified-Visual]. Add. 4 requires the exterior of all precast concrete wet well and vault joints to be sealed with CANUSA-CPS WrapidSeal (manhole encapsulation system) or approved equal, installed per the manufacturer [Add. 4 p.1, Clarification 2 — Verified; Add. 4 governs]. The generator sits on a 6" slab-on-grade (#4 @ 18" o.c. each way, mid-depth) with a thickened edge about 1'-6" min. deep and (2) #4 continuous; the generator itself is "per civil, attachment by others" and is not identified as permanent or temporary [plans_8 p.4, Detail 4 — Verified-Visual; identity — Unresolved, Issues, Missing information #5]. The flow splitter sits on a new concrete pad on the existing equipment slab-on-grade, reinforced #4 @ 12" o.c. each way top and bottom with #4 hooked dowels @ 24" o.c. drilled and epoxied around the perimeter and a roughened joint; flow splitter per civil, pad per mech and civil, attachment by others [plans_8 p.4, Detail 5 — Verified-Visual].
