## Summary

The new Influent Pump Station is a 96" precast wet well "per WSDOT Std Plan B-15.60-00 for precast manholes" (Unresolved: external reference, not staged) on a 12" thick precast base, interior bottom elev 9.0 and top elev 23.30, with the top slab per 1/S2.4 and 2/S2.4, a 36"×36" Halliday H1R3636 hatch and a 30"×48" Halliday H1R3048 hatch over the pumps (both H-20), and "WrapidSeal or equal" at all joints; it takes 8" influent at IE 19.50, 4" filtrate and digester supernatant at IE 13.40 and a 4" sample sump line at IE 18.00. Small and large pumps, each a duplex arrangement per specs (keyed notes 28, 52, 53), hang on stainless guide rails with stainless haul chains, manufacturer's disconnect elbows and flapper valves that close and seal when submerged; discharges pass through two precast valve vaults (4'-2" × 4'-2" × 5'-6" minimum inside, Utility Vault Co. 506-LA or equal, floor elev 18.0±) with 4" and 6" check and gate valves and pressure gages, into 4" and 6" DI restrained piping with buried gate valves. Floats F1–F4 on timer control start the redundant large lag (19.00), large lead (18.50), small lag (18.00) and small lead (17.50) pumps; the transducer, set 6" above the floor, runs T1 high level alarm 17.50 (with float), T2 large lag 17.00, T3 large lead 16.00, T4 small lag 14.00, T5 small lead 12.00, T6 pumps off 11.00 and T7 low level alarm and redundant off 10.50. Also shown: the influent sample sump structure (see 4/C7.5), a 2" SCH 80 conduit for the influent sampler intake tubing, a 3" stainless vent pipe with 24-mesh stainless screen (IE 21.00), clean outs, 2" PVC and 4" vault drains (IE 16.15), link-seal boots at every pipe-to-concrete penetration, 16 stainless anchor bolts, steps or ladder per WSDOT Std Plan B-30.90-01 (keyed note 21) and a 12" base of Class A gravel per WSDOT 9-03.12(1)A — both Unresolved: external reference, not staged. The vertical pipe brace points to detail 7/C7.2 (keyed note 27) and to "sheet M4.1" (elevation callout), which is not in the sheet index (Issue 11); Add. 4 p.1 Clarification 2 requires CANUSA-CPS WrapidSeal (Manhole Encapsulation System) or approved equal on the exterior of all precast concrete wet well and vault joints, installed per manufacturer, over the C4.1 call-out — Add. 4 governs.
