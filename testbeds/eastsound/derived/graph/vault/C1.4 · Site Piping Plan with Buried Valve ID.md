## Summary

Site piping plan with a buried valve identification legend of 13 valves (plans_3Part-2 p.1, C1.4 — Verified). Twelve are gate valves: 6-in influent flow meter isolation (1), 8-in Train 2 and Train 3 flow meter isolation (2, 3), 4-in drain flow meter isolation (4, 5, 13), 4-in treated effluent outlet box drain (6, 7), 4-in UV disinfection drain (8), 4-in sample sump drain (9) and 4-in pump station clean-out drain (10, 11) (plans_3Part-2 p.1, C1.4 — Verified). Valve 12 is an 8-in plug valve for effluent flow meter isolation (plans_3Part-2 p.1, C1.4 — Verified). About 300 characters of the text layer are labels from the UV equipment block (LAMP, STATUS, TOP, BOTTOM, INLET, OUTLET), not piping content (plans_3Part-2 p.1 — Verified; see Issues). C1.4 was not reissued with the Add. 4 C1.3 changes; Add. 4's general clause applies its changes to all relevant portions whether cited or not (Add. 4 p.1 General, p.3 — Verified).
