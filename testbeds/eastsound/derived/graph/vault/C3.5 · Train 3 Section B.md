## Summary

Section through Train 3 with the same top of tank (26.80), water levels (static 25.30, ADF 25.34, PHF 25.38), FG 23.30 and floor elev 9.80 as C3.4, dimensioned 17.45', 17.00' and 13.96'. Inlet side: 8" plant inlet IE 20.08, a 6" air inlet flanged connection on a pipe support, a Liqui-Lift nitrate recycle weir box, a RAS airlift weir box and the air header; at the floor, an 8" riser at IE 10.57, 4" RAS at IE 10.36 and a 10" clarifier inlet gate valve. Outlet side: 8" dia effluent flanged connection and 8" clarifier effluent IE 20.00. The clarifier side water depth is dimensioned 12.8' to 12.9', with V-notch weir, FRP launder cover, scum baffle, feed well, skimming arm, scraper arm drive, level adjustment rod, sludge scraper arms, 1'-8" O.D. sludge well and a plenum diffuser; the 12" base note repeats WSDOT 9-03.12(1)A — Unresolved: external reference, not staged.
