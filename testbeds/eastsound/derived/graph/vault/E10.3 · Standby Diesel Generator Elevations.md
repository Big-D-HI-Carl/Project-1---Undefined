## Summary

Side, end and plan views of the standby generator on a concrete mounting pad "per civil specifications" (plans_12 p.8) [Verified]. Unit: 480Y/277V 125 kW standby diesel generator in a painted aluminum sound-attenuated enclosure rated 72 dBA at 7 m, on a sub-base dual-wall diesel tank with leak detection (Regional series); fuel vents and accessories per the local jurisdiction (plans_12 p.8) [Verified]. General note 1: basis of design Cummins C125D6D, 60 Hz, standby duty, EPA Tier 3, 125 kW / 156 kVA standby rating, 480Y/277V 3-phase — matching E6.1 (plans_12 p.8; plans_10 p.2) [Verified]. General note 2: tank with primary and secondary emergency vents, normal vent, spill box with overfill prevention valve, high fuel alarm panel, low fuel switch, leak detection and fuel gauge; 351 gal usable at 10.1 gal/hr gives 35 h at full load and up to 48 h at normal loads (351 / 10.1 = 34.8 h, consistent) (plans_12 p.8) [Verified; arithmetic Inferred]. General note 3 sends the rest to the specifications (26 32 13, batch 6); the pad joins the generator pad item already in Issues (plans_12 p.8) [Verified].
