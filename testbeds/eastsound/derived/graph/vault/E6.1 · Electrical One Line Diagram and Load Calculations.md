## Summary

Service and distribution: OPALCO 225 kVA 480Y/277V pad-mount transformer → P-SEC → CT enclosure and utility meter → P-ATS → ATS (400 A service entrance, 4-wire 3-pole, NEMA 12, delayed open transition) with the 125 kW / 156 kVA standby diesel generator (sound-attenuated outdoor enclosure, sub-base fuel tank, 250/3, P-GEN) → P-MDP → MDP (480Y/277V, main lugs only, SPD) → 400/3 to the MCC and 125/3 to transformer TS (75 kVA 480–208Y/120V), which re-feeds the existing Office Building 400 A I-Line panel and, through its 200/3, Panel TPS (plans_10 p.2) [Verified-Visual; tags also in text]. MCC (480 V 3-ph 4-W, Blower Building): 400/3 main, power monitor with (3) 400:5 CTs, SPD; 30/3 units for TCP-1 and TCP-2 (greyed, "future") and TCP-3; 60/3 for dewatering skid DW-1 and for the IPS panel; 60/2 to T2 (20 kVA 480–120/240V) and its load center; ten VFDs with line reactors — 2W-P1 and 2W-P2 (20/3, 5 HP), WP-1, WP-2 (swing) and SD-1 (15/3, 3 HP), all five with dV/dt reactors, BL-1 to BL-4 (35/3, 20 HP) and DB (20/3, 5 HP) (plans_10 p.2) [Verified-Visual]. IPS Influent Pump Station control panel (480 V 3-ph 3-W, NEMA 4X SS): 60/3 main, contactor-and-overload starters for IP-1 and IP-2 (15/3, 3 HP) and IP-3 and IP-4 (20/3, 5 HP), and 30/2 to T3 (10 kVA 480–120/240V) and its load center; a local disconnect is drawn at each motor (plans_10 p.2) [Verified-Visual]. Key notes: 1 the manufacturer sets VFD overcurrent protection ratings; 2 3% line-side reactors on all VFDs; 3 dV/dt load-side reactors on VFD motor runs over 50 ft; 4 outdoor control panel and disconnect enclosures Type 316 SS; 5 aeration blower disconnects are on the MCC within sight of the motors (plans_10 p.2) [Verified; numbering Verified-Visual]. Load calculations: 21 loads, 348.9 A connected, 355.7 A demand and 188.0 A on the generator (156 kVA at 480 V); generator factor 1 for TCP-1 to TCP-3, 2W-P1, IP-1, BL-1 to BL-3, DB and WP-1, 0 for the standby/swing units and DW-1, and 0.25 for LP-LAB (existing lab and treatment, 45 kVA), LP-1 (20 kVA) and LP-2 (10 kVA); the 355.7 A demand exceeds the E6.2 MDP total — see Issues (plans_10 p.2) [Verified-Visual].
