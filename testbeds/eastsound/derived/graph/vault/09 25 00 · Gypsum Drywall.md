## Summary

Covers all gypsum drywall shown, taped and spackled, to ASTM C840 from a single source, with texture finish samples as the only submittal [¶1.01 A, ¶1.02 A–B, ¶1.03 A, p.225 — Verified]. Board is 5/8" USG Aqua-Tough (green board) unless noted, Type X at fire-rated assemblies and Fiberock at high-moisture locations, with ASTM C1002 screws, Dur-A-Bead corners, LC and L beads, Perf-A-Tape and USG Imperial QT spray texture [¶2.01 A–E, p.226 — Verified]. Board fastens to steel framing with self-drilling screws at 12" o.c. max., gets a 3-coat joint finish sanded between coats, and hollow metal door frames are spot-grouted at the jamb anchors [¶3.02 H, ¶3.02 L.1, ¶3.03 D, pp.227–228 — Verified]. Texture primer is referred to "Section 09900 Painting"; Add. 4 adds 09 90 00 Service Condition J for the Blower Building's gypsum board, which matches the painted GWB walls in the A1.1 finish schedule [¶3.04 A, p.228 — Verified; Add. 4 p.2 — Verified; plans_7 p.5 — Verified-Visual].
