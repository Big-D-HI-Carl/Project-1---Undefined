## Summary

Elevation of the four new blowers "per specs" (keyed note 5) on the Blower Building floor at FF 23.30 (TOP 27.80 marked at a blower enclosure), with a 3" air piping header (IE 24.26), 3" butterfly valves at the blowers (keyed note 1) and 6" butterfly valves (keyed note 2) on the 6" outlets (IE 25.92). Three 6" air lines leave the header: to Train #1 and to Train #2, each to "terminate with blind flange inside existing metal building" (keyed notes 3 and 4), and to Train #3, dropping outside the building to IE 15.90 ("6-in air piping to Train #3").
