## Summary

The Contractor designs, furnishes and builds the gravity block retaining wall; construction drawings and design calculations must be stamped by a Washington PE, with a manufacturer's certification before work starts (¶1.01, ¶1.04; main spec pp.408–409 — Verified). Units are Keystone Compaq II or approved equal to ASTM C1372: gray straight split face, running bond, 3,000 psi minimum, 8% maximum absorption, 8 x 18 x 12-in minimum, with two fiberglass pins per unit (¶2.02–2.03; pp.410–411 — Verified). The leveling pad (6 in minimum) and unit fill are crushed surfacing top course per WSDOT 9-03.9(3), and the pad is compacted to 95% standard or 92% modified Proctor (¶2.04–2.05, ¶3.02; p.411 — Verified; material Unresolved: external reference, not staged). The Owner's or Contractor's QA/QC representative inspects the excavation and tests foundation soils before the leveling pad; units are stacked no more than two courses ahead of fill, a footing drain and wall backfill are placed, and caps are glued (¶3.01, ¶3.03–3.04; pp.411–412 — Verified). As-built tolerances: ±1.5 in per 10 ft vertical and horizontal, batter within 2°, corners ±1 ft, gaps 1/2 in maximum (¶3.05; p.412 — Verified).
