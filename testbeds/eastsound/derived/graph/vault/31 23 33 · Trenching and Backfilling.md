## Summary

Covers trench excavation, bedding, backfill and compaction for utilities, with the WSDOT/APWA Standard Specifications as the reference standard and 29 CFR 1926 for trench safety, which is solely the Contractor's responsibility (¶1.01, ¶1.03–1.04, ¶3.02; pp.393–396 — Verified; WSDOT/APWA Unresolved: external reference, not staged). Submittals: disposal sites, gradation tests and proctors for imported material, geotextile cut sheets, and a traffic control plan per WSDOT 1-10 (¶1.05 C, ¶1.06; p.394 — Verified). Trench backfill is bank-run gravel (WSDOT 9-03.19) and bedding is pipe-zone gravel (9-03.12(3)), with no native backfill or bedding; bedding is compacted to 90% MDD in 6-in lifts, backfill to 95% under paving or within 5 ft of roadway and 90% in landscaped areas (¶2.01–2.02, ¶3.03–3.04; pp.394–396 — Verified; conflicts with C7.4, see Issues). APWA color-coded 2-in detectable warning tape is required over every new utility (red electric, blue water, green sewer, orange telephone/CATV, light blue air) and replaced where existing utilities are crossed (¶2.03; pp.394–395 — Verified). Open trench is limited to 200 ft ahead of pipe laying and trenches are closed or plated daily; owner notice is needed for traffic or parking disruptions (48 hours for shutdowns) (¶1.05 B, D, ¶3.02 F; pp.393–396 — Verified).
