## Summary

Schematic power wiring: 480 V 3-phase into a power distribution block with a surge suppressor, a phase-fail relay and control transformer CPT1 (120 VAC control power), then a breaker, motor starter and overload for each of pumps 1–4 (plans_11 p.6) [Verified-Visual; labels also in text]. Pump data as printed: pump no.1 and no.2 "XP" 3 HP, 4.8 FLA; pump no.3 and no.4 "XP" 5 HP, 7.6 FLA — matching the E6.1 horsepower for IP-1 to IP-4 (plans_11 p.6; plans_10 p.2) [Verified-Visual]. Pump control wiring, typical for each of 4 pumps: hand-off-auto; in auto the starter runs on a PLC contact or, in parallel, the high-float contact; the starter coil is interlocked by the overtemperature relay, the phase-fail relay and the overload; lights for pump running, overtemperature, seal fail and overload (plans_11 p.6) [Verified-Visual]. Seal-fail/overtemperature relay per pump, wired to the pump housing sensors (T1, T2) with a reset, driving relays CROT and CRSF (plans_11 p.6) [Verified-Visual; relay labels also in text]. General note 1: the system integrator provides the detailed panel design; only major components are specified (plans_11 p.6) [Verified].
