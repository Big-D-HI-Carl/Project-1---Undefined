## Summary

Material requirements for wastewater lines, with installation and testing per 33 30 00 (¶1.01, ¶3.02; main spec pp.430, 435 — Verified). Footing drains are 4-in perforated corrugated polyethylene per WSDOT 9-05.2(7); gravity sewer and drains are PVC ASTM D3034 SDR 35 or DI per WSDOT 9-05.13 (Class 50/51, cement-lined, poly-wrapped), with Sch. 40 PVC drain pipe at 4 in and under 'or as noted on the plans' (¶2.01 A, C; p.430 — Verified; WSDOT materials Unresolved: external reference, not staged). Pressure sewer is DI, PVC C900/C905 DR 18 or HDPE; buried 2W/3W water is PE4710 IPS HDPE DR11; buried WAS discharge piping is DI; buried aeration piping 4 in and larger is poly-wrapped DI, with stainless steel below 4 in (¶2.01 B, D, E, F; pp.430–431 — Verified; conflicts with C1.3, see Issues). Each 1-in yard hydrant gets two 3/4-in x 50-ft fire hoses and each 2-in yard hydrant two 1.5-in x 50-ft hoses; exposed DI is epoxy-coated per 'Section 09900' and above-grade wastewater pipe is insulated (Trymer 2000 XP) with aluminum jacketing (¶2.01 J–M, V; pp.432–434 — Verified). Water and sewer lines near each other follow Ecology's Criteria for Sewage Works Design C1-9, and floor drain piping points to 'Plumbing Spec 22 10 00', which is not in the manual (¶3.01 F, ¶2.01 C.3; pp.430, 435 — Verified; see Issues).
