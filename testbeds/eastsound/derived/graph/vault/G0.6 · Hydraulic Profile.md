## Summary

The liquid profile runs from the proposed influent sampling sump (FG 23.65) and the proposed influent & plant drain pump station (8" influent from the STEP collection system, IE 19.50; FG 23.30) through the plant influent flow meter to the proposed 3-way splitter box (HWL 28.0 at 0.77 MGD; bottom elev 26.00). Existing Trains #1 and #2 run at HWL 23.13 (0.257 MGD), NWL 23.09 (0.063 MGD), static 23.05; proposed Train #3 runs at HWL 25.38, NWL 25.34, static 25.30, top of concrete 26.80, with proposed Train #2 and Train #3 flow meters on the inlets. The proposed effluent flow meter feeds proposed UV disinfection channels #1 and #2 (HWL 21.49 at 0.50 MGD per channel; NWL 19.69 at 0.22 MGD per channel), then existing SSMH *1288 (rim 22.89) and 10" and 8" to the 12" outfall; the 100-year flood elevation is 13', and a proposed effluent sample sump is labeled. The solids profile shows proposed WAS pump equipment (pump rate 20–30 gpm), the proposed aerobic digester with a supernatant telescoping valve (TOC 26.80; supernatant to the plant drain P.S.), proposed sludge pump equipment (20–30 gpm), proposed rotary fan press dewatering equipment and the existing screw press, with filtrate to the plant drain P.S. and solids hauled off site.
