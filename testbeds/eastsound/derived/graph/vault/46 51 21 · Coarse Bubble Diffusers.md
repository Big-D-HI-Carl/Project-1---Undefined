## Summary

Furnish and install coarse bubble diffusers as indicated on the plans; the only location listed is the aerobic digester (¶1.01 A–B p.491). Diffusers are 24" long, 316 SS, clog-free, open-bottom, with a rubber check valve in the air inlet against backflow of liquid or sludge, by Aquarius Technologies, SSI Aeration or approved equal, and removable from above the water line without entering the basin (¶2.01 A–B p.491). Submittals per 01 33 00 are catalog data, fabrication and installation drawings coordinated with piping connections, and O&M information (¶1.02 A p.491); the contractor supplies tees, supports and fittings, installation follows the manufacturer's instructions, and no testing is specified (¶3.01 p.491). No quantity is given; G0.7 lists 6 diffusers at 20 cfm (Verified, plans_1 p.7).
