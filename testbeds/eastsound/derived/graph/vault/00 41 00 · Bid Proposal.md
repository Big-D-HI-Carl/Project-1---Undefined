## Summary

The form carries Base Bid Item 1 WWTP Upgrade (LS), unit-quantity Items 2–7, extra-work Items 8–17, Bid Alternate 1 (Item 18) and Bid Alternate 2 (Item 19), then 8.3% sales tax (Eastsound Sewer and Water District, San Juan County) and total bid (pp.23–25; Verified). The Owner pre-enters $100,000.00 for Item 7 Minor Changes (EST) and $100,000.00 for Item 17 Extra SCADA/PLC Programmer Services (FA) (pp.23–24; Verified). Base Bid Item 1 is based on the named manufacturers — Flygt submersible pumps, Smith and Loveless biological treatment, Fournier or Prime Solutions rotary fan press, Trojan UV, and Cummins/Kohler/Caterpillar/Generac generators — with Equipment Alternates A–E as lump-sum deductions; there is no pre-bid approval review and the bidder carries all redesign cost (pp.26–27; Verified). Mobilization is paid per WSDOT 1-09.7 (Unresolved: external reference, not staged); completion time points to "Supplementary Condition 15 – Completion Date", liquidated damages are per the General Conditions per calendar day, and the Owner may accept or reject bid prices within 60 days (p.27; Verified — the 60 days vs 45 days in 00 21 13 is Issues 1.4). Add. 4 p.1 Clarification 5 uses "Bid Item #18" for the temporary back-up generator while this form lists Item 18 as Train 3 stainless steel above water (p.25): Add. 4 governs the temporary generator requirements; the item number stays Unresolved per the existing flag (cited, not re-raised).
