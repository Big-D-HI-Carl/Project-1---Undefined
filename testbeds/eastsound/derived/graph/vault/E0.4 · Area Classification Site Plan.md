## Summary

Classifies plant areas per NFPA 820 (2020) on the process basis of wastewater from residential STEP stations (NFPA 820 Table 4.2.2 Row 9), and requires electrical work in classified areas to meet NFPA 70 and AHJ requirements (general notes 1–3; plans_9 p.1) [Verified]. Class I, Division 2 within the enclosed space: the Influent Pump Station (Table 4.2.2 Row 10a) and the below-grade metering vault with a closed piping system (Rows 10a and 34a) (classified area notes; plans_9 p.1) [Verified]. Unclassified: aerobic treatment, UV disinfection units, the 2W pump station, the effluent flow meter vault and the aerated digester open to atmosphere; the Office Building and Blower Building are not classified (plans_9 p.1) [Verified]. The existing Dewatering Building with the dewatering press is unclassified only with Type C ventilation, continuous at 6 air changes per hour per NFPA 820 Chapter 9, so its classification depends on ventilation held by another lane (plans_9 p.1) [Verified; dependency Inferred].
