## Summary

Three panel schedules (MDP, LP1, LP2); the text layer carries only the titles, so every schedule entry is read from the page image (plans_10 p.3) [Verified-Visual]. Panel MDP: 480Y/277V 3-ph 4-W, 400 A bus, 400 A main lugs only, surface mounted, 42,000 AIC; circuits 1-3-5 Blower Building MCC (400 A 3-pole, 60,000 VA per phase) and 2-4-6 transformer for existing building 480–208Y/120V (125 A 3-pole, 15,000 VA per phase); circuits 7–30 spaces; connected and demand 225.0 kVA, 270.6 A (plans_10 p.3) [Verified-Visual]. Panel LP1 (MCC) repeats the E2.2 schedule without change: 120/240 V 1-ph 3-W, 100 A bus, 100 A main breaker, 10,000 AIC; receptacles, louver/HVAC control, EF-1, EF-2, UV1/UV2 power (30 A) and controllers, Main Control Panel, Blower Building lighting, generator heater and battery charger, Hot Box 1 and 2 GFEP, effluent sampler, hypochlorite feed pump; demand 17.3 kVA, 72.1 A (plans_10 p.3) [Verified-Visual]. Panel LP2 (Influent Pump Station panel): 120/240 V 1-ph 3-W, 100 A bus, 60 A main breaker, 10,000 AIC; pump station control circuits, wall pack lighting, ventilation control panel, EF-3, EF-4, EF-5 (Treatment Building, 1,127 VA each), H2S and methane sensors, polymer feed pump and influent sampler receptacles, six spares; demand 7.8 kVA, 32.3 A; circuit numbers match the E4.1 callouts (plans_10 p.3) [Verified-Visual]. Gaps against other sheets: the MDP total (225.0 kVA, 270.6 A) is below the E6.1 load-calculation demand (355.7 A), and no pole-light circuit appears on LP1 or LP2 although E6.3 feeds P-LT from a "lighting panel" — see Issues (plans_10 pp.2–4) [Verified-Visual].
