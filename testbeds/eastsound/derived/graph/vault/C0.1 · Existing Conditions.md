## Summary

Existing-conditions plan of the WWTP parcel showing the Eastsound Water District office building (FF 23.5), the existing treatment plant (two circular tanks), the screw press room, a concrete retaining wall, chain-link fence with gate, and 10-ft building setback lines (plans_1 p.8, C0.1 — Verified-Visual). Parcel boundaries and easements shown include a 5-ft easement to the Port (AFN 2002 0701021), a 20-ft easement (AFN 107644 plus a second number, partly legible), and a 10-ft easement noted as approximate location and as-constructed (plans_1 p.8, C0.1 — Verified-Visual). The storm structure table lists DCB 1072 and DCB 1076 (Type I) and DMH 1287 (48-in pump-out manhole); the sewer structure table lists SSMH 1285 (septic tank), SSMH 1286 (sewage pump), SSMH 1288 and SSMH 2258, each with rim and invert data (plans_1 p.8, C0.1 — Verified-Visual). Other existing features: 8-in PVC influent sample port (IE 19.4), concrete fuel tank basin, pad-mounted transformer, generator, shed, and hangars on adjoining Port of Orcas parcels (plans_1 p.8, C0.1 — Verified-Visual). Labels 'WSE #205, #206, #208, #211' and 'TB-1' to 'TB-3' appear with no stated meaning on the sheet (plans_1 p.8, C0.1 — Verified-Visual).
