## Summary

LED lighting complete and operational per the plans and UL-listed; catalog numbers show design intent, and the written fixture schedule governs over fixture illustrations (¶1.01–1.03, p.349) [Verified]. Submittals: original product sheets, independent photometric reports where the schedule requires them, fixture types and project name marked, prepared by the local manufacturer representative, plus WSEC lighting control devices (¶1.06, p.350) [Verified]. Warranty: 5 years on LED products including parts and labor (¶1.08, p.350) [Verified]. Lighting controls (switches, contactors, occupancy sensors) where shown, compliant with the current Washington State Energy Code, tested and demonstrated as applicable (¶2.02, ¶3.01 I, p.351) [Verified]. Fixture mounting suited to the construction type and local seismic rules and coordinated with architectural ceiling plans (¶3.01 A–C, p.351) [Verified].
