## Summary

Covers all finish hardware (quantities not guaranteed), with submittals of manufacturer's data, a hardware schedule in sets cross-referenced to the door schedule, a keying schedule and templates; closers carry a 5-year and other hardware a 1-year guarantee [¶1.01 A, ¶1.03 A–E, ¶1.05 A, pp.216–218 — Verified]. Part 4 sets Hardware Group 1 (exterior single INS HM doors) and Group 2 (exterior double doors): Stanley FBB179 hinges, Corbin Russwin ML2000 mortise locksets with Lustra lever trim keyed to the District standard, Ives 458 flush bolts and DP2 strike, Sargent 421 closers, kickplates, Pemko 357SP astragal, S88D gasketing and 315SN sweeps, National Guard 713 thresholds and Ives FS446 exterior stops [Part 4, pp.223–224 — Verified]; A1.1 assigns no group, so doors map by type [Inferred]. ¶2.03 A instead calls key-in-knob cylindrical locksets keyed to the "Upper Skagit Indian Tribe standard," and ¶2.07 A points the exterior door stop detail to sheet C8.1, which 01 does not list [¶2.03 A, p.219; ¶2.07 A, p.220 — Verified; 01 — Verified; Unresolved — Issues]. ¶2.08 A adds one concrete pad with a Gempler's #2900 boot and shoe brush with scraper next to the Blower Building exterior door, location coordinated with the Owner [p.220 — Verified]. Execution requires adjusting and checking every operating item, instructing the Owner's staff, a return visit about six months after acceptance to re-adjust all hardware with a written report, and 2 sets of special tools [¶3.04 A–E, ¶3.05, p.222 — Verified].
