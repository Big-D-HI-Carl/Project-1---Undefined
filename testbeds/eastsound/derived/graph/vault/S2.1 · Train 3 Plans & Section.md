## Summary

Train 3 is a circular cast-in-place tank: a 12" concrete wall on a 12" mat slab foundation with a raised center pedestal [plans_8 p.1, S2.1 Detail 1 — Verified-Visual]. Wall reinforcing is #5 horizontal at 12" o.c. each face (Grade 75 bars, laps staggered per Detail 5) and #5 vertical at 12" o.c. each face with 3" clear, plus (2) #4 horizontal at the top [plans_8 p.1, Detail 4 — Verified-Visual]. Slab reinforcing is continuous #5 at 12" o.c. with #5 radial bars at 12" o.c. at the outer edge (top shown, bottom similar; 2" clear top, 3" clear bottom), the pedestal has #5 radial bars at 16" o.c., and the slab center adds #5 × 8'-0" top and bottom bars with radial bars ending at 3'-0" and 1'-0" diameter rings [plans_8 p.1, Details 1–3 — Verified-Visual]. Detail 4 requires a hydrophilic waterstop (submit for approval), a grout surface per spec, and subgrade preparation per the structural notes and geotech report, and shows "guardrail, mechanical equipment, and ladders per civil" [plans_8 p.1, Detail 4 — Verified-Visual]. Horizontal laps may align vertically only at every third bar or more, with lap length per 3/S4.1 [plans_8 p.1, Detail 5 — Verified-Visual]; the S4.1 table covers Grade 60 uncoated bars only while these horizontal bars are Grade 75 [plans_8 p.5; Add. 4 p.10, S4.1 Detail 3 Note 1 — Verified-Visual; Unresolved: need Grade 75 lap lengths — Issues, Conflicts #2].
