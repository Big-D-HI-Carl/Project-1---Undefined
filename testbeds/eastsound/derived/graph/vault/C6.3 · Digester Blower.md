## Summary

One digester blower sits on a new 3.25'×2.25' blower equipment pad per 3/C7.4 (keyed note 1) on the existing floor, dimensioned 2.50' from the west wall and 3.00' from the south wall, with a large unlabeled circular structure drawn beside it and a "2" separation between buildings" at the south wall. Its discharge is 4" air piping in 304 SS (keyed note 2) with a 4" butterfly valve (keyed note 3) on pipe supports per 5/C7.2 (keyed note 4), dropping below the floor to a 4" air pipe at INV 20.3 beside a 3" 2W water line at INV 21.5±. Add. 4 p.6 (C6.4 reissue) moves this blower pad to 4 ft from the wall and sets the building separation at 9 inches maximum; C6.3 was not reissued, and Add. 4 governs its dimensions (Issue 19).
