## Summary

Details 1–7 are typical concrete details: wall-to-mat-slab reinforcing (hydrophilic waterstop for hydraulic structures, submit for approval), wall corner and intersection reinforcing, development and lap splice lengths, standard hooks, beam stirrups, and small and large penetrations [plans_8 p.5; Add. 4 p.10 — Verified-Visual]. The development and splice table is for 4,500 psi concrete (for #5: Ld 28"/22" top/other, Ls 36"/28"), and its Note 1 limits the values to Grade 60 uncoated bars [plans_8 p.5; Add. 4 p.10, Detail 3 — Verified-Visual]. Add. 4 adds Detail 8, a trench in the existing slab for sub-floor pipe: saw-cut the existing slab (24" max.), encase the new pipe in CDF (1,000 psi min.), infill to match the existing slab thickness with #4 dowels @ 18" o.c. alternating sides, drilled and epoxied with 4" min. embedment, and sleeve per 6/S2.3 without coring through rebar where pipe passes under or through an existing footing [Add. 4 p.10, Detail 8 — Verified-Visual; Add. 4 governs]. Add. 4's C1.6A item sends floor repair at the sludge piping cuts to Detail 8, matching the Blower Building slab concrete and reinforcement, with new rebar doweled and epoxied at least 4" into existing concrete [Add. 4 p.3, Drawings C1.6A — Verified; Add. 4 governs].
