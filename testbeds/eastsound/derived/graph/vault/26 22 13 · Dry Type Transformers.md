## Summary

Dry-type transformers 600 V and below for distribution, lighting and control; NRTL-listed; sizes and windings per the drawings; ANSI/IEEE C57.12.01 and UL 506; GE, Square D, Westinghouse or equal; equipment must fit the space shown (¶1.01–1.04, ¶2.01, p.297) [Verified]. Construction: 15 kVA and above with two 2.5% taps above and four below rated primary voltage; copper terminals in the bottom third; 15 kVA and smaller weatherproof non-ventilated and wall-mountable, larger indoor units drip-proof ventilated and 20 kVA and larger floor-mounted (¶2.05–2.08, p.298) [Verified]. Sound limits of 42 dB for 10–45 kVA and 45 dB for 50–150 kVA, and minimum efficiencies such as 98.0% for a 75 kVA three-phase unit (¶2.09–2.010, pp.298–299) [Verified]. Installation on rubber-in-shear or external vibration isolators with flexible conduit connections; each transformer is a grounded-neutral separately derived system bonded to the building ground grid; engraved phenolic nameplates (¶3.01–3.03, p.299) [Verified]. Submittals: catalog data per 26 05 00 (¶1.05, p.297); no test paragraph (the only "test" term is the NRTL listing) [Verified].
