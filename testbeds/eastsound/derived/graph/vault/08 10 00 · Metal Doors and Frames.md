## Summary

Exterior insulated hollow metal (INS HM) doors are standard-duty, full flush, SDI 100 Grade II Model 1 with polyurethane or polyisocyanurate core and 18-gauge minimum faces, of cold-rolled A366 steel hot-dip galvanized to A526 G90 with factory baked-on primer [¶2.01 A, ¶2.02 A, pp.214–215 — Verified]. Frames are one-piece welded, 16-gauge minimum, galvanized, with integral stops, SDI 100 Grade II Model 2, and tempered glass windows are provided only where the door schedule calls for them [¶2.02 B–C, p.215 — Verified]. Hardware is furnished under 08 70 00 and installed here per SDI 107, doors install per SDI 105 with hardware adjusted and lubricated, and the section has no submittal or testing article of its own [¶1.01 A, ¶2.01 B, ¶3.01, pp.214–215 — Verified]. The matching items are A1.1's insulated hollow metal doors 101A, 101B and 101C (Types A and B, no glass) [plans_7 p.5 — Verified-Visual].
