## Summary

Blower Building layout: aeration blowers BL-1 to BL-4 (No.1–4), each with a motor symbol marked 20 and high-temperature switches (typ.); digester blower DB with a local disconnect drawn outside the building outline; MCC; Main Control Panel MCP; lighting panel LP-1; 2W pressure transducer; ceiling smoke/heat detector; door intrusion switch; GFCI receptacles; two power and two control/signal handholes (plan; plans_9 p.3) [Verified-Visual; MCP, MCC, LP-1, BL-2 to BL-4 also in text]. Key notes 1–6: grounding electrode system per NEC 250 (rods, concrete-encased electrode, structural steel, metal water pipe; detail 1/E10.1); stub-ups coordinated with the manufacturer; all blower connections per the manufacturer including motor and sensors, skids grounded; System Sensor 4WTAR-B smoke/heat detector and Interlogix Sentrol 2807T reed door switch wired to the PLC control panel; NEMA 4X stainless local disconnect (plans_9 p.3) [Verified]. Key notes 7–8: all field conduits leave through the handholes with power and signal/control separated and at least two spares from each power handhole to the MCC and each control handhole to the PLC panel, plus a separate PLC-to-MCC raceway (plans_9 p.3) [Verified]. Runs toward the Influent Pump Station control panel (E4.1): P-ICP, P-DW-1, P-SD1, P-WP1, P-WP2, S-FP3, S-FP4, S-ICP and spares; building runs P/C-BL1 to BL4, P/C-DB, C-SD1, C-2WPT, C-INT, P-MCP, C-MCP, S-MCP (plan; plans_9 p.3) [Verified; BL1, BL2 and DB runs Verified-Visual]. Add. 4 p.3 (A1.1/A1.2/C6.4 item) resizes the Blower Building and relocates the MCC, MCP, bladder tanks and exhaust fans, and Add. 4 p.1 Clarification 4 sets the footprint at 9.25 ft x 44.00 ft — Add. 4 governs over the E2.1 locations; the revised locations are on the A1.1 reissue (Add. 4 p.4, Structural & Building stack) [Verified].
