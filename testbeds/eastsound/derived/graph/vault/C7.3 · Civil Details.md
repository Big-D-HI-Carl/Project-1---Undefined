## Summary

Image-only sheet with six details: 1 WWTP Tank Sign Details, 2 Buried Valve Labeling Schedule, 3 Asphalt Concrete Pavement with Thickened Edge, 4 Typical Buried Valve Label Marker, 5 Gravel Surfacing Section, 6 1-in PVC Ball Valve (plans_6 p.5, C7.3 — Verified-Visual). Signs are baked enamel on steel, black on white, field-located by the Owner, for UV Disinfection Chamber (drawn 'DISENFECTION'), Blower Building, Dewatering Facility, Influent Pump Station, Aerobic Digester, Train 1, Train 2, Train 3 and 2W Water System Pump Station (Detail 1; plans_6 p.5, C7.3 — Verified-Visual). The labeling schedule lists 17 buried valves (1–5 match C1.4; 6–16 are CCB outlet box, CCB 1 and CCB 2 drains; 17 is an 8-in gate valve for effluent flow meter isolation), while its notes call for 14 bronze markers and name C2.4 and C2.5 as the schematic valve location sheets (Details 2, 4; plans_6 p.5, C7.3 — Verified-Visual; conflicts, see Issues). Each buried valve gets a 4-1/2-in minimum cast iron valve box in a 2.5 ft square, 4-in concrete pad with a Berntsen 3-1/2-in bronze marker (#C35FB) stamped with valve number and description (Detail 4; plans_6 p.5, C7.3 — Verified-Visual). Pavement is 4 in of HMA Class 1/2-in PG 58H-22 in two lifts over 4 in of crushed surfacing base course and 12 in of gravel base with a thickened edge; gravel surfacing is 4 in of base course; both defer to WSDOT 5-04, 9-03.9(3) and 9-03.10 (Details 3, 5; plans_6 p.5, C7.3 — Verified-Visual; Unresolved: external reference, not staged).
