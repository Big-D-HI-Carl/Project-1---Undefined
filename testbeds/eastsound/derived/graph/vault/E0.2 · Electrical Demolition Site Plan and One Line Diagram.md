## Summary

Existing 208Y/120V service being removed: OPALCO 45 kVA pad-mount utility transformer, CT enclosure and utility meter, 300 A SUSE-rated NEMA 3R service entrance breaker (SES), a 4-wire 3-pole automatic transfer switch (ATS), and a 30 kW / 37.5 kVA 208Y/120V diesel standby generator (GEN) with fuel tank, exhaust and louvers (one-line and demo plan; plans_8 p.7) [Verified-Visual]. Demo notes D1–D7: OPALCO replaces the 208Y/120V transformer with a 480Y/277V unit on the existing pad (downtime per specifications); remove the generator, fuel tank, exhaust and louvers and repair building openings per civil/architectural; remove the CT enclosure and meter after the new service is operational; remove the 300 A main breaker and the ATS; replace the 200 A feeder to Panel TPS; keep the existing grounding electrode system and bond new work to it; remove the obsolete meter base and wall raceway but keep the telephone junction box and service (plans_8 p.7) [Verified]. General note 1: the new 480Y/277V service with a new diesel generator re-feeds the existing 208 V equipment; general note 2: the Owner may keep any demo material and the contractor legally disposes of the rest (plans_8 p.7) [Verified]. Kept in service: Panel TPS in the Treatment Building (208Y/120V, 3-ph 4-W, 200 A) with its 100 A load center, and the Office Building distribution (400 A Square-D I-Line panel, 100 A switchboard, Lab panel, Panels SP and SH), labeled existing and outside the demo hatch (plans_8 p.7) [Verified for TPS; Office Building panels Verified-Visual].
