## Summary

Second Allen-Bradley 1769-IF8 8-channel analog input card with the same wiring as sheet 1 (plans_11 p.3) [Verified]. Channels 0–3: Cell #1 and Cell #2 dissolved oxygen, zones 1 and 2, drawn greyed and labeled "(FUTURE)" with range "0-XX PPM" (plans_11 p.3) [Verified-Visual]. Channels 4–5: Cell #3 dissolved oxygen zone 1 and zone 2, 4–20 mA, the two Train 3 DO transmitters (DO-1 and DO-2 by zone number); channels 6–7 spare (plans_11 p.3) [Verified; tag match Inferred]. What Phase I provides for the four future channels beyond the card itself is not stated — see Issues (plans_11 p.3) [Unresolved: Phase I scope of Train 1 and 2 provisions].
