## Summary

Digester alarm set points: high alarm 25.55 on transducer level sensor S1 and low alarm 15.30 on transducer level sensor S2. Section B runs through the digester (top of wall 26.80, high water surface 25.3, T-valve operation range 21.3 to 25.3, bottom EL 14.3 and 13.3, TOC 14.8 and 15.3, slope 3.1%, 30° minimum slope for non-shrink grout) and the UV chamber, showing a 4" telescoping valve "see specifications", the 2" SCH 80 PVC digested sludge line (IE 20.50) on vertical pipe supports per 7/C7.2, the 3" WAS pipe (IE 19.50), the 4" drain pipe (IE 17.25), the 10" effluent pipe (IE 18.75), a "12" D.I. final effluent" label, 8" DI secondary effluent into the 8" influent pipe (IE 19.61), a control panel, concrete supports for the UV equipment (four total), HWL 21.49 and 21.09, handwheels for exposed valves, and 1" SCH 80 PVC drains to a floor drain (IE 16.00). Section C (digester) shows the 4" air pipe (IE 20.0) to coarse bubble diffusers, the 4" telescoping valve on vertical pipe supports per 6/C7.2, a 2" intake for digested sludge (IE 14.80) in a 2.00' square sump, the 4" ductile iron supernatant drain (IE 15.3), the I-beam ("see structural") and an 8.3% floor slope. Section D (UV chamber) shows the two UV units on supports, 4" floor drains (rim EL 18.74), 1" SCH 80 PVC drains for all four drain ports and a 4" ductile iron floor drain (IE 16.00). The anti-buoyancy lip and the 12" CSBC base "per std specs 9-03.12(1)A" repeat C5.2 — Unresolved: external reference, not staged.
