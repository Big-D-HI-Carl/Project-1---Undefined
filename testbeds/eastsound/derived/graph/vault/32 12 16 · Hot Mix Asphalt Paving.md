## Summary

Covers HMA paving, restoration of all asphalt disturbed by construction, and temporary cold-mix (MC-2) patches, under the 2022 WSDOT/APWA Standard Specifications (¶1.01, ¶2.02; main spec pp.404–405 — Verified; Unresolved: external reference, not staged). HMA is Class 1/2-in PG 58H-22 per WSDOT 5-04.2 at 91% minimum density, commercial classification, with the mix design submitted on WSDOT form 350-042 and verified by WSDOT; lab certificates (sieve analysis, mix design) are due at least 5 days before placement (¶1.02, ¶2.01; p.404 — Verified). Asphalt is placed in 2-in lifts, matching existing thickness or the plan repair section, whichever is thicker; no material transfer devices; joints sealed with hot-poured sealant (¶3.02, ¶3.04; p.405 — Verified). The Contractor performs QC testing (asphalt content, gradation, voids, fracture, equivalence, in-place density) 'with Section 01400', which is not in the manual (¶3.05; p.405 — Verified; see Issues). Sawcuts are continuous, full-depth and pre-approved by the Owner in the field, trench patches extend 1 ft beyond the trench, and castings and monuments are referenced before removal and adjusted to grade (¶3.06–3.09; pp.405–407 — Verified).
