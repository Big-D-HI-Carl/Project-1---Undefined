## Summary

The contractor furnishes and installs a molded, UV-protected, opaque FRP cover over the Train 3 clarifier weir launder and weir, in curved sections at least 4 ft long, each a single section on two pivoting brackets opening toward the tank center, with spring-loaded latches, an optional stainless tether, fixed or vertical panels at the bridge support, no piano hinges, no inspection hatches, and not walk-on except for a reinforced walk-on section at the launder entry (¶1.01 A–H pp.243–244). Panels are isophthalic polyester with UV suppression, at least 35% glass by weight (ASTM D2584-18), nominal 1/4" thick and no more than 55 lb each, with tensile 26,000 psi, flexural 30,000 psi, flexural modulus 1.9×10^6 psi, Barcol 50, notched Izod 20 ft-lb/in and water absorption 0.25% max; fasteners, handles, hinges and latches are stainless (¶3.01 B–G pp.244–245). Submittals per 01 33 00 are catalog data and O&M information (¶2.01 A p.244); the contractor adds a 1-year parts-and-labor extended warranty from substantial completion (¶1.02 A p.244); three sets of wear parts are furnished (¶3.02 p.245); and the manufacturer provides installation certification, 2 days of start-up and field testing and 1 day of operator training (¶4.02 p.245). ¶3.01 A, under the launder cover heading, repeats the packaged sludge pump paragraph of 22 13 36 ¶2.01 A word for word (Edson peristaltic pumps, 3 HP, a VFD per pump) — Issue 20. The section runs in four parts (Part 2 submittals, Part 3 products, Part 4 execution).
