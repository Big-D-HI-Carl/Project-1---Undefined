## Summary

Grounding per the NEC with soft-drawn bare stranded copper sized per the drawings; Burndy Hyground compression terminals and connectors; Cadweld or T&B exothermic welds; copper-clad rods with 0.010 in minimum cladding, 3/4 in x 8 ft minimum (¶1.01, ¶2.01, p.289) [Verified]. Installation: contact surfaces cleaned to bright metal; grounding conductors in PVC where subject to damage and all conductors smaller than 6 AWG protected; compression connections and splices; an equipment grounding conductor in every PVC conduit (¶3.01–3.02, p.289) [Verified]. No submittal or test paragraph; ground resistance testing sits in 26 05 19 ¶3.07 (25 ohms per rod, 15 ohms composite) (p.289; p.288) [Verified]. The rod size matches the E10.1 ground rod and handhole details (8 ft copper-clad) (p.289; plans_12 p.6) [Verified].
