## Summary

Project meetings coordinate the work, resolve conflicts, review progress and quality, and keep the work on schedule; attendees must have authority to act (¶1.01, p.126; Verified). Related sections include "00 25 13 Pre-Bid Conference", which is not in the 02 index (Issues 2.5), and "01 11 00", the accepted alias of 01 11 10 (¶1.02, p.126). The pre-construction meeting follows the Notice to Proceed per the General Conditions time frame and covers schedule, phasing, liquidated damages, Owner-furnished materials status, staking, labor standards, rights-of-way and placement of project signs and posters (¶1.03, pp.126–127; Verified; timing conflicts with SC 13 — Issues 1.10). The Owner runs weekly progress meetings near the site with Engineer minutes within a week (¶1.04, p.127; Verified). A preliminary CPM schedule is due within 10 days of Contract Award, updated monthly with the pay estimate and kept at the Contractor's field office (¶1.05, pp.127–128; Verified; due date conflicts — Issues 1.12).
