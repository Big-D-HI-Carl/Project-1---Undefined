## Summary

2W Pump Station: two submersible pumps tagged P-2W-1 and P-2W-2 in equipment tag boxes (motors marked 5) with a factory float tree and two factory junction boxes, plus Hot Box #1 (1-in flow meter, isolation valve solenoid, GFEP, J-box, heat trace) and Hot Box #2 (heat trace); home runs P-2W, C-2W, S-2WFM and P-HB1, with P-HB2 and P-2WSV at the hot boxes (plan and hot box details; plans_9 p.8) [Verified-Visual; P-2WSV, S-2WFM, P-HB1, P-HB2 also in text]. Key notes 1–2: the pump system includes two factory junction boxes with strain-relief grips (one for motor cables, one for floats), power raceway to MCC buckets and control wiring to the PLC control panel, and a factory float tree with four floats — F1 (EQ# LSH-111) high level alarm and redundant valve close 22.00, F2 valve close 20.00, F3 valve open 19.00, F4 (EQ# LSL-111) low level alarm and pumps off 15.00 (plans_9 p.8) [Verified; float table Verified-Visual]. Key note 3: heat trace on all exposed piping — Raychem BTV self-regulating cable or equal on a dedicated 120 V 20 A GFEP circuit, NEMA 4X thermostat and end-of-line kit with indicator light (plans_9 p.8) [Verified]. Key notes 4–5: the 2W flow meter is a 1-in Endress+Hauser Promag W400 (24 VAC/DC, EtherNet/IP, local display, NEMA 4X) with two 316L grounding rings, matching Add. 4 p.2 (26 80 00 ¶2.04 D); solenoid and heat-trace power lands in a J-box in Hot Box #1 and extends to Hot Box #2 (plans_9 p.8) [Verified]. The pump tags differ from E6.1 (2W-P1, 2W-P2) and the meter signal run reads S-FM2W at the hot box but S-2WFM at the home run; Add. 4 p.3 (C1.3 item) re-orients "the 2W (Air Gap) Water Hot Box" for vehicle traffic — see Issues (plans_9 p.8; plans_10 p.2; Add. 4 p.3) [Verified-Visual].
