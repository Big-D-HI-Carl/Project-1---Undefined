## Summary

The Contractor designs, details, fabricates and installs galvanized steel stairs, ladders and platform framing where shown on the plans, with non-skid grating treads and aluminum grating platforms [¶1.01 A.1, p.200 — Verified]. Submittals are PE-sealed calculations and location-specific shop drawings (typical-detail sets are rejected), coordinated with the grating drawings and with aluminum handrails "as shown in the civil drawings" per 05 52 00 and 05 53 00 [¶1.03 A.1–3, p.200 — Verified], matching the deferred submittal for metal stairs and landings on S1.1 [plans_7 p.8 — Verified-Visual]. The Drawing Structural Notes govern over less specific requirements, with AISC 360, AWS D1.1 (2015) and IBC 2018 as standards, and all framing is hot-dip galvanized after fabrication and bolted in the field [¶1.04 A–D, p.200; ¶2.03, p.201 — Verified]. Unless shown otherwise, stairs use 7" risers, 11" treads and 1" nosing with 4'-0" min. clear width, access ladders have 3'-0" min. clear width, and field welding needs written approval at shop drawing review [¶2.04, p.201; ¶3.01 D, p.202 — Verified].
