## Summary

Detail 1 shows the Trojan UV disinfection unit: one bank (1A) of "4-64 UV PTP" modules, "6 required / bank" (read at stored resolution; the text layer also carries 6 module label groups), in a steel channel about 13'-7 1/2" long (a 9'-7" module section between 2'-0 3/4" transition boxes, 1'-4" inside channel height) with removable cover plates, module lamp status viewing plates, an access plate with warning label, solid blocking, 2" NPT drains with plugs, a standard 3600K level control weir with a 1" NPT drain plug, and flanged influent and effluent connections drilled to fit 12" DIN, BS and JIS pipe; HWL 21.49 upstream, 21.09 downstream, EL 19.08. Power: a splitter panel within 10 ft of the module power end (120 V, 1-phase, 19.1 A from distribution panel DP, not shown), power distribution receptacles (6.3 A each, from the splitter panel), 10' grounded power cords, and a wall-mounted system monitor within 15 ft of the channel (120 V, 5 A from DP). Notes: do not slope the channel floor; hold channel width and depth to ±1/4"; the contractor supplies all anchor bolts (3/8" [M10], 4 per transition box); electrical requirements cover the Trojan equipment only, with inrush per local code and extra outlets excluded from the interconnect amperage; review all Trojan installation instructions first; do not encase the steel channel in concrete. The module count and "4-64" label do not match G0.7's 8 modules per channel and 2 lamps per module (Issue 13).
