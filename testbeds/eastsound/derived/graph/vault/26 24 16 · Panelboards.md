## Summary

Lighting and power distribution panelboards by Square D, Cutler Hammer or equal; submittals are catalog data per 26 05 00 (¶1.01–1.02, ¶2.01, p.300) [Verified]. Interiors: main ratings up to 600 A (main breaker) or 800 A (main lug), one fully rated plated-copper bus per phase, bolt-on breakers, suitable for service equipment where applied; thermal-magnetic trip-free main breakers (¶2.02 A–B, pp.300–301) [Verified]. Minimum interrupting ratings 10,000 AIC at 240 V and 208Y/120V and 25,000 AIC at 480 V and 480Y/277V; spaces fully bussed; surge arresters with indicators on all panels; schedules on the drawings (¶2.02 G–I, p.303) [Verified]. Execution: top of cabinet at 6 ft-6 in unless noted; typed circuit index; field quality control with inspection, phase load balancing within 20% and torque checks (¶3.01–3.03, pp.303–304) [Verified]. The E6.2 schedules show an SPD only at MDP (via E6.1); the spec adds surge arresters on LP1 and LP2 — drawings and specifications are complementary per 26 05 00 ¶1.011 B (pp.303, 274; plans_10 pp.2–3) [Inferred].
