## Summary

The Blower Building is one room (Room 101, Blower Room, FF=23.30); Add. 4 resizes it from 42.00' × 9.50' to 44.00' × 9.25' [plans_7 p.5 — Verified-Visual; Add. 4 p.1 Clarification 4 and p.4 — Verified; Add. 4 governs]. Add. 4 extends the new slab 9" north to abut the existing Train 2 building slab, has the Contractor field-measure and match the Train 2 finished floor, and states about 9" clearance between the buildings with the 10-ft setback kept from the south and east property lines [Add. 4 p.3, p.4 — Verified; Add. 4 governs]. The base plan shows four blowers on a concrete blower pad 22.60' long and 4.50' deep (3" typical blower-to-pad edge) set 6.00' from the west wall (7.00' per Add. 4), an electrical panel bank with a 3.5-ft clearance note, two Ø24" bladder tanks per 1/C4.3, a 36"×84" motorized louver and a 24"×24" manual louver on the west wall, and two 24"×24" vents with exhaust fans on the east wall [plans_7 p.5 — Verified-Visual; Add. 4 p.4 — Verified; Add. 4 governs]. Add. 4 moves the MCC as far east as possible and adds a section (eight 20" sections, 160", spare section for future expansion), moves the MCP panel and its raceways, relocates the 2W bladder tank assembly per C6.4, and calls for 2 fire extinguisher assemblies (base shows 1) at wall locations coordinated with the Owner [Add. 4 p.1 Clarification 4, p.3, p.4 — Verified; plans_7 p.5 — Verified-Visual; Add. 4 governs]. The door schedule lists insulated hollow-metal, painted doors 101A (two pairs, 10'-0"×8'-0"×1-3/4", Type B), 101B (3'-0" wide, Type A; height illegible at stored resolution) and 101C (5'-0"×8'-0"×1-3/4", Type A) with no hardware group, and the finish schedule with Details 1–2 calls for a sealed concrete floor, 6" rubber base, painted GWB walls, painted exposed-structure ceiling, and R-25 wall / R-30 roof fiberglass insulation with vapor barrier and board on furring channels at 24" o.c. [plans_7 p.5 — Verified-Visual; 101B height — Unresolved: need native sheet].
