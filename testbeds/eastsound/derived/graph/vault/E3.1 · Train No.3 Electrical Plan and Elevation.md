## Summary

Train No.3: a Train No.3 control panel furnished by the treatment system provider serves the clarifier drive motor and two submersible mixers, each motor with a NEMA 4X Type 316 stainless local disconnect by the electrical contractor; dissolved oxygen sensor/transmitter DO-1 (Zone 1) and a second DO run C-DO2; a platform pole light; conduits P-TCP3, C-TCP3, P-CL3, P-MX3A, P-MX3B, P-TS3A, P-TS3B and capped spares (plan and key notes; plans_9 p.5) [Verified]. Key note 1: the provider's panel takes 480 V 3-phase and includes a NEMA 4X 316 SS enclosure with drip shield, full-voltage starters for the clarifier drive and both mixers, seal-fail and over-temperature protection, a 480–120 V control transformer, hard-wired relay logic, alarm light and horn, HOA switches and indicators, field terminals, dry contacts (run/fail per motor, mixer seal-fail/over-temp, clarifier alarm/shutdown), a panel heater and a 120 V circuit for the pole light and receptacle (plans_9 p.5) [Verified]. Ratings, raceway and conductors shown are estimates to be adjusted during the submittal period from the treatment-system submittal, and all outdoor control panel and disconnect enclosures are Type 316 stainless (key note; general note 4; plans_9 p.5) [Verified]. DO monitoring: two complete monitors per cell, Hach LDO probe with SC200 transmitter (NEMA 4X, analog output, sun shield), 24 VDC, mounted at the wall top with stainless hardware (key note; DO probe elevation detail 1; plans_9 p.5) [Verified]. Pole light: 4-in square aluminum pole, 12 ft, secured to the handrail with stainless hardware, with receptacle, photocell and local switch, Lithonia "#DXS0-LED-P3-30K-T3M-MVOLT-SPA-DNAXD" as printed or equal; power and signal conduits to the UV/Digester area and the 2W Pump Station pass through this area (see E5.1, E4.3) (plans_9 p.5) [Verified].
