## Summary

The plant water (2W) pump station is a 96" diameter precast wet well (floor 10.00', top 21.50', on a 12" crushed surfacing base course) under a 30" fiberglass wet well and access structure (top 0.2' above FG, 23.1'±) with an insulated fiberglass gasketed lid on stainless bolts and a 38" traffic-rated lid and manhole ring per 3/C7.5. 2W water arrives "from air gap, see C4.3" in 4" SCH 80 PVC at 2% slope (4" inlet IE 19.50; Kor-N-Seal or equal at the wall penetration), while the plan view labels a 6" inlet pipe (Issue 39). Two submersible well pumps per specifications (single-pump duty points 20 gpm @ 380' TDH, 72 gpm @ 150' TDH, 80 gpm @ 85' TDH) each discharge through a 2" high-pressure discharge assembly (ball valve, true union ball check valve, high-pressure flex hose, quick disconnect; Orenco or approved equal) set within 12" of the lid, with a pressure relief valve rated 110 psi or greater within 24" of the wet well top, then 2" SCH 80 PVC and a 2"×3" reducer to 3" HDPE; field-fit brackets steady the flow-induced tower assembly. Floats: F1 high level alarm and redundant valve close 22.00, F2 valve close 20.00, F3 valve open 19.00, F4 low level alarm and pumps off 15.00; a 1" PVC vent is tapped into the basin and run to a field-located point out of the vehicle drive path (coordinate with Owner). Add. 4 p.1 Clarification 2 (exterior of all precast concrete wet well joints sealed with CANUSA-CPS WrapidSeal or approved equal) applies to this precast wet well, where C4.2 calls no joint sealant — Add. 4 governs.
