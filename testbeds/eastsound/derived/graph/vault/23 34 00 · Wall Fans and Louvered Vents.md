## Summary

Scope (¶1.01 A p.265): in the Blower Building, two 3,300 CFM wall fans on individual thermostats, one motorized 36"×84" louvered vent that opens when either fan runs and stays open until both are off, and one manual 24"×24" louvered vent; in the "Existing Dewatering/Train 1&2 Building Area", three 3,300 CFM wall fans on individual manual on/off switches and two manual 36"×84" louvered vents. Fans are Acme FQ18G6 (3,300 CFM, 1/2 HP, 120 V 1-phase, 12 sones max) with wall collar and guard, each with a Louvers & Dampers EBE445 aluminum gravity combination louver (about 24"×24") with stainless bird screen and a 2-year minimum manufacturer warranty; thermostats are Schaffer TH109 two-speed, 40–100°F, in NEMA 4X enclosures with stainless coil sensors, 4' above floor (¶1.01 B; ¶2.1 A–B pp.265–266). Vents are "Pottoroff" (as printed) EXD-645 combination louver/dampers with drainable blades, vinyl seals, washable 18×16 stainless insect screens and non-protruding actuation; the motorized unit uses a Pottoroff AF120 120 V spring-return actuator driven from the fan thermostat auxiliary contacts (¶2.2–2.3 pp.266–267). ¶2.1 A.4 calls all wall fans dual speed and thermostat controlled, while ¶1.01 A puts the three existing-building fans on manual switches (Issue 25). The section has no submittal or testing paragraph; installation follows the manufacturer's instructions, with controls 4' above floor (¶3.1 p.267).
