## Summary

Start-up design (after Phase I) is ADF 0.10, MMF 0.12, max day 0.30 and peak hour 0.49 MGD; build-out (year 2040, after Phase II — phase numerals read from the image, Verified-Visual) is 0.19, 0.22, 0.46 and 0.77 MGD. The Influent Pump Station has 2 small submersible pumps (120–220 gpm each) and 2 large (320–500 gpm each), firm capacity 535 gpm (0.77 MGD) with one small and one large out of service; electromagnetic flow meters are influent 6" (0–880 gpm), Train #2 and Train #3 8" (0–470 gpm each), effluent 8" (0–940 gpm), plant drain 4" (0–200 gpm) and 2W water 1" (0–25 gpm). Train 3 has one 5 HP submersible mixer in each anoxic zone (#1 15,686 gal, #2 8,789 gal), aeration zone #1 (29,793 gal, 8 diffusers, 192 scfm) and zone #2 (9,889 gal, 2 diffusers, 48 scfm) with "medium bubble" aeration mixing, a RAS airlift (15 scfm, 3", 51 gpm) and a nitrate recycle airlift (61 scfm, 6", 203 gpm), for 316 scfm total at 9.2 psig; clarifiers are 3 units, 18 ft, SWD 13.25' (cells 1–2) and 15.50' (cell 3). UV has 2 lamps per module, 8 modules per channel and 2 channels at 0.5 MGD each, design dose printed "30,000 MJ/CM2" and UVT 50% or greater; the aerobic digester is 15,000 gal with coarse bubble aeration (6 diffusers × 20 cfm) and manual T-valve decant control. WAS and digester pumps are each 1 duty + 1 standby (swing) hose (peristaltic) pumps, 0–40 gpm; one rotary fan press takes 12 gpm at 2% or 20 gpm at 0.5% solids and makes 12–16% cake (up to 1,000 wet / 120 dry lb/hr); 2W water pumps are 1 duty + 1 standby submersible, 20–80 gpm on VFD control.
