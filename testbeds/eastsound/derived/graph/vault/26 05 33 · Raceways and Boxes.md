## Summary

Raceways for power, control, instrumentation, grounding, lighting, receptacles and signaling; scheduled raceway per the drawings, which prevail over ¶3.1 on conflict; unscheduled raceway 1/2 in minimum exposed and 1 in minimum embedded (¶1.01, ¶2.01, p.290) [Verified]. Raceway types (¶2.02, pp.290–291): galvanized rigid steel unless noted; liquid-tight flexible metal conduit to motors and vibrating equipment; UV-resistant Schedule 80 PVC above grade; Schedule 40 PVC underground with PVC-coated GRS sweeps and risers; PVC-coated rigid steel with 40 mil coating where called for [Verified]. Conduit schedule and tags (¶2.05–2.06, p.294): the drawing schedule governs sizing and routing, adjusted for equipment submittals at no added cost; crosslinked polyolefin tags, Impact Industries or equal (p.294) [Verified]. Handholes and tape (¶2.07–2.08, p.294): precast 3,000 psi reinforced concrete, galvanized checker-plate traffic covers for H-20 loading, solid slab bottoms with sumps and drains; detectable 2 in marking tape, red for electric lines (p.294) [Verified]. Conduit runs (¶3.01 A, p.294): no more than 270 degrees of bends between pull boxes and runs of 400 ft less 100 ft per 90 degrees of bend; no product submittal or test paragraph found in the section (pp.290–296) [Verified; submittal and test terms searched on every page].
