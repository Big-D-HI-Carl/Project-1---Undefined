## Summary

Covers HDPE pipe installation; fusion operator qualifications go to the Engineer for approval before pipe is ordered (¶1.01–1.02; main spec p.444 — Verified). Operators must be manufacturer-trained with 5,000 LF of HDPE fusion experience, a comparable project in the last three years, and current 49 CFR 192.283/192.285/192.287 certifications for the machine used (¶1.03; p.444 — Verified). Pipe is PE4710 (ASTM D3350 cell class 445574C), DriscoPlex 4000/4100 IPS or equal, at the DR in 33 31 00 or on the plans, with color stripes: green sewage, light blue 2W, dark blue potable (¶2.01–2.02; pp.444–445 — Verified). Joints are butt-fused above ground to the manufacturer's procedure; all joints are visually inspected and data-logged (logs submitted), and 3 joints or 3%, whichever is greater, are destructively tested (¶3.03; p.446 — Verified). Pressure testing follows ASTM F2164 and Performance Pipe Technical Note 802 (¶3.04; p.446 — Verified).
