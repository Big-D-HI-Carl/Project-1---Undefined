## Summary

Covers a spray-applied, monolithic, high-build 100%-solids epoxy lining on all interior concrete surfaces of the listed structure — the Influent Pump Station, with detailed locations per the drawings [¶1.01 A–B, p.236 — Verified]. Submittals are product data with ASTM results, MSDS, project guidelines and applicator qualifications (manufacturer certification, trained equipment operators, 5 references, permits) [¶1.02 A, p.236 — Verified]. The product is Raven 405 or Tnemec G436 PermaShield (amine-cured, light green), applied by a certified applicator with heated plural-component spray on concrete cured at least 28 days and blasted to CSP 3–5, at 80 mils min. and 100 mils average wet film [¶2.01 A, ¶2.04 A, ¶2.05, ¶3.03 E, ¶3.05 D, pp.237–239 — Verified]. Testing covers wet-film readings (ASTM D4414), optional ultrasonic dry-film checks (SSPC-PA 9), high-voltage holiday testing at 100 V/mil (NACE RP0188 or ASTM D4787), and pull-off adhesion (ASTM D7234) with 2 of 3 pulls over 200 psi per test area, all documented to the Owner [¶3.06 A–D, pp.239–241 — Verified].
