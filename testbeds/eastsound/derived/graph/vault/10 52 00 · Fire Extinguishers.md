## Summary

Covers furnishing and installing all fire extinguishers and cabinets shown, with product data (plus cabinet roughing-in and mounting details) as the submittal [¶1.01 A–B, ¶1.02 A, p.242 — Verified]. Extinguishers are J.L. Industries Cosmic 5E, UL 2A-10BC, standard red, on the manufacturer's standard brackets that prevent accidental dislodgment [¶2.01 A–B, p.242 — Verified]. At completion the work is visually inspected and checked for proper operation, and each extinguisher carries an inspection tag [¶3.01 C, p.242 — Verified]. The Blower Building gets 2 wall-mounted assemblies per Add. 4, where base A1.1 shows 1 [Add. 4 p.4 — Verified; plans_7 p.5 — Verified-Visual; Add. 4 governs].
