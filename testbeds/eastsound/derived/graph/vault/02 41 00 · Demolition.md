## Summary

Covers demolition, removal, rehabilitation and equipment salvage shown on the drawings; removed items become the Contractor's property except miscellaneous valves, piping and electronic components the Owner selects for salvage (¶1.01 B, ¶1.02 A; main spec p.161 — Verified). All pipes connected to abandoned structures are plugged or grouted, proposed wastewater equipment must be purchased and on hand before specified structures are removed, and removal is coordinated with the Owner's staff at least 2 weeks ahead (¶3.01 B; p.161 — Verified). Structures to be removed: the existing septic tank, the existing pump sump pump station, and miscellaneous valves, piping, concrete and accessories per the plans, with electrical drawings and specifications for more demolition (¶3.01 C; p.162 — Verified). Debris is hauled to a legal disposal site and no burning is allowed on site (¶3.02; p.162 — Verified). The section has no submittal or testing paragraph; the demolition plan with schedule is a C0.5 General Note requirement (p.161–162 — Verified; C0.5, plans_2Part-4 p.1 — Verified-Visual).
