## Summary

Six details, not to scale: 1 Typical Yard Hydrant, 2 Typical Floor Drain, 3 Traffic Rated Riser and Lid, 4 Parking Stall Striping, 5 Gravity Sewer Cleanout, 6 Tracer Wire Locater Box (plans_6 p.3, C7.1 — Verified; numbering Verified-Visual). The yard hydrant is a 1-in frostproof hydrant with a 1-in atmospheric vacuum breaker anchored to a post, and its callout points to 'Site Piping Plan, Sheet C2.2', which is the storm drainage sheet (Detail 1; plans_6 p.3, C7.1 — Verified-Visual; see Issues). The floor drain is a 4-in stainless steel drain with a 2-in trap and PVC outlet; the traffic-rated riser uses a 24-in cast iron frame and cover sealed with asphaltic sealant (Ramnek or equal), with a 12 x 8-in reinforced collar in traffic areas (Details 2–3; plans_6 p.3, C7.1 — Verified-Visual). Parking stalls are 9 ft minimum by 18'-6" with a van-accessible stall and 5-ft aisle; striping conforms to WSDOT 8-22 and SJCC 18.30.630(A)(4) (Detail 4; plans_6 p.3, C7.1 — Verified-Visual; WSDOT part Unresolved: external reference, not staged). Tracer wire is required on all new water piping, zip-strapped at 6-ft intervals and continuous, with locater boxes next to every building or structure the pipe enters; the Contractor performs a conductivity/locate test on completion and final box locations go to the Engineer (Detail 6 Notes 1–6; plans_6 p.3, C7.1 — Verified-Visual).
