## Summary

Receptacles, switches and other wiring devices: UL, NEMA WD-1, grounding type; 20 A duplex NEMA 5-20R (Hubbell 5362 or equal) indoors; GFCI (Hubbell GF-5362 or equal) where NEC 210.8 or local code requires (¶1.01–1.02, p.312) [Verified]. Outdoor, process and corrosive areas use corrosion-resistant duplex 20 A devices; 20 A switches with weatherproof neoprene plates in copper-free aluminum or PVC boxes; engraved phenolic plates naming panel and circuit (¶1.02 B–D, pp.312–313) [Verified]. Boxes independently supported and devices tested for correct connections (¶2.01, p.313) [Verified]. No submittal paragraph found in the section (term search on both pages) (pp.312–313) [Verified].
