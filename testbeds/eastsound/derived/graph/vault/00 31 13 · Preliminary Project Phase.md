## Summary

The phasing plan is for planning only and does not dictate means and methods (p.21; Verified). San Juan County building permit applications were submitted Nov 2021 with approvals June 2022; bid phase: advertise Jan 4, pre-bid Jan 18, open bids Feb 8, award Feb 15, preconstruction meeting Mar 15, 2023 (p.21; Verified). Construction starts with submittals after award (long-lead items first) and March 2023 mobilization, a temporary bypass pipe per the Plan of Interim Operation (April 2023), then Blower Building, digester, UV disinfection chamber, sludge pumps, yard piping, vaults and electrical (April 2023), the 2W air gap water system (May 2023), influent pump station (June 2023) and Train 3 (August 2023) (¶A.1–10, p.21; Verified). A temporary 480V generator is rented from Sept 25, 2023 until the permanent 480V generator is installed, tested and on line in April 2024, and the new 480V service, MCC and PLC panel complete Oct 4, 2023 (¶A.11–12, p.21; ¶A.21, p.22; Verified). Milestones: major piping Nov 1, 2023; dewatering system Feb 9, 2024; Train 3 complete July 8, 2024 then seeded with 10,000 gal of activated sludge July 8–12; Substantial Completion July 26, 2024; Final Completion Oct 16, 2024 (¶A.18–27, p.22; Verified).
