## Summary

The Contractor must not interrupt District operations, must verify locations and depths of existing utilities (shown for information only), must report dimension discrepancies to the Engineer, and must repair damage to existing improvements within 48 hours (General Notes 1, 3–6; plans_1 p.3, G0.3 — Verified). Trenches and excavations must meet federal OSHA requirements, and trench shoring design and installation are the Contractor's full responsibility (General Note 7; plans_1 p.3, G0.3 — Verified). A 1-week notice to the District is required before any utility service disruption, and the Contractor must provide temporary equipment (power generator, pumps, piping) and an approved plan for all WWTP disruptions; the note cites 'Section 01 11 00', whose body number is 01 11 10 (General Note 10; plans_1 p.3, G0.3 — Verified; alias already flagged, 02 conflict 1). The Operation Plan makes the Contractor responsible for sequencing so the existing WWTP is not interrupted and for all temporary bypass pumping, piping, holding tanks and storage (Operation Plan 1–3; plans_1 p.3, G0.3 — Verified). Survey monuments disturbed by construction need WA DNR permits under WAC 332-120 and replacement by a registered land surveyor at Contractor cost; earthwork, concrete and asphalt repair are to be prioritized in dry-weather months; County inspections (footings, concrete structures, electrical, final) and S1.1 special inspections need 24-hour notice to Owner and Engineer (Schedule, Survey Monumentation and Construction Inspections notes; plans_1 p.3, G0.3 — Verified).
