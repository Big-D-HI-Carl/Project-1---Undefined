## Summary

Addendum-only sheet with two plan views of existing-building areas showing process pipes to be routed below the existing concrete slabs where clouded in red (Add. 4 p.8, C1.6A — Verified-Visual). Pipes called out: 3-in 2W water reducing to 2-in before the sub-floor run to the dewatering skid, 2-in 2W water to the skid, 4-in pressate drain from the dewatering skid to the influent pump station, 2-in digested sludge (digester to pump; pump to dewatering skid), 2-in WAS (Train #3 to pump; pump to digester), 8-in secondary effluent from the Train #2 clarifier to UV disinfection, 4-in air from blower to digester, and 1-in 2W water from the bladder tanks to the 3-in 2W pipe (Add. 4 p.8, C1.6A — Verified). The sheet note requires concrete coring and/or saw cutting for access to sub-floor piping and cites Addendum #3 (Add. 4 p.8, C1.6A — Verified). Add. 4 p.3 governs the work: sludge piping runs below the existing floor in the clouded areas, all cuts are clean-edge by coring or saw cutting, floor repair matches the Blower Building slab design per Detail 8 on S4.1, and new rebar is doweled and epoxied with 4 in minimum embedment (Add. 4 p.3 — Verified). The base issue of C1.6A is not in the library (already flagged, 01 item 11).
