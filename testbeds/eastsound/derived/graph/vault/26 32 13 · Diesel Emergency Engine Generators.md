## Summary

Basis of design Cummins C125D6D; pre-approved Cummins, Kohler, Caterpillar and Generac, equals if performance is shown; single-source package; 2-year warranty from Substantial Completion (¶2.01, p.325; warranty paragraph pp.324–325) [Verified]. Performance: NFPA 37, 70, 99 and NFPA 110 Level 2 EPSS; UL 2200; EPA Tier 3; noise within state and local limits at adjacent property lines (¶2.02, p.325) [Verified]. Sub-base double-wall listed steel tank with fuel for 35 hours at 100% rated power, sound-attenuated lockable aluminum housing (¶2.05 B, p.329; ¶2.08, p.332) — matching E10.3 (plans_12 p.8) [Verified]. Submittals: action, informational, closeout and maintenance-material submittals including fuel consumption at 0.8 pf and plans and elevations (¶1.04–1.08, pp.322–324) [Verified; pages scanned by key terms]. Field quality control and on-site acceptance: resistive load bank at full load with temporary connections, run at least 2 hours per NFPA 110; full fuel tank at final commissioning; at least 4 hours of training for up to 5 people (¶3.06–3.07, p.336; ¶3.09, p.337) [Verified].
