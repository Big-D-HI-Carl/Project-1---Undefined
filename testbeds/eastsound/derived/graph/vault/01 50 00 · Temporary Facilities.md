## Summary

The Contractor arranges all temporary utilities; a field office is encouraged but not required, while water and sanitation must be provided for all workers (¶1.01.A–B, p.142; Verified; field office conflicts with 01 31 00 — Issues 1.16). Required facilities: temporary power and lighting to the closest utility source, metered construction water with backflow prevention if agreed, temporary restrooms, drinking water, protected storage, first aid per WAC 296-24, and access at all times to homes, the WWTP and water treatment plant, and the helicopter pad for emergency vehicles (¶1.04.A–G, I, pp.142–144; Verified). Wastewater bypass systems are required for both influent and effluent; bypass pumping and other temporary process equipment need continuous oversight by on-site staff or an alarm with automatic cellular notification, 24/7 call-out with on-site response within 30 minutes, and backup alarms and running status wired to the District's existing control system, with PLC/SCADA programming incidental (¶1.01.D.2, ¶1.04.H, pp.142–144; Verified). The clean water leakage test repeats 01 45 00 but groups the aeration basins, anoxic basins and clarifier under Train #3 and names UV channels #1 and #2 and the 2W pump station wet well (¶1.04.C, p.143; Verified; Issues 1.22). Cleanup, noise, dust, erosion control per 31 32 11, pollution control and barriers at all hazards close the section (¶1.05–1.08, pp.144–145; Verified; barrier text repeats 00 73 00 SC 23), and temporary items come out at completion of the work (¶1.01.C, ¶1.04.A.4, ¶1.04.B.3, ¶1.04.D.1; removal timing conflicts with 01 70 00 — Issues 1.19).
