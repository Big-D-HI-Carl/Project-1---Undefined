## Summary

Keyed note 7 makes the flow splitter a prefabricated 316 SS unit sized to match the drawing, with 4 equal splitter chambers (flow to each of the 3 trains and 1 additional train), an adjustable weir in each chamber and a removable cover; manufacturer Smith & Loveless or approved equal. The box is about 7'-0" × 4'-6" in plan (outlet spacing 5'-3") with its bottom at elev 26.00 on a proposed concrete support about 6.5' × 4.0' × 1.3' tall; the contractor coordinates the support with the actual box, top of concrete elev 26.00, see structural drawings (keyed note 2); the existing slab TOC is 24.7. The inlet is 6" DI restrained pipe and a restrained 90° bend (INV 20.75) rising through a 12"×6" DI eccentric reducer, 12" DI flange spools and 90° bends on 12" pipe supports per 5/C7.2; outlets are 8" DI flanged bends and spools (one at INV 20.75) on 8" pipe supports per 5/C7.2, labeled To Cell #1, #2 and #3, with a future connection sealed by a blind flange (keyed note 1). A manually adjustable weir is configured to fine-tune an even split among all four outlet pipes.
