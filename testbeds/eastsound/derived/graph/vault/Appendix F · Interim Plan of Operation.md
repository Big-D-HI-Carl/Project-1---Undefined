## Summary

Wilson Engineering's plan of interim operation lists the operational milestones from construction through start-up and states it is not part of the construction Contract Documents (§1.0–1.2; main spec p.614 — Verified). Scope summary: a third bullseye-style Bardenpho treatment train with new clarifier, an aerobic digester, UV disinfection, new dewatering equipment, a new influent flow splitter, new influent and 2W pump stations, a new blower building and stormwater improvements (§2.0; p.615 — Verified). Preliminary schedule: bid opening Feb 8, 2023, notice to proceed Mar 15, 2023, substantial completion July 2024 and final completion October 2024, with a CPM schedule due within 10 days of award and updated monthly per 01 31 00 (§3.0, Table 3-1; p.616 — Verified). Treatment stays continuous: the Contractor provides temporary piping from the existing chlorine disinfection chamber outlet to the 8-in effluent pipe just upstream of SSMH #1288, limits each disruption to 1 hour (influent pump station start-up with the flow splitter, the new 10-in effluent tie-in, the new transformer), lowers Train #2 with temporary pumps for the splitter tie-in, and submits a bypass plan; the detailed sequence is on C0.7 (§5.1–5.2; pp.618–619 — Verified). The Owner must have full use of the new generator before the old generator and switchgear are demolished, and at start-up new concrete basins get clean-water leakage tests and equipment gets operational tests per 01 45 00 and the equipment sections (§5.3.1, §6.0; p.619 — Verified).
