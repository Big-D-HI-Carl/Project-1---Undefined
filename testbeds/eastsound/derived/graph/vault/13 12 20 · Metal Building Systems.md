## Summary

Covers a rigid-frame, bolted, pre-manufactured metal building with galvanized cold-formed secondary members and pre-coated metal roofing and siding, for the Blower Building and the existing building modifications shown on A1.3 [¶1.01 A–B, p.246 — Verified]. Design loads — 5 psf collateral, 25 psf snow, 20 psf roof live, 105 mph ultimate wind (Exposure C), Site Class D with SDS 0.79 / SD1 0.51, SDC D, Ie 1.25, 18" frost — match S1.1, and submittals are WA SE-sealed design calculations and drawings for permit, a WA SE-sealed anchor bolt embedment design by the Contractor, and erection drawings [¶1.03 A, p.246; ¶1.04 A–B, p.247 — Verified; plans_7 p.8 — Verified-Visual]. Garco Building Systems or equal (AISC Category MB certified, MBMA member) supplies 50 ksi hot-dip galvanized primary framing, G-90 16-gauge secondary members, A325 bolts, galvanized cable or rod bracing, 24-gauge Galvalume standing-seam roof panels (UL 90) and 26-gauge Galvalume R- or A-type wall panels, factory-finished in polyester or Kynar at the Owner's choice [¶1.05, ¶2.01–¶2.04, pp.247–249 — Verified; Add. 4 p.1 Clarification 3 — Verified]. ¶2.05 A.1 describes the Blower Building as a rigid frame of "42' bldg. width, 9.75' bldg. length" with 9'-0" minimum haunch clearance, which Add. 4 supersedes with a 44.00' × 9.25' footprint and 10.25' clearance [p.249 — Verified; Add. 4 p.1 Clarification 4, p.4, p.5 — Verified; Add. 4 governs]. Installers need 5 years' experience, anchor bolts and base plates are verified against the approved erection drawings, bolts are tightened by turn-of-nut (preferred), and panels are lapped and butyl-sealed with trim and closures for a weather-tight enclosure; the section has no testing article [¶3.01–¶3.03, pp.250–251 — Verified].
