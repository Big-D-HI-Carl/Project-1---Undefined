## Summary

One concrete structure about 45.00' × 13.00' (44.00' and 12.00' inside dimensions shown) holds the UV disinfection chamber (UV Channels #1 and #2), an effluent sample chamber ("effluent sample location") and the aerobic digester; section cuts A–D refer to C5.2 and C5.3. An 8" inlet splits to the two channels through 8" gate valves with hand wheels (keyed note 5); each channel holds a bank of UV modules with 1" drains, work areas sloped 1% minimum, rim EL 18.74 and TOC 18.83, with two wall-mounted control panels, aluminum access ladders (nominal 2' wide, keyed note 4) and a 4" UV drain with 4" gate valves (keyed note 3). The digester (TOC 15.3 down to 14.3; floor slopes 2.4%, 3.1% and 8.3%; 6" lip all around) has 3" and 4" air lines, an HDG I-beam ("see structural"), a 4" T-valve on a 4" supernatant drain pipe, a 2" SCH 80 PVC digested sludge outlet, a 3" WAS line, a 4" drain and wall-mount pipe supports per 7/C7.2 (keyed note 6); link-seal boots seal all pipe-to-concrete connections (keyed note 1). The grating & guardrail plan shows guardrail with removable guard chains (4 locations) around the UV chamber, a removable guard chain with a 2'-8" opening at the effluent sample chamber, and removable grating over the aerobic digester. 01 sets this sheet to Text, but its text layer holds only UV module labels (LAMP/STATUS/TOP/BOTTOM) and view titles, so the plan content above was read from the image.
