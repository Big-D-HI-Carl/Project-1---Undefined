## Summary

The drawing is titled "Main PLC and Influent Control Panel — Power Wiring Diagram (Typ)", so it serves both the main PLC panel and the Influent Pump Station panel; the operator interface and blower PTC relays are marked "main PLC only" and the 800 W panel heater "influent only" (plans_10 p.7) [Verified-Visual; title partly in text]. 120 VAC side: power from the lighting panel through a 20 A breaker and TVSS, 5 A branches for the door switch and enclosure light(s), a GFCI duplex programmer receptacle and a GFCI general receptacle, 1 A branches CBA4–8 for five motor PTC relay modules, the panel heater and six spares; final 120 VAC quantity and sizing per the system integrator (plans_10 p.7) [Verified-Visual]. 24 VDC side: 10 A 24 VDC power supply on a 20 A breaker, DC-UPS controller with a 12 VDC 26 AH battery assembly on a 30 A breaker and power-normal and alarm contacts to PLC inputs, 5 A breakers for the PLC power supply, operator interface and network switch, and 2 A fuses per card group for digital inputs, analog inputs, digital outputs, analog outputs and 24 VDC field power (plans_10 p.7) [Verified; fuse layout Verified-Visual]. Key note 1: the blower supplier provides a PTC relay module for installation in the main PLC panel; the system integrator installs it, provides a 120 V circuit to each and wires the overtemperature signal to the PLC to shut the blower motor down on high temperature (plans_10 p.7) [Verified].
