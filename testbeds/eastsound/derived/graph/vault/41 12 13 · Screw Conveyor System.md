## Summary

One complete screw conveyor system carries dewatered cake "from the rotary presses" to the final discharge point, with drives, motors, instrumentation, controls and supports, designed by the manufacturer to CEMA, AGMA, AWS D1.1 and AISC standards (¶1.1–1.2, ¶1.5 pp.453–455). The schedule (pp.460–461) lists two units: a "Receiving Conveyor" (horizontal, shaftless, 9" flight at 9" pitch, 6'-9" long, 304 SS U-trough 11 ga, two inlets from rotary press cake chutes, outlet to the inclined conveyor, Nord SK2282 SCP, 1 HP, 24 rpm) and an "Inclined Conveyor" (shafted, 9" flight at 5" pitch, 17'-4" long at 35°, 304 SS tubular O-trough 10 ga, outlet to bin, Nord SK2282 SCP, 5 HP, 53 rpm), both 0.5 wet ton/hr at 45% loading and 45 lb/ft³, with 3/8" UHMW liners and 2" drains. Troughs follow CEMA 300 in sections of 8 ft max, with UHMW wear liners in 4-ft pieces, a 2" stainless ball drain valve, anti-flotation devices every 16 ft on shaftless units and saddle supports at 16 ft max; drives are TEFC inverter-duty 1800 rpm motors on Nord shaft-mount reducers (AGMA Class 11, 1.4 service factor), with Siemens Sitrans WM100 zero-speed switches (120 VAC, NEMA 4X) wired to the PLC and interlocked with the MCC starter, and the conveyors run from the rotary press PLC and operator interface (¶1.8–1.13 pp.456–459). Submittals cite "Section 01340" and "Section 01 30 00" and repeat telescoping valve wording (¶1.3 A p.454; Issue 23), plus data sheets and component lists (¶1.3 B); each conveyor is shop assembled and run before shipment (¶3.02 A p.460). The two-conveyor, two-inlet arrangement does not match C6.1's single 28° conveyor from one press (Issue 22); Part 2 numbering runs 1.6–1.13 as printed.
