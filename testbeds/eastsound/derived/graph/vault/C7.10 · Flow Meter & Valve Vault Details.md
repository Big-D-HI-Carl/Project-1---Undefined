## Summary

Plan and elevation of the Train 2 (Detail 1) and Train 3 (Detail 2) flow meter vaults, each a precast concrete vault 4'-2" x 4'-2" x 5'-0" minimum inside (Utility Vault 506-LA or equal) with a 36 x 36-in H-20 single-leaf hatch (Halliday H1R3636) opening south and a Bilco LadderUp (plans_7 p.4, C7.10 — Verified; details Verified-Visual). Each vault houses an 8-in flow meter with an 8-in flanged coupling adapter with restraints, with 8-in plug valves in valve cans upstream and downstream; the pipe is labeled 8-in DI force main in plan and 8-in DI gravity on the elevation (plans_7 p.4, C7.10 — Verified-Visual; see Issues). Pipe inverts are 20.72 (Train 2) and 20.68 (Train 3) over a nominal floor at 19.0 with 1% grout slope to a floor drain (2/C7.1) that drains to the influent pump station; pipe supports follow 5/C7.2 with 18 in minimum clearance (plans_7 p.4, C7.10 — Verified-Visual). Pipe penetrations get Link-Seal boots, steps or ladder must be non-corrosive per WSDOT Standard Plan B-30.90-02 (galvanized not accepted), and the base is 12 in of Class A foundation gravel per Standard Specifications 9-03.12(1)A (plans_7 p.4, C7.10 — Verified-Visual; WSDOT parts Unresolved: external reference, not staged). Add. 4 Clarification 2 requires the exterior of all precast vault joints to be sealed with CANUSA-CPS WrapidSeal or approved equal — Add. 4 governs (Add. 4 p.1, Clarification 2 — Verified).
