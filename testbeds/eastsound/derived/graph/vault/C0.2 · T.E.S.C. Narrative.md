## Summary

Construction stormwater narrative organized by the 13 required elements (clearing limits, construction access, flow rates, sediment controls, soil stabilization, slopes, drain inlets, channels and outlets, pollutants, dewatering, BMP maintenance, project management, low-impact-development BMPs) plus general notes on BMP installation, maintenance and removal (plans_2Part-1 p.1, C0.2 — Verified-Visual). Project description: total project area 0.45 ac, proposed impervious area 0.04 ac, area to be disturbed 0.30 ac, estimated cut 3,382 CY and fill 1,596 CY, stated as for the TESC narrative only and not for bidding or take-off (Item 15; plans_2Part-1 p.1, C0.2 — Verified-Visual). The site is mostly flat, with grass turf along the north and south property lines and no trees or shrubs; runoff flows southeast to northwest into a ditch on the adjoining site along the north boundary; there are no streams, lakes, wetlands, residential or critical areas; soils are mapped as Roche-Killebrew complex, 2 to 10 percent slopes (Items 16–21; plans_2Part-1 p.1, C0.2 — Verified-Visual). Sequence: install silt fence, construction entrance and inlet protection; remove pavement for sewer structures; clear and grade for the building addition and retaining wall; build them; install stormwater conveyance; pave and stabilize landscape areas (Item 22; plans_2Part-1 p.1, C0.2 — Verified-Visual). Construction is expected from spring 2023 to fall 2024; no sediment ponds, diversions or waterways are used and no detention calculations are needed (Items 23–24; plans_2Part-1 p.1, C0.2 — Verified-Visual).
