## Summary

Aluminum handrails (two horizontal bars) and guardrails (6" sphere rule) are provided where shown, with height, picket spacing and toe plates detailed on the Civil Drawings, to OSHA and IBC 2018 [¶1.01 A–B, p.203 — Verified]. Submittals are shop drawings with a dimensional layout, the manufacturer's assembly and installation instructions, and calculations sealed by a Washington State registered structural engineer; partial or unsealed submittals are rejected [¶1.02 A–B, p.203 — Verified]. Rails, posts and stanchions are 1-1/2" IPS Schedule 40 aluminum with a clear satin anodized Class I finish (0.7 mil min.) from Golden Railing, CRL or Ametco or equal, with 3/16" min. aluminum toeboards on all guardrails [Part 2 A–B, p.203 — Verified]. Connections to structure use Type 316 stainless bolts, posts and rails get weep holes, removable sections mount independently, aluminum touching concrete gets bituminous paint, and embedded posts are grouted into blockouts after the concrete reaches design strength [¶3.01 B–D, ¶3.02 A–B, p.204 — Verified].
