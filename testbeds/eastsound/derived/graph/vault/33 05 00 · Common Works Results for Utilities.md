## Summary

Supplements the drawings and WSDOT Standard Specifications for furnishing and installing all utility pipe and fittings, including trenching, over-excavation, bedding, backfill and trench dewatering; pipe must be new and sized as inside diameter unless it is a nominal-size product (¶1.01, ¶1.03; main spec p.413 — Verified; WSDOT Unresolved: external reference, not staged). Submittals: field-fabrication data, material certifications, and verification of every existing pipe to be connected (exposed and checked for size, material, elevation, location and service) (¶1.04; p.414 — Verified). Bolts and nuts are Type 316 stainless, thrust-block and tie concrete is 3,000 psi, and thrust restraint is not shown on the drawings and is incidental (¶2.01, ¶2.05, ¶3.018–3.020; pp.414–417 — Verified). Work near existing electrical and water services needs 3 business days' coordination with OPALCO, the Eastsound Water Users Association, the District and the Engineer (¶3.03; p.415 — Verified). Buried valves on ductile iron or nonmetallic lines get bituminous-coated nuts and bolts and an 8-mil polyethylene wrap; the section cites 'Section 01 30 00', which is not in the manual (¶3.025 B, ¶1.06 A.1; pp.414, 419 — Verified; see Issues).
