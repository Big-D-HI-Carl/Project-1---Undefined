## Summary

Covers thermal insulation and vapor barrier in exterior roof and wall construction, installed by a metal building insulation installer with 3 years' experience; materials arrive with instructions and typical shop drawings, and the section has no separate submittal article [¶1.1 A, ¶1.2 A–B, p.209 — Verified]. The system is the Thermal Design Simple Saver interior finish and insulation system or equal — steel straps, woven reinforced polyethylene liner fabric with welded seams, sealants and tapes — with exposed parts at flame spread 25 or less and smoke developed 50 or less (ASTM E84) [¶1.2 C, ¶2.1 A–B, p.209 — Verified]. Insulation is unfaced fiberglass batt to the in-place R-value noted on the drawings (ASTM C518) with 1/8" × 3" XPS thermal blocks, and the vapor barrier goes on the interior side, continuous and taped at all joints and penetrations [¶2.2 A–B, p.209; ¶3.1 A–C, p.210 — Verified]; A1.1 sets R-25 walls and R-30 roof on furring with GWB walls, an assembly that differs from the liner system [plans_7 p.5 — Verified-Visual; Unresolved — Issues, Conflicts]. The section does not cover the R-10 XPS footing-perimeter insulation or the Dryvit EIFS shown on A1.2, and 02 lists no other Division 07 section except 07 92 00 [¶1.1 A, p.209; plans_7 p.6 — Verified-Visual; 02 index — Verified; Unresolved — Issues, Missing information].
