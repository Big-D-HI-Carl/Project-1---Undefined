## Summary

Two Allen-Bradley 1769-IQ16 16-point input cards, all 32 channels used: influent pumps 1–4 (hand, auto, running, fault, seal fail, overtemperature — six each), wet well floats F1–F4 through relays ISR-F1B to ISR-F4B, ventilation fans 1–3 "called to run" from the ventilation control panel, and phase fail (plans_11 p.7) [Verified-Visual]. General note 2 calls for added I/O cards to give at least 20% spare inputs, not shown; with 32 of 32 inputs used that means at least 7 more inputs, a third card (plans_11 p.7) [Verified-Visual; card count Inferred by arithmetic]. Each pump input block is labeled "INFLUENT PUMP n: MCC", and pump 2 shows "VFD FAULT" with no "auto selected" input, unlike pumps 1, 3 and 4 — see Issues (plans_11 p.7) [Verified-Visual]. High-level float control circuit (hardwired backup): each wet well high-level float passes an intrinsic safety barrier (relays ISR-FnA/FnB), lights a "high float activated" panel light, has a test button, and "starts pump n" through relay CRHn with a time-off-delay relay TDRn, 0–60 s; key note 1 sets true-off delay, field-adjusted to wet well pump-down rate (plans_11 p.7) [Verified-Visual; key note Verified]. Float n is wired to pump n (F1 to 3 HP pump 1), while the E4.2 level table assigns F1 and F2 to the large pumps — see Issues (plans_11 p.7; plans_9 p.7) [Verified-Visual; large-pump reading Inferred from horsepower].
