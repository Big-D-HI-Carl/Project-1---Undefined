## Summary

Covers preparation and application of paint coatings, but ¶1.01 A sends the new Blower Building and masonry, concrete and stucco painting to a "09 90 00 – Paints and Coatings" section that exists in neither the main spec nor 02 [¶1.01 A, p.229 — Verified; Unresolved — Issues, Missing information]. Submittals are product data and procedures per service condition, color charts 30 days and a painting schedule 14 days before work; the Contractor measures wet-film thickness of every coat, the Engineer approves each coat, and hold points follow surface preparation and final coating [¶1.03 A–C, ¶1.02 E, p.229; ¶3.03 D, p.234; ¶3.06, p.235 — Verified]. Service conditions are A epoxy (Tnemec 161 or SW Macropoxy 646) on exposed metal piping, valves, supports and misc. ferrous metal but not galvanized, aluminum or stainless; B interior wood; C metal doors and frames (two coats SW Industrial Enamel, DF 4.0, over factory primer); D PVC piping; E exterior wood stain; and H non-skid epoxy at the headworks floor [¶2.01 I.1–6, pp.231–232 — Verified]. Add. 4 adds Service Condition J for the new Blower Building gypsum board: one coat Sherwin-Williams wall primer (DF 2.0) and two coats Sherwin-Williams Pro-Mar alkyd egg-shell (DF 4.0) [Add. 4 p.2, 09 90 00 ¶2.01 I — Verified; Add. 4 governs]. Execution requires concrete cured 28 days before painting, galvanized surfaces passivated before priming, and exposed piping painted and labeled by service (for example, 2W light blue, air white) with a labeling walk-through before Substantial Completion [¶3.02 G and L, pp.233–234; ¶3.05, p.235 — Verified].
