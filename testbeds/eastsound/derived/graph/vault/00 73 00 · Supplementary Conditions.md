## Summary

Thirty-five Supplemental Conditions modify the WSDOT Standard Specifications (2022 Ed.), which serve as the base conditions (Unresolved: external reference, not staged); precedence runs Change Orders, Contract Form, Addenda, permits, Drawings, Supplemental Conditions, Technical Specifications, District standards, WSDOT drawings, WSDOT specs (SC 1–2, 5, pp.88–89; Verified); the 00 53 00 and 00 54 00 funding inserts are not placed in that order (Issues 2.7). The Contractor stakes the work, picks up the District Land Disturbance permit and San Juan County Building Permit #21-0385 (stated as still in process), gives 48-hour utility and connection notices, works 7AM–5PM Mon–Fri with $150/hr inspection charge past 40 hr/week, and self-performs at least 50% of the contract price (SC 7–14, pp.89–91; Verified; permit status conflicts with 00 31 13 — Issues 1.9). SC 15 sets completion by Oct. 16, 2024 within 360 working days on site; SC 16 requires the CPM schedule at the pre-construction meeting and the Schedule of Values within 10 working days of NTP; SC 17 holds 5% retainage; SC 18 sets liquidated damages per working day as LD = 0.15C/T per WSDOT 1-08.9 (pp.91–92; Verified; WSDOT part Unresolved: external reference, not staged; basis conflicts with 00 41 00 — Issues 1.8). SC 19 defines Substantial Completion as the new facility in beneficial use (influent pump station, flow splitter, Train 3, digester, dewatering, blower building, UV, yard piping, electrical, instruments, controls, backup generator, SCADA/PLC programming) and Final Completion as including removal of temporary pump/process equipment, site work, paving, performance testing and cleanup (p.92; Verified). Red-line record drawings before 90% payment, temporary barriers at all hazards, insurance ($1M/$2M bodily injury, $1M property damage, builders risk equal to the bid, Owner and Engineer additional insured), one-year guarantee (start date conflicts — Issues 1.11), and funding clauses follow (SC 20–35, pp.92–97; Verified); pp.98–121 repeat the 00 53 00 Ecology insert word for word (Issues 1.13).
