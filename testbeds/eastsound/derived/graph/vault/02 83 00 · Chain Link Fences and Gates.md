## Summary

All chain-link fence and gates are 6-ft galvanized fabric with 3-strand barbed wire on top (7 ft total), with bottom rails to stop access under the fabric (¶1.01 A; main spec p.163 — Verified). Submittals: three samples (fabric, post section, accessories) and shop drawings showing height, fabric, post and gate locations and sizes, footings and hardware (¶1.04; p.163 — Verified). Materials: ASTM A392 No. 9 gage 2-in mesh Class II galvanized fabric, Sch. 40 posts (2.375-in line, 2.875-in end/corner/pull, 4-in or 6-5/8-in gate posts by leaf width), 1.66-in rails and braces, swing or non-motorized rolling gates with locking latches (¶2.01–2.09; pp.164–166 — Verified). Line posts at 10 ft maximum, concrete-set, with fabric hung no sooner than 5 days after posts are set; fence under or within 50 ft of a primary power line is grounded (¶3.04, ¶3.06, ¶3.08; pp.167–168 — Verified). Cross-references use older numbers: 'Section 05500' and 'Section 03300' (¶1.02 A, ¶2.10 A; pp.163, 166 — Verified; see Issues).
