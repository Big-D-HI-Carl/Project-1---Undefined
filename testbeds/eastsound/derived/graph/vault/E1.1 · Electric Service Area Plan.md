## Summary

New 480Y/277V service: OPALCO furnishes and installs a new 225 kVA 480Y/277V transformer and the primary on the existing pad, which the contractor modifies per OPALCO; the contractor provides secondary conduit and wire (P-SEC) and a new CT enclosure, landing pads and meter base, with meter and CTs by the utility (key notes 1–2; plans_9 p.2) [Verified; 225 kVA and P-SEC Verified-Visual]. Equipment: 480Y/277V 400 A service-entrance automatic transfer switch ATS, 480Y/277V 400 A panel MDP, 75 kVA 480–208Y/120V transformer TS feeding the existing 208Y/120V 400 A panel, and a new 480Y/277V 125 kW diesel generator GEN on a "concrete generator pad per civil" (detail 1/E10.3); conduits P-ATS, C-ATS, P-GEN, C-GEN, P-AUX, P-MDP, P-MCC, P-TSP, P-TSS (plan; plans_9 p.2) [Verified-Visual; tags also in text]. Key notes 3–6: a J-box combining ATS and generator status signals wired to the PLC control panel in the Blower Building; a 120 V 20 A circuit from the load center in the MCC to the generator auxiliaries (battery charger, coolant heater); concrete and wall repair for new conduit entries; replacement of the existing 200 A 208Y/120V feeder from the existing switchgear to Panel TPS (plans_9 p.2) [Verified]. Grounding notes G1–G4: utility transformer grounding per NEC and OPALCO; bond the concrete-encased generator pad rebar to the generator and the grounding electrode system; ground "the transformer" as a separately derived system (the 75 kVA TS by context — Inferred); install new grounding electrode systems tied to the existing building system (plans_9 p.2) [Verified].
