## Summary

Wet well plan and elevation: IP-1 and IP-2 (motors marked 3) and IP-3 and IP-4 (marked 5), four float switches F1–F4 and a submersible level transducer LT on a stainless hanger with Kellems cord grips (instrumentation support detail 2), cables routed to handholes (plans_9 p.7) [Verified-Visual; IP-3/IP-4 and detail labels also in text]. Key notes: WIKA submersible pressure transmitter (316 SS, 0–15 psi, LevelGuard, polyurethane cable, 4–20 mA loop powered, vented, NEMA 4X termination enclosure); Eco-Float GP60NONC non-mercury NO/NC high-level floats; service loops in the wet well, connections in the handholes with approved connectors, conduits leaving the handholes sealed per NEC Article 500; one oversized conduit per cable (plans_9 p.7) [Verified]. Hazardous area connection detail 1: precast traffic-rated handholes with solid bottom, sump and drain sized per NEC 314.28(A) and 314.30(A), explosion-proof junction boxes where equipment cables meet panel wiring, and conduit seals at the classified boundary (plans_9 p.7) [Verified]. Level control table (ft): redundant large lag on 19.00 (F1, timer), redundant large lead on 18.50 (F2), redundant small lag on 18.00 (F3), redundant small lead on 17.50 (F4), high level alarm 17.50 (transducer and float), large lag on 17.00, large lead on 16.00, small lag on 14.00, small lead on 12.00, pumps off 11.00, low level alarm and redundant off 10.50 — the table does not say which tags are the large pair; by E6.1 horsepower the 5 HP units IP-3/IP-4 are the large pumps (plans_9 p.7; plans_10 p.2) [Verified-Visual; large-pair assignment Inferred]. General notes number "4." twice (cosmetic) and repeat that handholes are required but not shown, with final locations coordinated with the Engineer (plans_9 p.7) [Verified].
