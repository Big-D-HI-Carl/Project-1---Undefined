## Summary

The work is all labor, equipment and materials for the WWTP improvements and miscellaneous site improvements — in general, expansion of the existing plant — including demolition, shoring, bracing, sheeting, cribbing, falsework, pumping, dewatering, drainage and forms, except items pre-purchased or supplied by the Owner; 00 24 13 holds the detailed scope (¶1.01, p.123; Verified). No Owner-furnished items are listed in this section (p.123; Verified absence). Sequence is the Contractor's option, with the preliminary phasing in 00 31 13, Owner approval of stockpile areas, and staging coordinated with the District (¶1.03, ¶1.05, pp.123–124; Verified). Suppliers are responsible for complete, compatible installations including missing connections; design is based on the first-named manufacturer and substitutes carry their own redesign cost; gravity drainage has right-of-way over mechanical and electrical, and electrical over landscaping (¶1.04, p.124; Verified). Excavation follows RCW 19.122 with 811 notice 48 hours ahead and Engineer notice 48 hours before starting, plus access, storage and restoration duties (¶1.06–1.07, p.125; Verified).
