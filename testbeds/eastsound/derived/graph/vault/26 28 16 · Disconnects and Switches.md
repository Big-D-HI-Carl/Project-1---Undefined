## Summary

Fused and unfused disconnects required by code for equipment under this and other divisions; NEC, OSHA and NRTL listing (¶1.01–1.02, p.314) [Verified]. Heavy-duty, horsepower-rated, quick-make quick-break switches opening all ungrounded conductors; NEMA-rated enclosures per the 26 05 00 area rules; fusible units with Class R clips only where the equipment manufacturer requires; Cutler Hammer, Square D, Westinghouse or equal (¶2.01–2.02, p.314) [Verified]. Mounting on corrosion-resistant Unistrut stands or walls with 1 in spacers off concrete or block, arranged by driven equipment, handle center at 4 ft 6 in (¶3.01, pp.314–315) [Verified]. Submittals: catalog data per 26 05 00 ¶1.012 (¶1.03, p.314); no test paragraph (the only "test" term is the NRTL listing) [Verified].
