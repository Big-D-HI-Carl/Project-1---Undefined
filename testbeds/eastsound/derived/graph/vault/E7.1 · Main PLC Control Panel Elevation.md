## Summary

Main PLC control panel exterior: NEMA 12 painted steel enclosure with an Allen-Bradley PanelView Plus operator interface on the door, set on a concrete housekeeping pad "per civil specs" (plans_10 p.6) [Verified]. Backpanel: network switch; CompactLogix PLC and I/O "per drawings"; terminals, fuses and power distribution for PLC I/O; DC power supply and UPS with a DC UPS battery pack; line filter and power distribution; output relays; GFCI receptacle (plans_10 p.6) [Verified-Visual; labels also in text]. The backpanel also carries five blower motor protective relays "supplied by blower manufacturer" and an intrinsically safe area with a barrier for a level transducer; E7.5 puts the barrier on the digester level loop (plans_10 p.6; plans_11 p.2) [Verified-Visual]. General note 1: the system integrator provides the detailed panel design and documentation for a complete and operational system; only major components are specified (plans_10 p.6) [Verified].
