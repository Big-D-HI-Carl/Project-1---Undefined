## Summary

Furnish and install one UV transmissivity analyzer, Hach Model DR 1900 (¶1.01 A p.509). ¶2.01 A, under the heading "UV ANALYZER", reads "The pumps shall be Hach Model DR1900" (Issue 34); standard replacement parts come with the unit (¶2.02). Submittals per 01 33 00 are catalog data with wiring diagrams and O&M information (¶1.02 A); the contractor gives a 1-year parts-and-labor extended warranty (¶1.03 A); and the manufacturer provides installation certification, 1 day of start-up and field testing and 1 day of operator training (¶3.01). No location is given in the section or on the P&M sheets.
