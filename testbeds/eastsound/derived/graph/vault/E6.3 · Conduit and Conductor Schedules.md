## Summary

Power schedule: 40 named runs plus two unnamed 120 V UV controller circuits and an AC spare with pull string, each with voltage, conduit, conductor count and size, ground, from and to — e.g., P-SEC and P-ATS (2) 2-in 4 ea #3/0, P-GEN 3-in 4 #250 kcmil + #4 G, P-MCC (2) 2-in 4 ea #3/0 + #3 G, P-TSS (2) 2-in 4 ea #3/0 + #3 G (plans_10 p.4) [Verified-Visual; text layer carries titles only]. Control and signal schedule: 38 named 24 VDC runs plus a DC spare — C-ATS, C-GEN, C-2W, C-TCP3, C-DO1, C-DO2, C-MCP, C-INT, C-2WPT, C-SD1, C-DB, C-FME, C-UV, C-BL1 to C-BL4; S-LAB, S-LT-D, S-ICP, S-MCP, S-FLT1 to S-FLT4, S-LT-IP, S-DW1; factory coil and electrode cables S-FME, S-FMIN, S-FMPD, S-FMP2, S-FMP3; transmitter runs S-FMDW and S-FM2W; flow-pace runs S-FP1 to S-FP4 (plans_10 p.4) [Verified-Visual]. Closes batch 1 open items: P-TS3A (10 #14) feeds Train No.3 field devices and P-TS3B (4 #12) the Train No.3 pole light and receptacle; C-DO2 serves "DO transmitter No.2"; the Blower Building 2W transducer is called "2W pressure sensor" (plans_10 p.4) [Verified-Visual]. Disagreements with other sheets: P-IP1 to P-IP4 run "from MCC" while E6.1 feeds the influent pumps from the IPS panel; P-2W is one 2-in conduit with (2) 3-conductor cables to both 2W pumps while E6.1 shows P-2W-P1 and P-2W-P2; P-LT comes from an unnamed "lighting panel"; ID spellings differ (P-BL-1/P-BL1, P-SD-1/P-SD1, S-DW1/S-DW-1, S-FM2W/S-2WFM) — cosmetic (plans_10 p.4 vs plans_9 pp.3, 6, 8 and plans_10 p.2) [Verified-Visual]. Existing equipment is named differently from E0.2/E6.1: P-TSS ends at the "existing 208Y/120V 400A shop panel" and P-TPS starts at the "existing 208Y/120V shop SWBD" — both are the Office Building 400 A I-Line panel on the E6.1 one-line (plans_10 pp.2, 4) [Verified-Visual; identity Inferred].
