## Summary

One Allen-Bradley 1769-IF8 8-channel analog input card, differential current wiring, 24 VDC loop power through 0.25 A fused terminals (plans_12 p.1) [Verified]. Channel 0: influent wet well level transducer, "0-15 FT", through an intrinsic safety barrier and a panel level indicator; channel 1: hydrogen sulfide sensor (ppm); channel 2: methane gas sensor (ppm); channels 3–7 spare (plans_12 p.1) [Verified-Visual; device labels are not in the text layer]. The wet well range (0–15 ft) differs from the E4.2 transducer (0–15 psi) — see Issues (plans_12 p.1; plans_9 p.7) [Verified-Visual]. The gas sensors land on the IPS PLC, which drives the alarm lights and horn (E8.4 outputs 7–10) (plans_12 p.1; plans_11 p.8) [Verified-Visual].
