## Summary

Furnish and install three sludge pumps — one WAS pump, one digested sludge pump and one swing pump for either service — for dilute sludge at 0.5% to 3.0% solids (¶1.01 A–B p.263). Base ¶2.01 A (p.263) calls for Edson Platinum peristaltic pumps or equal, 3 HP, 460 V, 3-phase, 0 to 43 gpm (typical set point 10 to 30 gpm), corrosion resistant, with a VFD and a local speed dial per pump, an electrical control panel per pump and a 304 SS enclosure of about 40"×40"×40" per pump. Add. 4 p.2 replaces "Each pump is to include a VFD" with: pumps powered from VFDs in the Motor Control Center, VFDs controlled by the PLC in the Main Control Panel, no local speed control dial at the pump, see Electrical Sheets — Add. 4 governs. Controls follow 26 80 00 ¶3.09 A (WAS pump control) and H (rotary fan press system) (¶2.02 p.263); submittals per 01 33 00 are catalog data with wiring diagrams and O&M information (¶1.02 A p.263); the manufacturer furnishes three sets of wear parts (¶2.03 p.263) and provides installation certification, 2 days of start-up and field testing and 1 day of operator training (¶3.02 p.264).
