## Summary

Scope: 600 V conductors and cables for power, lighting, receptacle, signal and control; scheduled circuits per the drawings and schedules; lighting and receptacle circuits sized by the contractor to hold voltage drop to 3% (¶1.01, ¶2.01, p.282) [Verified]. Products: copper, stranded (12 AWG solid allowed for lighting and receptacles), 12 AWG minimum branch size; THHN/THWN for branch and control circuits and small power conductors, XHHW-2 for power conductors 6 AWG and larger; Okonite, Southwire or equal; compression connectors 8 AWG and larger; cast epoxy splices underground or in damp or corrosive locations (¶2.02–2.05, pp.282–283) [Verified]. Execution: shielded power cable from VFDs to motors where indicated; signal circuits in individually shielded twisted pairs or triads without splices, shields bonded at the control panel only; color code 480Y/277V brown-orange-yellow with gray neutral, 208Y/120V black-red-blue with white neutral, green ground, externally powered control wiring yellow (¶3.02 H, ¶3.03, ¶3.04, pp.285–286) [Verified]. Tests (¶3.07, pp.286–288): insulation resistance at 1,000 VDC for 30 s with 1 megohm minimum at 20 °C on scheduled power feeders and on service and feeder conductors; continuity; thermal imaging of all power terminations; ground resistance at each new rod, 25 ohms maximum per rod by the three-rod method and 15 ohms maximum composite; reports to the Engineer before final acceptance [Verified]. No product submittal paragraph in the section, read in full (pp.282–288) [Verified].
