## Summary

Plan of the new flow splitter box beside existing Train #1, with 8" outlets to Train #1 (west), Train #2 and Train #3 (south), a future Train #4 connection sealed with an 8" blind flange (keyed note 1), and the inlet from the north; dashed vaults with flow meter symbols sit on the inlet line and on the Train #2 and Train #3 lines, and a vault drain is labeled. Keyed notes: proposed concrete support for the splitter box (2); existing sampling port (3); pipe supports per 5/C7.2 (4); carefully cut a hole through the building for the new 8" Train #1 influent and seal with flashing (5); 8" Train #1 influent elbow discharging into the aeration basin beside the existing influent pipe (7); confirm the existing Train #2 influent pipe and connect new 8" DI piping with positive drainage from the splitter box (8); pot-hole existing 8" piping before construction (9). Construction sequence: install the new splitter box and piping to Trains #1 and #3 without disturbing existing piping and operations; when complete and tested, connect the 8" influent to the new influent pump station and direct flow to Trains #1 and #3; then dewater, abandon and remove existing influent piping conflicts so the Train #2 piping and flow meter vault can be installed. Train #2 downtime is limited to 5 days max (Monday–Friday).
