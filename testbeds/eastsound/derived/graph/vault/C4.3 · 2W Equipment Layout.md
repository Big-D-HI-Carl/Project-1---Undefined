## Summary

Two air diaphragm tanks, 119 gallons each, "model to be as described in specifications" and configured for the Owner's desired operating pressure range, stand side by side against a wall on a floor at FF 23.30' (TOC 23.30'). Their 1" SCH 80 PVC manifold on 4 floor pipe supports carries pressure transducer PIT-620 (labeled "EQ# PIT-620"; 0–150 psi per specifications; operating pressure 55–75 psi), a 0–150 psi pressure gage with stainless accessories, a pressure relief valve rated 120 psi or greater (Clayton Mark or equal), a 5/8" faucet for air relief and sampling, Simmons S1704 tank tees or equal, 1" ball valves and a pressure-rated end plug. Notes: fill all piping with clean domestic potable water before starting and testing the 2W system equipment; the contractor coordinates the piping connection style (grooved, flanged, threaded) and performs pressure testing to demonstrate watertight construction; valve handles and hoist chains are not shown. The sheet does not name the room; Add. 4 p.3 (A1.1, A1.2, C6.4 item) adjusts "Bladder Tanks" with the Blower Building layout, taken here to be these tanks (Inferred; checked against C6.4 in batch 4).
