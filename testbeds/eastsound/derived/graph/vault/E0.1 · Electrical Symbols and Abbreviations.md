## Summary

Legend of power, control and instrument symbols (selector switches, relays, starters, field instruments, meters) plus a standard abbreviation list, including "E or (E) = existing device", MCP = main control panel, LCP = local control panel and MTS = manual transfer switch (E0.1 legend and abbreviations; plans_8 p.6) [Verified]. Project general notes 1–2: the electrical drawings and schedules are functional and do not fix exact locations or terminations; packaged systems need contractor–manufacturer coordination to deliver a complete and operational system, and minor changes in locations, terminations, boxes and conduit are in the contract price (plans_8 p.6) [Verified]. Notes 3–6: coordinate removal of existing equipment and phasing with the Owner to keep the facility operating, dispose of demo material the Owner does not want, conceal conduit in new walls and ceilings where possible, and hand over redline markups (routing, conductor counts, panel schedules, receptacle configurations) for record drawings (plans_8 p.6) [Verified]. Note 7: all conductors copper; terminations rated 60 °C at 100 A and below and 75 °C above 100 A, citing NEC Article 110-14C as printed (plans_8 p.6) [Verified]. The electrical sheet index lists E0.1–E10.3 (set pp.62–96); its E3.1 and E5.1 titles match the title blocks, while its E0.4, E1.1 and E8.2 titles match the G0.1 cover-index wording — new evidence for 01 title log items 1, 3, 4, 5 and 6 (E0.1 sheet index; plans_8 p.6) [Verified].
