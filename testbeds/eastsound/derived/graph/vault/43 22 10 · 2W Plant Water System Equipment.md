## Summary

A complete 2W plant water system: two 2W pumps, pump station accessories, one fiberglass enclosure with a flow meter system, one fiberglass enclosure with an air gap system, two air diaphragm tanks, yard hydrants, wall-mount eye wash stations, and the piping and valving shown (¶1.01 A–B p.472). Base ¶2.01 A (p.472): two ITT Goulds 55GS50 4"-well submersible pumps with stainless components and Centripro M50434 5 HP 460 V 3-phase motors (or equal), 20–80 gpm, "Pumps are to include variable frequency drives (VFDs)", holding a constant user-set pressure (about 50–75 psi) and alternating after each on/off cycle; Add. 4 p.2 replaces the VFD sentence with VFDs in the Motor Control Center controlled by the PLC in the Main Control Panel — Add. 4 governs. Accessories are Orenco SBEX splice boxes, SBCS conduit seals, an MF2 float switch assembly with MFCOLLAR1 collars, HV discharge assemblies (2" with ball valve, check valve, flex hose and quick disconnect per pump), a PB30 pump basin and pressure relief valves each set at 110 psi (Clayton Mark or equal) (¶2.02 A p.473); the tanks are Amtrol Well-X-Trol WX-350D, 119 gal (26" × 62"), with relief valves at 100 psi on the tank tee and 120 psi on the pump discharge piping, a further 120 psi relief valve, a pressure transducer for pump on/off and a 0–150 psi gauge (¶2.03 A p.473; Issue 26). Exposed plant water pipe gets 1.25" closed-cell polyisocyanurate insulation (Trymer 2000 XP or equal) under a 26-gauge aluminum jacket, and exposed plant water piping, hydrants and pump discharge piping are labeled and painted per "Section 09 91 00" and Divisions 9 and 33, stainless pipe excepted (¶2.04–2.05 pp.473–474; Issue 27). Submittals per 01 33 00 are catalog data, fabrication and installation drawings and O&M information (¶1.02 A p.472); one set of special tools is furnished (¶1.03 A); installation includes verifying pump rotation, and the suppliers train the Owner (¶3.01–3.02 p.475).
