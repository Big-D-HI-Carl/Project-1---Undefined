## Summary

A single-source, shop-tested "plug and play" dewatering skid: one rotary fan press with one 36" dewatering channel, a flocculator, sludge manifold and piping, cake chute, an emulsified-polymer make-up system with dosing pump, an air compressor for pneumatic valving, screw conveyors (receiving and inclined "as needed" to reach the District's dumpster, as shown on drawings) and one control panel for the whole system, which also sets the digested sludge feed pump rate (¶1.0 A, C p.510; ¶1.7 p.514). Duty: at least 120 dry lb/hr from 0.5–2.5% solids at 5–25 gpm, producing 12–16% cake and up to 1,000 wet lb/hr, with design runtimes of 4.6–35.0 hours per week across current and 2040 cases (¶1.0 B, D, E pp.510–511); the press has a 1.5 HP 480 V TEFC drive, stainless hard-chrome filtration elements, six scrapers, a wash system limited to 750 gallons per 2.5 hours because "the 2W (air gap) water system has limited capacity", an LLDPE filtrate collector (7.5" outlet) to a 6" Sch 80 PVC manifold and LLDPE cake chutes (¶1.1–1.4 pp.512–514). The flocculator (model 05-300-900, 17 gal, 100 psi) has a 1 HP Nord SK0282 variable-speed agitator and a PVC air breather tied to "the potable water line", feeding the press through Sch 10 316 SS piping with Victaulic joints, 3" pneumatic feed and recirculation ball valves, lever channel isolating valves, 1" pneumatic wash valves and a 1" stainless polymer check valve, with the feed manifold shop tested at 100 psi (¶2.1–2.2 pp.515–518). Instruments and controls: E+H 10W 3" sludge and 10P 1" polymer electromagnetic flowmeters and a PMC71 0–30 psi pressure transmitter at the flocculator inlet, per-channel cake sensors and outlet air regulators, a wash-water pressure switch, and a NEMA 12, 480 V, CSA control cabinet with an Allen-Bradley CompactLogix 5370 PLC, PanelView Plus 7 HMI, PowerFlex 52X VFDs, an eWON Cosy 141 modem, a NEMA 4X press junction box and E-stop, programmed with the listed alarms, shutdowns and screens (¶4.1–4.4 pp.520–527). Submittals cover certified shop and erection drawings, electrical schematics and I/O diagrams, bills of material, spare parts lists and a detailed O&M manual (¶5.0–5.2 pp.527–530); the warranty is 1 year non-prorated from substantial completion (¶6.1 pp.530–531); there is no field start-up, field testing or training paragraph (Issue 37); and ¶1.1 cites "Section 22 13 16" for both the screw conveyor and the sludge pump (Issue 35).
