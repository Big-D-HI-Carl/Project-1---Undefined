## Summary

The legend adds concrete walkways per 1/C7.2, a concrete generator pad per 4/S2.4, gravel per 5/C7.3 and existing concrete to remain; walkways run along the west side of the existing District building (Missing_Pages_Page_004.png, C2.6 — Verified-Visual). Parking stall striping, a handicap symbol and disabled parking signage per 4/C7.1 are called out along the west side (keyed note 2) (Missing_Pages_Page_004.png, C2.6 — Verified-Visual). No bollards are shown or labeled, so the 12 bollards in Bid Item 3 are still not located on any sheet read (Unresolved); Add. 4 Clarification 1 points to this sheet for new surfacing (Add. 4 p.1 — Verified).
