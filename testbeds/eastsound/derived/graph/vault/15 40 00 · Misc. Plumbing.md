## Summary

Covers miscellaneous plumbing "for exterior purposes only" as described and shown on the plans (¶1.01 A p.253), with shop drawing and product data submittals (¶1.02 A) and compliance with the UBC, UPC and Eastsound Sewer and Water District standards (¶1.03 A). Materials: ABS DWV soil and waste piping above and below ground, unions at equipment connections, full-size cleanouts ("provide brass lugs, with graphite lubricant on threads"), 1" frost-proof yard hydrants (stainless rod and pipe, automatic draining, backflow preventer, 3/4" hose thread, Smith Cooper 0190167II vacuum breaker or equal, 4' bury; Woodford Iowa Y2 or Merrill CV-1000 or equal), and 2" post hydrants (316 SS hardware, 304 SS lower stem rod, hand wheel, brass working parts, 4' bury; American AVK Dry Barrel 67XX or Eclipse No. 2A or equal) with 2" Danfoss AVB atmospheric vacuum breakers (125 psi, ASSE 1001) (¶2.01 A–E p.253). Installation slopes drains 1/4" per foot, provides code cleanouts and follows manufacturer instructions and local codes (¶3.01 p.254); no testing is specified. No sheet in the P&M stack shows hydrant or drain locations or quantities (cross-lane item for Merge).
