## Summary

Four biological treatment blowers stand on a common pad in the Blower Building, with 3" stainless steel air pipe (keyed note 1, "see exposed aeration piping, Section 33 31 00") and 6" stainless steel air pipe (keyed note 2, "see exposed & buried aeration piping, Section 33 31 00"); the digester blower of C6.3 sits north of the Blower Building wall, and 3" and 1" 2W pipes run along the west and south walls. The base sheet places two 2W bladder tanks inside the Blower Building near the south doors, fed by the 1" 2W pipe. Add. 4 p.6 (governs) adds red edits: increase the distance between the blower pad and the wall to 4 ft; increase the building separation to 9 inches maximum and keep the 10-ft setback from property lines; move the 2W bladder tanks and their 1" supply piping to the Train #2 Building (dimensioned 2.0' and 1.0'), configure as shown, field adjust as needed and coordinate with the Owner; route the 1" 2W pipe below the existing concrete floor and penetrate the slab at the bladder tank connection. Add. 4 p.3 adds that the Blower Building dimensions are adjusted, leaving about 9" to the existing Train 2 building while the new slab extends to the existing Train 2 slab, and that the MCC, MCP, bladder tanks and exhaust fans move (A1.1, A1.2, C6.4 item); Add. 4 p.1 Clarification 4 sets the Blower Building footprint at 9.25' × 44.00' (context; primary lane Electrical & Controls per 03).
