## Summary

Covers re-establishing grass in areas disturbed by construction: stripping, stockpiling, topsoil, compost and hydroseeding, with a one-year seeding guarantee and 2 in of straw on graded areas not seeded or paved by October 15 (¶1.1, ¶1.3; main spec p.169 — Verified). Submittals: product data and installation recommendations, seed vendor blue-tag certification, and maintenance instructions after acceptance (¶1.03; p.169 — Verified). Seed is Sunmark 'DOT Multipurpose Mixture' at 8 lb/1,000 SF, fertilizer 6.5 lb/1,000 SF, wood fiber mulch 2,000 lb/acre, seeding August 15 to October 15 (¶2.2–2.4, ¶3.1 F–H; pp.170–171 — Verified). Topsoil follows 'Section 9.14.1(2) of the Standard Specifications' and hydroseeding follows WSDOT 8-01.3(2)B (¶2.1, ¶3.1 E; pp.170–171 — Verified; Unresolved: external reference, not staged). ¶2.2 B names 'R.I. Galleries' and a 'field behind recreation center', which appear on no Civil & Site sheet (p.170 — Verified; see Issues).
