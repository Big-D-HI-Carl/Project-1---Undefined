## Summary

Covers metal fabrications that are not structural steel or other specified metal systems — embedded and non-embedded metal work, anchor bolts, beams, bracing, framing and supports for mechanical and electrical equipment, shelf angles, metal ladders and ship ladders, and metal pipe and tube railings — related to 03 30 00, 13 12 20 and 09 90 00 [¶1.01 A–B, ¶1.02, p.186 — Verified]. Submittals are shop drawings for all fabricated items, product data, nosing samples, PE qualification data, stainless mill certificates, paint compatibility certificates and welder certificates, with welding to AWS D1.1/D1.6 by state-licensed welders [¶1.04, p.187; ¶1.05 B, p.187; ¶2.04 A, p.192 — Verified]. Ferrous metal not fully embedded in concrete is hot-dip galvanized after fabrication (2 oz/sf min.), aluminum touching concrete gets bituminous paint, and all-thread hanger rod is allowed only in stainless [¶2.02 D, p.191; ¶2.010, p.193; ¶3.04 A, p.197; ¶2.08, p.193 — Verified]. Mechanical anchors are limited to non-water-bearing concrete (building slabs, walkways, Engineer-approved locations), and chemical anchors are required on all water-bearing structures and wherever spacing or edge distance is under 3.5" [¶2.03 B, pp.191–192 — Verified]. Handrails and guardrails are specified here as 1-1/4" steel pipe (ASTM A53 Gr B) while ¶2.02 B specifies aluminum pipe railings, and grouting is referred to a Section 04 60 00 that exists neither in the main spec nor in 02 [¶2.014 A, p.196; ¶2.02 B.1, p.191; ¶3.03 E, p.197 — Verified; Unresolved — Issues].
