## Summary

Finished-grade spot elevations and match points set the site grading; areas are graded from finished grade to daylight at 2H:1V, building doors are matched 0.02 ft below finished floor, all catch basins get herringbone grates, and non-stormwater hatches and covers sit at least 0.02 ft above the surrounding asphalt (keyed note 4; notes 1–3) (Missing_Pages_Page_002.png, C2.1 — Verified-Visual). Catch basin rims are given for SDCB #1 (22.3) through #10, and SDCB #1 is at the presumed location of the existing storm sewer, to be adjusted after potholing (keyed note 7) (Missing_Pages_Page_002.png, C2.1 — Verified-Visual). Keyed notes add a Keystone block retaining wall (plan, profile and details per C2.5 and 1/C7.8), 4-in roof drains, an asphalt wedge curb per 4/C7.7, a footing drain, the sawcut line per C0.5, and two runs of 6-ft chain link fence, 149 LF and 134 LF, each connected to existing (keyed notes 1–3, 5–9) (Missing_Pages_Page_002.png, C2.1 — Verified-Visual). The fence lengths differ from the 126 LF on C2.5 Keyed Note 4 (Unresolved — see Civil_and_Site_Resplit_Pass.md).
