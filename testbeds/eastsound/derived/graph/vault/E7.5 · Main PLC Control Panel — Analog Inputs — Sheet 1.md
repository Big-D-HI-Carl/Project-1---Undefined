## Summary

One Allen-Bradley 1769-IF8 8-channel analog input card, differential current wiring, loop power from 24 VDC through 0.25 A fused terminals; the terminal jumper is removed for 4-wire devices (plans_11 p.2) [Verified]. Channel 0: 2W system pressure transducer, 0–150 psi; channel 1: digester level transducer, 0–15 ft, through an intrinsic safety barrier; channels 2–3: UV1 and UV2 intensity from the UV controllers; channels 4–7 spare (plans_11 p.2) [Verified-Visual; device labels are not in the text layer]. The digester level range (0–15 ft) differs from the E5.1 transducer (0–15 psi, about 34.6 ft of water), and the barrier serves a digester that E0.4 lists as unclassified — see Issues (plans_11 p.2; plans_10 p.1; plans_9 p.1) [Verified-Visual; conversion Inferred at 2.307 ft of water per psi]. General note 1: the schematics show functional requirements; the system integrator provides the detailed panel design (plans_11 p.2) [Verified].
