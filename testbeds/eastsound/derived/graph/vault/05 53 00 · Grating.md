## Summary

Scope is aluminum grating set in pultruded vinyl ester FRP embedment angles with an integral anchor, to the NAAMM Metal Bar Grating Manual, with shop drawings and manufacturer's instructions as the submittal (the paragraph asks for a "dimensional layout of all handrailing," as written) [¶1.01 A, ¶1.02 A, ¶1.03 A, p.205 — Verified]. Solid plank grating is for UV channels only: 2" all-aluminum slip-resistant plank (SlipNOT or equal) that blocks all light from leaving the UV channel, on embedment angles per the grating manufacturer [¶2.01 A–C, p.205 — Verified]. Aluminum bar grating carries 100 psf at L/360 (2.0" deep, 4.0 lb/sf for 2'-6" to 6'-7" spans), is banded I-bar, and sits flush on 2"×2"×1/4" gray pultruded FRP embedment angles (IKG or equal) [¶2.02 A–C, p.205 — Verified]. Grating is fastened with removable aluminum clips and Type 316 stainless bolts, with galvanic isolation and bituminous paint where aluminum meets concrete or grout [¶3.01 A–C, p.206 — Verified]; S2.2 instead supports the grating on HDG steel angles and calls solid plank grating as typical at both basins [plans_8 p.2, Details 5–6 — Verified-Visual; Unresolved — Issues, Conflicts].
