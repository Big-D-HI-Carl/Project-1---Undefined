## Summary

The foundation plan shows two cast-in-place basins on 12" mat slab foundations — a UV basin with 8" concrete walls and a digester with 12" concrete walls — with an HDG W8×18 beam spanning the digester, connected to the wall at each end per Detail 7 [plans_8 p.2, S2.2 Detail 1 — Verified-Visual]. Mat slab reinforcing is called out twice as "typical for mat slabs this plan," once as #6 @ 10" o.c. T&B and once as #6 @ 12" o.c. T&B, and reinforcing at penetrations follows 6/S4.1 and 7/S4.1 [plans_8 p.2, Detail 1 — Verified-Visual; spacing — Unresolved: need engineer clarification, Issues, Conflicts #1]. Sections A-A, B-B and C-C show #5 and #6 wall bars at 10"–12" o.c. (each face in the 12" walls, centered in the 8" walls) hooked 3" clear from bottom, a hydrophilic waterstop at all construction joints (submit for approval), an unreinforced sloped concrete topping per civil, subgrade preparation per the structural notes and geotech report, and "guardrail & ladders per civil" [plans_8 p.2, Details 2–4 — Verified-Visual]. Inside the UV basin, 8" concrete walls support the equipment in 4 places (#5 @ 12" o.c. each way, vertical bars drilled and epoxied to the slab), with 3" mechanical equipment pads reinforced per civil at each end of the equipment [plans_8 p.2, Detail 3 — Verified-Visual]. Aluminum solid plank grating per the specifications (submit for approval; attachment by manufacturer) bears on a continuous HDG 1/4" steel edge angle at wall tops (Detail 5) and a continuous HDG L3×3×1/4 with 5/8" drilled-and-epoxied anchors at 24" o.c. at wall faces (Detail 6); Detail 7 connects the HDG beam to the wall with HDG L4×4×3/8 angles, 3/4" A307 bolts and 5/8" epoxy anchors [plans_8 p.2, Details 5–7 — Verified-Visual].
