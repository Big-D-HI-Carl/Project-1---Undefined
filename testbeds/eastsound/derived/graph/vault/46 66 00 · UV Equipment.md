## Summary

Two open-channel, gravity-flow, low-pressure high-intensity UV systems, each able to disinfect alone at max day 0.46 MGD, max month 0.22 MGD and minimum 0.03 MGD with TSS up to 30 mg/L, 33–85°F and UVT of at least 50% at 253.7 nm, to 200 FC/100 mL monthly and 400 FC/100 mL weekly geometric means, guaranteed regardless of influent count (¶1.01 A p.502; design criteria pp.502–503). Arrangement: 4 lamps per module, 6 modules per bank, 2 banks, 48 lamps (12 modules), in stainless channels furnished with the system, delivering at least 30,000 µWs/cm² (30 mJ/cm²) at 50% UVT after sleeve, fouling and aging reductions, proven by an independent bioassay to EPA/625/1-86/021, for outdoor installation without shelter (pp.503–504); basis is Trojan UV3000 PTP (base bid), with alternates bid as an Equipment Alternate and needing 500 North American installations, a bioassay and a 5-year guarantee (¶1.03 A p.502; Part 2 A p.504). Construction: 316 SS or quartz in contact with effluent; modules about 68"×20"×2.8", 38 lb, Type 6P, with electronic ballasts, 10-ft 120 V plug cords and LED lamp status; low-pressure slimline hot-cathode lamps warranted 12,000 hours non-prorated; Type 214 quartz sleeves; per-unit effluent channel with drain, module rack and downstream level control weir holding about 12" depth within 1.5" from zero to peak flow; two inlet and two outlet 304 SS transition boxes; a 304 SS maintenance rack; maximum draw 2,100 W at 120 V (pp.505–507). Monitoring: two submersible UV intensity sensors (one per bank, 10-year warranty), intensity and lamp-age displays and a low-UV dry contact in a fiberglass Type 4X wall panel less than 12 ft from the module LED end, plus GFCI duplex PDRs by the UV manufacturer, with the contractor supplying a 120 V circuit of about 19.2 A and a separate 120 V 5 A monitoring supply (pp.506–507). Submittals include shop drawings, electrical schematics, hydraulic calculations, bioassay and dose calculations and a lifetime performance guarantee (¶1.04 pp.503–504); equipment warranty is 12 months from substantial completion (¶1.05 p.504); spares are 4 lamps, 4 sleeves, 4 seals and an operator kit per bank (p.507); and the manufacturer provides 2 days of start-up and field testing and 1 day of training (¶3.02 p.508).
