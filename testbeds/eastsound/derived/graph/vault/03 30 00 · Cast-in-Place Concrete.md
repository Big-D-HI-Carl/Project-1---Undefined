## Summary

Covers labor, materials and equipment for subgrade preparation, forms, rebar and placement of all cast-in-place concrete shown, paid as incidental to the lump-sum bid items in which the work appears; the only extra-work items are overexcavation and structural fill under 31 20 00, by written authorization [¶1.01 A.1, ¶1.03, p.172 — Verified]. The Drawing Structural Notes take precedence over less specific spec requirements and set the mix designs and reinforcing, while WSDOT M41-10 (2022), WSDOT material sources, WSDOT-approved aggregate sources and WSDOT 9-04.2 joint sealant are incorporated by reference [¶1.05 A, ¶2.03 A, ¶2.04 B–C, pp.172–175 — Verified; ¶1.04 A.1, ¶1.06 D.4, ¶2.04 A, ¶2.04 D — Unresolved: external reference, not staged]. Submittals are ACI SP-66 shop drawings, mix designs 15 working days before use, material certificates, a PE-prepared hot and cold weather plan, joint layout plans (control joints no more than 30× slab thickness where not shown), and WA SE-sealed formwork plans and calculations for the "Clarifier, digester and chlorine contact basin structures" — names that do not all match the sheet titles [¶1.06 A–G, p.173 — Verified; Issues, Missing information]. The Owner provides independent testing and inspection (subgrade compaction and moisture, reinforcing placement, concrete placement and cylinders) and special inspections, and anchor bolts are set to AISC 303-16 §7.5 tolerances by a registered land surveyor [¶1.07 B, p.174; ¶3.012 A, p.180; ¶3.03 B, p.176 — Verified]. Products and execution include hydrophilic waterstop (Cetco RX-101T or Greenstreak Hydrotite CJ0725, 3" min. cover), 15-mil Stego Wrap vapor retarder, 40-diameter laps unless shown otherwise, steel-trowel interior slabs damp-cured only with 1/8"/ft grout slopes to sumps, and a moist cure of 90 days min. for all structural concrete against 7 days for building slabs [¶2.04 F, ¶2.05, ¶3.05 B, ¶3.09 A.1, ¶3.010 D, pp.175–179 — Verified; cure duration — Unresolved, Issues, Conflicts].
