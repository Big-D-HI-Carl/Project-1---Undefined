Darrow, WA PLS 50697, Wilson Engineering; field work January 2013 with additional data March 2021 (Notes 1.E, 2.B; plans_1 p.4, G0.4 — Verified). Horizontal basis is NAD 83/91 Washington State Plane North Zone, local ground values held on San Juan County monument 00165 (N 628,007.20, E 1,138,590.22 USft); bearings hold the inverse to the E 1/16 corner of Sections 11 and 14, S 88°16'54" E, 1301.67 ft grid (plans_1 p.4, G0.4 — Verified). Vertical datum is NGVD 29 on USGS benchmark 20 RLM (31.0 ft) per the perpetuation of County benchmark 00165 (34.28 ft); contours come from direct field observation at 1-ft major intervals (Notes 2.A, 2.C–2.D; plans_1 p.4, G0.4 — Verified). Utility lines come from surface-visible structures and locate marks, and Wilson does not guarantee their accuracy (Notes 3.A–3.C; plans_1 p.4, G0.4 — Verified). The parcel is described per AFN 2021-1022007 in the SE 1/4 of Section 11, T37N, R2W, W.M.; the On-Site Survey Control Table heading is in the text layer without values, and the table was not read (plans_1 p.4, G0.4 — Verified).
