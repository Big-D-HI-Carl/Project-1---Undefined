## Summary

Seven details, not to scale: 1 Concrete Walkway, 2 Pipe Sleeve, 3 Floor Sleeve, 4 Forcemain Thrust Blocking Schedule, 5 Pipe Support, 6 Guide Structure Support, 7 Vertical Pipe Brace (plans_6 p.4, C7.2 — Verified; numbers 1–5 matched on the image, Verified-Visual). Concrete walkways are 6 in minimum (match existing) with #3 bars at 12 in each way over 2 in of compacted crushed surfacing top course and subgrade at 95% (ASTM D1557), with control joints at 12 ft and expansion joints at 36 ft maximum (Detail 1; plans_6 p.4, C7.2 — Verified-Visual). Pipe and floor sleeves are standard-weight 316 stainless steel with seep rings, sealed with Dow Corning 732 RTV silicone (Details 2–3; plans_6 p.4, C7.2 — Verified-Visual). The thrust block table sizes bearing area for 4-in to 20-in pipe on 200 psi internal pressure, 3,000 psf soil bearing and a safety factor of 1.5, and all bends, tees and crosses also get restrained joints (Romac Gripper) (Detail 4 Notes 1, 7; plans_6 p.4, C7.2 — Verified-Visual). Pipe supports are galvanized steel saddle supports on grout pads, hot-dip galvanized after fabrication; guide and brace supports use stainless steel plates and anchors (Details 5–7; plans_6 p.4, C7.2 — Verified-Visual).
