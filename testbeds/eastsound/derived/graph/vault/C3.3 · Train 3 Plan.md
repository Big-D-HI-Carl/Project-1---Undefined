## Summary

Plan of the new Train 3 bullseye tank: 32.00' inner and 34.00' outer wall diameter, 12" walls and a 12" footing extension, around an 18'-0" clarifier (R300, 500.7 SF) with a 5'-0" feed well, sludge scraper arms and scraper arm drive, skimmer arm, scum skimmer airlift, a 3'-0" clarifier bridge and a 2' peripheral clarifier walkway with handrail. The annulus holds Aeration Zone #1 (3,983 CF), Aeration Zone #2 (1,322 CF), Anoxic Zone #1 (2,097 CF) and Anoxic Zone #2 (1,175 CF), with plenum diffusers, an adjustable weir, DO #1 and DO #2 DO/pH sensors (detail 1/C7.4), two submersible mixers, a RAS airlift weir box, a nitrate recycle weir box and a 6" nitrate recycle (MLR) pipe; a 3" RAS airlift sludge duct and a 10" clarifier inlet pipe run inside the clarifier. Connections: Train 3 8" influent IE 20.08, Train 3 8" effluent IE 20.00, 2" WAS/clarifier drain to the WAS pump, and a 6" air inlet. Two davit crane assemblies and a prefab aluminum ladder assembly connected to the access platform (clarifier bridge) are shown; note 1 requires partitions reinforced for individual zone dewatering (not shown for clarity).
