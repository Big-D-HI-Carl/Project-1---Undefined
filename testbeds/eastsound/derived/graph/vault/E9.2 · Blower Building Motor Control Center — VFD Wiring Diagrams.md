## Summary

Typical VFD unit: lockable circuit breaker disconnect, power distribution block, 480/120 V 350 VA control transformer, 3% line reactor, Allen-Bradley PowerFlex 753 with Ethernet communication module, door-mounted HIM on RS485, dV/dt filter for motor runs over 50 ft, motor terminal block (plans_12 p.4) [Verified-Visual; PowerFlex 753 also in text]. Control: hand-off-auto with hand and auto status to the PLC panel; PLC relay "call to run" through the motor overtemperature contact to relay CR1; a 10 kΩ hand speed potentiometer; running (CR3) and fault (CR4) contacts to the PLC with push-to-test running and fault lights; MCC cooling fan(s) on a thermostat and the blower enclosure fan on the unit control power (plans_12 p.4) [Verified-Visual]. Key note 1: provide a 120 V blower fan circuit from each MCC unit for each blower; basis of design 0.21 HP and 1.51 A per fan, confirmed with the manufacturer during submittals (plans_12 p.4) [Verified]. Key note 2: in hand, speed comes from the potentiometer on the MCC door or field panel; in auto, speed command and feedback go direct to the PLC over Ethernet, and analog speed hardwiring is not required. Key note 3: wire the motor thermal contact to each VFD where available; jumper the terminals when the motor has none (plans_12 p.4) [Verified].
