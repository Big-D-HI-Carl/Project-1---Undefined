## Summary

The material list names one each of: rotary press model (steel), flocculator (steel), sludge manifold and piping (316 SS), cake chute (LLDPE), air compressor and control panel, all "Section 46 76 26"; an emulsion polymer make-up system, "Section 46 33 33"; and screw conveyor equipment (SS), "Section 46 12 13" (not a section in the spec — Issue 14). The plan (press line about 161" [13'-5"] by 84 3/4" [7'-0 3/4"], conveyor 131 11/16" [10'-11 11/16"]) shows the press, flocculator, sludge manifold, operator interface, control panel, a 30"×30" "dry polymer mix unit" (Issue 16), 3" digested sludge in, 2" 2W water, and 4" pressate out "to influent pump station". The elevation shows the screw conveyor rising at 28° over 147 15/16" [12'-3 15/16"] to discharge at 90" [7'-6"] into a mobile dumpster for sludge cake collection (40" [3'-4"] high), with the 2W water supply pipe, 3" digester sludge and 4" pressate drain pipe below the floor.
