## Summary

Site piping plan annotated with pipe invert elevations (IE) at structures, connections and pipe ends, on the same base as C1.3 (plans_3Part-3 p.1, C1.5 — Verified-Visual). It repeats the C0.1 storm and sewer structure tables and marks 'match existing' at the sewer connection near the office building (plans_3Part-3 p.1, C1.5 — Verified-Visual). The elevations are graphics only; the text layer holds only UV equipment block labels, although 01 lists the sheet as Text (plans_3Part-3 p.1 — Verified; see Issues). Add. 4 p.3 lists no C1.5 edit, so the sheet stands as issued (Add. 4 p.3 — Verified).
