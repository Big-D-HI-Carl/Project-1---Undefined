## Summary

Main PLC control panel: Allen-Bradley CompactLogix PLC and PanelView Plus operator interface on a network switch that links the MCC power monitor, the 2W and effluent flow meters and the VFDs in the MCC (typ of 10) (plans_10 p.5) [Verified-Visual; PLC, switch and meter labels also in text]. Influent Pump Station control panel: its own CompactLogix PLC (1768-ENBT) and network switch serving six flow meters — Train #1 (future), Train #2, Train #3, plant drain, influent and WAS (plans_10 p.5) [Verified]. Office: SCADA computer with alarm dialer on the existing telephone and internet service with a network switch (S-LAB); dewatering skid with its own network switch, operator interface and CompactLogix PLC (S-DW1); control handhole(s) on the S-ICP route; the cable legend distinguishes fiber optic and Cat 6 Ethernet (plans_10 p.5) [Verified-Visual]. General notes: the system integrator provides the detailed control panel design and documentation for a complete and operational system (only major components are specified), and all Ethernet-enabled equipment joins the control network with ports as required (plans_10 p.5) [Verified]. E7.0 calls the plant meters "Train #2/#3" and the dewatering meter "WAS"; E4.1 calls them plant 2, plant 3 and dewatering flow meters — the same meters by count and panel; the Train #1 meter appears only here (plans_10 p.5; plans_9 p.6) [Inferred].
