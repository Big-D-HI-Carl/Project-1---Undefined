## Summary

UV disinfection chamber: UV units UV1 and UV2, each with a UV control panel and UV power distribution (key note 3, typ of 2), an effluent flow meter in a vault with a local transmitter panel, a pole light with weatherproof GFCI receptacle, and a digester level transducer with a desiccant J-box; home runs P-UV1, P-UV2, C-UV, S-LT-D, P-LT and a spare to the MCC and PLC panel, plus S-FME and C-FME (plan and elevation; plans_10 p.1) [Verified-Visual]. Key notes 1–2: WIKA submersible level transducer (316 SS, 0–15 psi, LevelGuard, 4–20 mA, vented, desiccant, NEMA 4X; enclosure on the handrail with SS hardware); Promag W400 flow meter with remote housing and an IP68 Type 6 potted submersible sensor and two 316L grounding rings, matching Add. 4 p.2 (26 80 00 ¶2.04 D) (plans_10 p.1) [Verified]. Key notes 3–4: install the local UV control panels and power distribution; the contractor provides the wiring gutter, J-boxes and three duplex GFCI power distribution receptacles (PDRs) with in-use wet-location covers per UV unit on SS Unistrut, with stainless conduit, fittings and J-boxes; a stainless local panel holds the effluent flow transmitter and instrument terminals, wired back to the PLC (plans_10 p.1) [Verified]. UV interconnection diagram (typ of 2): UV system controller, stainless splitter panel within 10 ft of the module power end, PDRs feeding UV modules on 10-ft cords (2 per PDR), a 120 V 30 A circuit to the MCC power panel, 120 V power and control wiring to the MCC and PLC panel, and a factory UV sensor cable in conduit (plans_10 p.1) [Verified]. Key note 5: UV-area pole light on a concrete base secured to the basin side (coordinate with civil), 4-in square aluminum pole 12 ft, photocell, local on/off switch, Lithonia "#DXS0-LED-P3-30K-T3M-MVOLT-SPA-DNAXD" or equal; general note "4." appears twice and carries a wet-well conduit note (plans_10 p.1) [Verified].
