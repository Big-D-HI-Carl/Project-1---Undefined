## Summary

Plan: exhaust fans EF-1 (circuit LP1-5) and EF-2 (LP1-7), each motor marked 1/2, motorized louver ML-1 (marked 1/6) on the louver/HVAC control circuit LP1-3, a manual louver, interior fixtures L1 on an occupancy sensor, exterior wall pack L2, emergency units X1 and thermostats (plan; plans_9 p.4) [Verified for EF-1, EF-2, LP-1; ML-1, manual louver, markings and fixtures Verified-Visual]. Key notes 1–4: photocell and dedicated switch on the dark-sky exterior fixture; emergency lights on the interior lighting circuit; occupancy sensor per Washington State Energy Code; a complete ventilation control panel with relay controls and one thermostat per exhaust fan, with the motorized louver operating whenever either fan runs (plans_9 p.4) [Verified]. Blower Building fixture schedule: L1 narrow 4-ft vapor-tight LED (Acuity CSVT-L48…, 42 VA), L2 LED wet-location wall pack with photocell (Acuity ARC1 LED…, 17 VA), X1 LED emergency combo with battery (Lithonia ECC, 6 VA), all 120 V (plans_9 p.4) [Verified-Visual]. Panel LP1 (MCC) schedule: 120/240 V 1-ph 3-W, 100 A bus, 100 A main breaker, 10,000 AIC, surface mounted; loads are Blower Building receptacles and lighting, EF-1/EF-2, louver/HVAC control, UV1/UV2 power and controllers, Main Control Panel, generator heater and battery charger, Hot Box 1 and 2 (GFEP), effluent sampler and hypochlorite feed pump; demand 17.3 kVA, 72.1 A (plans_9 p.4) [Verified-Visual]. General notes: branch circuits not on the conduit schedule are sized per NEC, and all conduit and boxes are underground or concealed in walls, coordinated with the "CMU installer" (see Issues); Add. 4 p.3 also relocates the exhaust fans — Add. 4 governs (A1.1 reissue, Add. 4 p.4) (plans_9 p.4) [Verified].
