## Summary

One Allen-Bradley 1769-OB16 16-point output card driving relays CR0–CR15, 11 of 16 outputs used (plans_11 p.8) [Verified-Visual; card label in text]. Outputs 0–3: influent pumps 1–4 "call to run" into each pump control circuit; outputs 4–6: WAS valve #1, #2 and #3 solenoids at 120 V (plans_11 p.8) [Verified-Visual]. Output 7: "ventilation alarm light and horn station" at 120 V; outputs 8–10: alarm horn, "HS2 alarm light" (H2S by context) and methane alarm light at the ventilation control panel; outputs 11–15 spare (plans_11 p.8) [Verified-Visual; H2S reading Inferred from E9.3]. General note 1: the schematics show functional requirements; the system integrator designs the panel (plans_11 p.8) [Verified].
