## Summary

Details of Hot Box #1 (flow meter and isolation valve; Detail 1) and Hot Box #2 (air gap piping; Details 2–3), each a locked, insulated, heated enclosure on a 58 x 24 x 4-in, 3,000 psi concrete slab over 6-in gravel base with 6-in crushed surfacing (plans_6 p.8, C7.6 — Verified; details Verified-Visual). Hot Box #1 holds an ASCO Redhat 8221 slow-closing, normally closed 3/4-in solenoid valve (120 VAC, NEMA 4X), a 1-in flow meter 'per electrical design requirements', a Spears S1780 1.25-in check valve, an Orenco 1.25-in FCA flow control assembly with spare orifice plates, manual ball valves and unions (Material List 1–13; plans_6 p.8, C7.6 — Verified-Visual). Hot Box #2 provides a two-pipe-diameter air gap into a funnel draining through 4-in Sch. 80 PVC, in a Hot Box model HB2ST or approved equal (Details 2–3; plans_6 p.8, C7.6 — Verified-Visual). All piping inside both boxes is Sch. 80 PVC, exposed piping gets 90 W, 120 V heat trace and a receptacle, and corrosive surfaces get two coats of epoxy paint (Notes 1–3, Material List 12; plans_6 p.8, C7.6 — Verified-Visual). Add. 4 relocates and reorients the hot boxes on C1.3 but does not reissue C7.6 (Add. 4 p.3 — Verified).
