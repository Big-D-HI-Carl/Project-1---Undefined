## What the documents require

### 33 33 00 — HDPE Piping
- Fusion operator qualifications to the Engineer before pipe is ordered: manufacturer-trained, 5,000 LF of HDPE fusion, a comparable project in the last three years, 49 CFR 192.283/.285/.287 certifications. (33 33 00 ¶1.02–1.03; main spec p.444 — Verified)
- PE4710, ASTM D3350 cell class 445574C (DriscoPlex 4000/4100 IPS or equal); stripe light blue for 2W, green for sewage, dark blue for potable. (33 33 00 ¶2.01–2.02; main spec pp.444–445 — Verified)
- Butt fusion above ground per the manufacturer; every joint visually inspected and data-logged (logs submitted); 3 joints or 3%, whichever is greater, destructively tested; pressure test per ASTM F2164. (33 33 00 ¶3.03–3.04; main spec p.446 — Verified)

### Appendix F — applies by its scope; not on the Ledger row (Inferred)
- Temporary piping from the existing chlorine disinfection chamber outlet to the existing 8-in effluent pipe just upstream of SSMH #1288 (match to this pipe Inferred). (Appendix F §5.1; main spec p.618 — Verified (reference document))
