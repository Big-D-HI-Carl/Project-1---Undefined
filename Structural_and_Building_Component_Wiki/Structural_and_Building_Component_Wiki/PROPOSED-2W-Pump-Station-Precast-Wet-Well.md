- **Summary:** The 2W Plant Water Pump Station wet well is an 8' ID Oldcastle 96" precast wet well with a 48"×30" H-20 aluminum hatch and safety grate (LW Products HD-3C), per 03 40 00 and drawing C4.2 [main spec p.183 — Verified]. Add. 4 seals the exterior of all precast wet well and vault joints with CANUSA-CPS WrapidSeal or approved equal [Add. 4 p.1, Clarification 2 — Verified; Add. 4 governs]. The drawing, C4.2, sits in the Process & Mechanical lane [01 — Verified].
