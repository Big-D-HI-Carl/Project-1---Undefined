# Influent Pump Station Wet Well

- **Component tag:** PROPOSED-Influent-Pump-Station-Precast-Wet-Well (proposed — no tag shown in the documents)
- **Type:** Precast — wet well with hatch
- **Discipline:** Structural
- **Revision/Date:** Add. 4 (issued Feb 9, 2023) governs — Add. 4 p.1, Clarification 2 (exterior of all precast joints sealed with CANUSA-CPS WrapidSeal or approved equal)
- **Summary:** The Influent Pump Station wet well is an 8' ID Oldcastle 96" precast wet well with a 48"×30" H-20 aluminum hatch and safety grate (LW Products HD-3C), per the 03 40 00 table and drawing C4.1 [main spec p.183 — Verified]. Precast wet wells and vaults are 5,000 psi with A615 Gr 60 rebar to ACI 318-14 and ASTM C857, with WA-engineer-sealed shop drawings and calculations and no testing stated [main spec pp.182–183 — Verified]. Add. 4 seals the exterior of all precast wet well and vault joints with CANUSA-CPS WrapidSeal or approved equal [Add. 4 p.1, Clarification 2 — Verified; Add. 4 governs]. That hatch conflicts with ¶2.02 D.1, which gives the station's top slab a 3'-0"×5'-0" light-duty Flygt FLED-13 hatch, and 09 97 23 lines the station's interior concrete with epoxy [main spec pp.183, 236 — Verified; hatch — Unresolved].
