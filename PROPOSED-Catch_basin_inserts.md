### 31 32 11 — Soil Surface Erosion Control
- Install all BMPs before land-disturbing work and keep clearing to the minimum. (31 32 11 ¶3.01 A; main spec p.399 — Verified)
- Submittals: silt fence geotextile, compost, inlet protection products, barrier fence, track clean plates and any other erosion control product; installer must be a specialist. (31 32 11 ¶1.04–1.05; main spec p.398 — Verified)
